# ChemRxiv submission draft

## Title

Modeling CO₂ absorption in aqueous monoethanolamine with ePC-SAFT

## Author and affiliation

Tanner W. Polley; ORCID 0009-0008-5957-4152; tpolley3@byu.edu. Department of Chemical Engineering, Brigham Young University, Provo, UT 84602, United States.

## Abstract

We examine whether one explicit-ion electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) formulation with carbamate chemistry can represent the equilibrium, caloric and density properties of loaded aqueous monoethanolamine (MEA). Sixteen parameters are fitted jointly to pressure, species, integral absorption heat, density, heat capacity and binary equilibrium data. For nominal 30 wt% MEA, average absolute relative deviations are 11.86% over 47 fitted pressures and 20.55% over the 159-pressure comparison, and 7.10% over 94 fitted species fractions. The fitted integral-heat root-mean-square error is 1.83 kJ/mol CO₂; the average absolute relative density deviation is 0.18% over 47 fitted loaded densities, calculated without volume translation. Pressure deviations lie within the range reported for related models on different data sets and with different fitting roles. A comparison fit predicts additional liquid phases; narrowing the protonated-amine diameter and water-interaction bounds removes the detected instabilities in a finite search at 313.15–333.15 K and loading 10⁻⁵–0.5, with a small increase in fitting cost. Carbonate shares, titration bicarbonate, warm densities and unloaded heat capacity remain discrepant, and pressure agreement deteriorates away from 30 wt% MEA. The available comparisons do not yet establish suitability across an absorber's operating range.
