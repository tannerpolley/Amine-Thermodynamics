Dear Editors of Fluid Phase Equilibria,

Please consider “Modeling CO₂ absorption in aqueous monoethanolamine with ePC-SAFT” for publication as a research article.

The paper examines whether one explicit-ion ePC-SAFT formulation with carbamate chemistry represents the equilibrium, caloric and density properties of loaded aqueous MEA. Sixteen parameters are fitted to pressure, species, integral heat, density, heat capacity and binary VLE data. Pressure deviation is 11.86% over 47 fitted observations at 313.00–333.15 K and 20.55% over the 159-pressure comparison at 313.00–393.15 K, including 112 not fitted but examined during model development. The fitted integral-heat root-mean-square error is 1.84 kJ/mol CO₂. Loaded-density deviation is 0.18% over 47 fitted observations, without volume translation, with 44 within twice the assigned scale and three accepted misses at 353.15 K. The manuscript presents calculated property curves and discusses the remaining discrepancies beside each property.

The 159-pressure deviation of 20.55% lies within the published range for related MEA models using different data sets and fitting roles; no common-data accuracy ranking is claimed. The application introduces explicit carbamate ions within ePC-SAFT. A finite phase search covers 162 states at 313.15–393.15 K and loading 10⁻⁵–0.6 and illustrates how similar property agreement can conceal additional calculated liquids. Narrowing two parameter bounds removes the detected instabilities at a 0.45% increase in fitting cost, with three of the 47 fitted loaded densities outside twice the assigned scale. This finite numerical result does not establish phase behavior outside the searched states.

The model represents selected nominal 30 wt% observations. Pressure deviations reach 29.5 and 56.0% at 15 and 45 wt% MEA. Bicarbonate and carbonate fractions, the 0.51 MPa Arcis integral heats, unloaded heat capacity at 318.15 K and loaded densities above 353.15 K remain discrepant, and the available comparisons do not establish suitability across absorber and stripper temperatures.

Processed observations, parameters, calculations and presentation code are prepared for a Zenodo deposit, with the DOI to be added at submission. The compiled ePC-SAFT library used here will be supplied to referees through restricted access coordinated by the corresponding author. Deposit publication, access arrangements and submission remain subject to the author's approval.
