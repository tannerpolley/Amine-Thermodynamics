\begin{abstract}
% P:abstract-01
We fit a nine-species electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) model of \COtwo absorption in 30 wt\% aqueous monoethanolamine (MEA), with explicit carbamate chemistry and a solvation-shell Born term.
Sixteen parameters are fitted jointly to 307 quantities: \COtwo partial pressures, species fractions, integral heats, densities, heat capacities and binary phase equilibria.
The reported parameter set gives average absolute relative deviations of 11.86\,\% for 47 fitted pressures and 7.10\,\% for 94 species fractions, and an integral-heat root-mean-square error of \SI{1.83}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} \COtwo.
Densities are calculated without volume translation; 44 of 47 fitted loaded densities lie within twice their assigned scale.
Tangent-plane searches at 73 states (313.15--\SI{333.15}{\kelvin}, loading $10^{-5}$--0.5) detect instability at 12 states for a fit with the original parameter bounds; at \SI{333.15}{\kelvin} and loadings 0.4 and 0.5 that fit splits into a water-rich liquid and a liquid richer in amine and ions.
Narrowing the bounds on the protonated-amine diameter and its water interaction gives the reported set: it removes every detected instability, raises the total cost by 0.45\,\% and leaves three densities 0.40--0.53\,\% low at \SI{353.15}{\kelvin}.
The fitted protonated-amine dissociation constant exceeds the Bates--Pinching value by 0.49 pK at \SI{313.15}{\kelvin}.
Carbonate shares, titration bicarbonate, an unloaded heat capacity and pressures at 45 wt\% MEA remain discrepant.
The phase searches do not reach the \SI{353.15}{\kelvin} density failures or the higher-temperature pressure data, and the temperature dependence of the heat is untested beyond 313.15 and \SI{322.5}{\kelvin}.
\end{abstract}
