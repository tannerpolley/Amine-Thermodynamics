The $2s$ requirement concerns the 47 fitted loaded densities only; density scales are not uniformly measured uncertainties and $2s$ is not a 95\,\% interval.

\section{Finite phase-family states and A6 split confirmations}\label{sec:s-phase}
% P:supplement-06
The table gives the exact 73 paired temperature/pressure/loading states and the resolved-trial counts for A6 and A7.
Index 0 repeats the index-41 state with its pressure rounded to \SI{5918.5286}{\pascal}; both are listed.
At each grid location, the three pressure levels have their stated values rather than one shared pressure across the family.
A6 has 12 resolved-descent points and A7 none.
No descent was numerically resolved in 657 trials at 73 specified states (313.15--\SI{333.15}{\kelvin}, $\alpha$ $10^{-5}$--0.5).
The result makes no stability claim above \SI{333.15}{\kelvin}, above loading 0.5 or at the \SI{353.15}{\kelvin} density failures.

\section{Restricted-box probes and Hartono loading sensitivity}\label{sec:s-probes}
% P:supplement-08
The fixed-parameter size probe changes only the \MEAH segment diameter and its packing/Debye--H\"uckel ties at the first restricted endpoint.
The sampled sizes are 5.49, 5.45, 5.40 and \SI{5.35}{\angstrom}; the corresponding numbers within $2s$ are 44, 44, 22 and 7 of 47 fitted loaded densities.
No sampled vector has resolved descent on its 73-point family.
Reducing size raises densities: improving the warm rows can move cold Hartono rows above $+2s$.
The samples neither establish the minimum residual span between them nor exclude an untested size meeting the requirement.
The subsequent joint refit uses a \MEAH size lower bound of \SI{5.35}{\angstrom} and ends at that bound, with 44 of 47 densities within $2s$.

% P:supplement-09
Hartono et al.\ report the apparent \COtwo component mole fraction $x_3$ alongside the printed loading $\alpha$ \cite[Table~3, footnote~a, p.~1810]{Hartono2014}.
The sensitivity uses the alternative loading $\alpha_{x_3}=x_3(1+W)/(1-x_3)$, where $W=7.911062165$ is the declared molar ratio of water to \MEA in the nominal 30 wt\% \COtwo-free solvent.
Here $x_3$ is an apparent component fraction, not the true-species mole fraction of molecular \COtwo in the reacting liquid.
The separate sensitivity changes only the declared 32 Hartono feed/loadings to this basis, keeps the printed-loading scales frozen, and refits in the second restricted box.
It retains 44 of 47 densities within $2s$ on its own basis, with failures at Amundsen 081, Amundsen 077 and the Hartono \SI{353.15}{\kelvin} observation in the column labelled $\alpha=0.39$ in the source.
The same sensitivity endpoint evaluated on nominal printed-loading rows has 40 of 47 within $2s$; this is a cross-basis comparison, not a replacement fit.
Its endpoint family also has no resolved descent in 657 trials at its 73 states.
This single sensitivity does not replace the primary A7 result or validate one loading interpretation.
The exact inputs and results are in \nolinkurl{analyses/mea_parameter_bundle/results/joint-recalibration-170/amendment-7/restricted-2-x3/}; the primary size-probe reductions are in \nolinkurl{amendment-7/fixed-study/wheel-8298c602/size-probe/} under the same result directory.

\section{Complete A7 species, association and binary inputs}\label{sec:s-parameters}
% P:supplement-18
These tables list every species, association, correlation and binary input of the selected A7 parameter file, with its fitted, tied or fixed role.

The fixed neutral \MEA segment count, segment diameter, dispersion energy and self-association energy are the 2B values of Fakouri Baygi and Pahlavanzadeh \cite[Table~2]{Baygi2015}; the association-volume conversion used here is described in the main text.
