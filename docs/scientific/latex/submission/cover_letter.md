Dear Editors of Fluid Phase Equilibria,

Please consider “Thermodynamics of CO₂-loaded aqueous monoethanolamine with ePC-SAFT. Part I: phase equilibria, speciation, absorption heat, density and phase stability” for publication as a research article.

Part I jointly estimates sixteen ePC-SAFT parameters for aqueous MEA from pressure, speciation, integral absorption heat, density, heat capacity and binary equilibrium data. The work calculates absorption heat and density from the same Helmholtz energy and compares the phase behavior of parameter sets with similar property agreement.

For nominal 30 wt% MEA, pressure average absolute relative deviation (AARD) is 11.79% for 47 fitted and 23.82% for 112 non-fitted pressures (20.27% overall), over 313.00–393.15 K. For 94 of the 101 fitted species fractions, AARD is 7.11%; fitted integral-heat root-mean-square error is 1.84 kJ/mol CO₂, and fitted loaded-density AARD is 0.162% without volume translation. Non-fitted data were accessed during development; the comparisons are not blind tests.

A fit without a phase-stability requirement has detected liquid-phase instability at 54 of 162 searched states. Penalizing tangent-plane instability during fitting gives a parameter set with no detected instability at the 1903 penalized states or at 738 states checked outside the fit. These finite searches are numerical verification of phase behavior, not physical validation. Six parameters lie on bounds; conditional covariance gives pressure standard errors of 5.41–16.00% at the 47 fitted pressure states.

Pressure deviations at 15 and 45 wt% are 33.89% and 44.53%, respectively. Bicarbonate/carbonate and higher-temperature caloric discrepancies are reported with their source conflicts and model assumptions. A companion Part II on transfer of the shared parameters to other amine classes is in preparation.
