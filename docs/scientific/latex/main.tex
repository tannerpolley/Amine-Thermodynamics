\begin{abstract}
% P:abstract-01
Equilibrium \COtwo partial pressure over aqueous monoethanolamine (\MEA) depends on liquid speciation and phase equilibrium.
We present a nine-species electrolyte PC-SAFT (ePC-SAFT) model of \COtwo solubility in 30 wt\% \MEA with explicit carbamate chemistry, five reactions and the solvation-shell and dielectric-saturation (SSM+DS) Born term of Figiel et al.
Fitting five interaction coordinates to 48 \COtwo partial pressures (40 and \SI{60}{\degreeCelsius}) and 94 NMR and titration species measurements (20--\SI{60}{\degreeCelsius}) gives average absolute relative deviations (AARD) of 13.53\,\% and 8.28\,\%.
Prediction here means calculation outside the current fit.
For the 11 Jou 1995 targets at \SI{80}{\degreeCelsius}, the pressure AARD is 9.24\,\%; all 21 canonical rows at \SI{80}{\degreeCelsius} give 20.27\,\%.
These are tests outside the present (142-target, five-coordinate) fit, not data unseen during model development.
The SSM+DS and Original Born terms were fitted with identical coordinates and data.
Their ranking depends on the role of 18 Matin et al.\ bicarbonate observations, whose titration values those authors report as higher than NMR values.
Fitting them favors Original Born (weighted cost 40.20 against 47.63, 160 targets); leaving them report-only favors SSM+DS (32.99 against 34.58, 142 targets).
Transfer to 15 and 45 wt\% \MEA gives pressure AARDs of 75.95\,\% and 46.15\,\% (33 and 37 Aronu rows); 57 canonical pressure rows at 100--\SI{120}{\degreeCelsius} give 26.44\,\%.
The bicarbonate--water coefficient lies at its bound; density and heat capacity remain unqualified.
The results are numerical verification and assessment on existing measurements, not physical validation.
\end{abstract}
