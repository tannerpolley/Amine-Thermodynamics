## Kamps 2003: CO2 in aqueous PZ

**Property and method.** Total pressure p (MPa) at the bubble point of a known overall composition (synthetic method, p.2664). A known mass of CO2 is charged to a 30 cm³ cell with sapphire windows. Solvent is added until the gas dissolves completely, then liquid is withdrawn in small steps until the first stable bubbles appear. That pressure is the reported p. No phase is sampled or analysed.

**Basis.** Stoichiometric molalities of PZ and CO2, mol per kg water (Table 3; notation p.2669). The loading m̄_CO2/m̄_PZ is per PZ molecule.

**Conditions and rows (Table 3, p.2666).** 95 rows in 10 isotherm blocks:

|---|---:|---:|---|
| 313.13 | 1.995 | 8 | 313.75 |
| 333.14 | 1.995 | 8 | 334.05 |
| 333.14 | 2.035 | 6 | 334.05 |
| 353.16 | 1.995 | 13 | 354.35 |
| 373.15 | 2.035 | 10 | 374.65 |
| 393.15 | 2.035 | 10 | 395.0 |
| 333.16 | 3.964 | 13 | 334.05 |
| 353.14 | 3.964 | 8 | 354.35 |

That gives 55 rows near 2 m and 40 near 4 m. p spans 0.0133–9.560 MPa and m̄_CO2 spans 0–5.369 mol/kg. Hand check of the loadings:
- Two rows have m̄_CO2 = 0, both at 393 K (one at each PZ molality). They are unloaded total pressures.
- The 93 loaded rows span 0.50–1.69 mol CO2/mol PZ, which is 0.25–0.84 mol/mol alkalinity.
- 66 of the 93 rows are above 1.0 mol CO2/mol PZ.
- 19 rows are at or below 0.9 mol CO2/mol PZ, the upper end of Hilliard's suggested operating range of 0.25–0.45 mol CO2/(2 mol PZ).

The #237 data map's entry (95 rows, 314–395 K, 2.0–4.0 m, 0–5.4 mol/kg, 13–9 560 kPa) came from Ermatchkov 2006 Table 5, p.1793. It agrees with the paper. Its 314–395 K is the corrected temperature range.

**Precipitation.** One row: printed 313.13 K, m̄_PZ = 1.995, m̄_CO2 = 3.366 mol/kg (1.69 mol/mol PZ), p = 4.446 MPa. It is marked with an asterisk in Table 3 (footnote, p.2666). The text says "the precipitation of a salt (probably piperazine bicarbonate) was observed at high carbon dioxide molalities in the aqueous 2-molal piperazine solution at 313 K". It was "only observed in a single experiment", and "the precipitate could not be analyzed" (p.2665). The row was excluded from their fit (p.2667). The #237 map said "rows"; the paper, and Ermatchkov 2006 p.1793 ("one experimental point"), say one.

**Uncertainty (p.2664, p.2667).**
- T: below ±0.1 K as stated, but see the correction below.
- m̄_PZ: relative uncertainty below ±0.1 %.
- Charged CO2 mass (up to about 5 g): ±0.008 g.
- Solvent mass: at most ±0.7 % relative.
- p: ±0.1 % of the full scale of the transducer used (4 MPa or 10 MPa, so ±4 kPa or ±10 kPa). The paper does not say which transducer was used for which row.
- Below 0.1 MPa, the uncertainty "can reach up to 10 %" (p.2667).

Hand check: 4 rows lie below 0.1 MPa and 11 below 0.2 MPa. Their own Pitzer correlation has an average relative deviation of 4 % (4.3 % at 2 m, 3.5 % at 4 m). That excludes rows below 0.1 MPa and the precipitation row (p.2667). Nine Pitzer parameters were fitted to these rows (Table 2, p.2664).

**Temperature correction (Ermatchkov 2006, p.1792; Appendix Table 6, p.1795).** A recalibration of the thermometers showed that T = T_old + ΔT, with ΔT ≈ 0.6, 0.9, 1.2, 1.5 and 1.8 K at 313, 333, 353, 373 and 393 K. Table 6 reprints all 95 rows with corrected temperatures and the same footnote for the precipitation row.

Hand check of the reprint against Table 3: the 95 (m̄_CO2, p) pairs are identical except one. At about 2 m, 354 K and m̄_CO2 = 2.606, Kamps prints 5.063 MPa and Ermatchkov 2006 prints 5.062 MPa.

A second hand check supports the correction but is not a validation. At m̄_CO2 = 0 and 2.035 m, the measured 0.2021 MPa was compared with Raoult's-law water, x_w·p_sat, using the IAPWS saturation curve and neglecting PZ:
- at 393.15 K, x_w·p_sat = 191.6 kPa and measured/Raoult = 1.055;
- at 395.0 K, x_w·p_sat = 203.1 kPa and the ratio is 0.995.

The 3.950 m row gives 0.954 at 395.0 K, which is consistent with a water activity coefficient below one.

**Model conventions (Table 1, p.2663).** ln K_R = A + B/(T/K) + C ln(T/K) + D (T/K) + E/(T/K)², molality scale. K5 and K6 come from Hetzer 1968, and K7–K9 from Ermatchkov 2003 (p.2663). Hand check:
- R5: ln 10 × (6.1316, 1656.59, 0.006556) = (14.119, 3814.4, 0.015096). R6: ln 10 × (4.3919, 952.11, 0.007555) = (10.113, 2192.3, 0.017396). These are Hetzer's laws negated, so R5 and R6 are written as protonations: R5 is the reverse of our P1 and R6 the reverse of P2. They are stated valid for 273–323 K, and the paper applies them up to 393 K.
- R7, R8 and R9 are Ermatchkov 2003 reactions VI, VII and VIII times ln 10: 3.750, 1.587 and 4.354 give 8.635, 3.654 and 10.026; 1570.4, 574.2 and 1517.0 give 3616.0, 1322.1 and 3493.0. Table 1 prints 8.635/3616.1, 3.655/1322.3 and 10.026/3493.1.
- These reaction directions were inferred from the coefficient matches. The reaction scheme in Fig. 1 was not read.

Their model neglects amines in the vapor phase (p.2663).

**What the rows probe (pp.2665–2666).** The authors describe the liquid in two regions:
- above 1 mol CO2/mol PZ: the strong electrolyte PIPH3HCO3, plus molecular CO2 and the zwitterion dissolved physically;
- between 0.5 and 1 mol CO2/mol PZ: carbamate and bicarbonate salts of PZH+, plus PZ and the zwitterion.

Most Kamps rows therefore test PZH+–HCO3− and zwitterion interactions, and CO2 physical solubility at MPa pressure. They do not test the lean absorber region.

## MDEA + PZ blend (Kamps Table 4, p.2666)

- Conditions: 353.14 K as printed, m̄_MDEA = 1.975, m̄_PZ = 1.966 mol/kg.
- Rows: 10. m̄_CO2 runs 2.526–4.478 mol/kg (hand check: 0.64–1.14 mol CO2 per mol total amine). Total pressure runs 0.1807–6.400 MPa.
- The authors predicted these rows from the single-amine parameters and found deviations of about 35 % (p.2667, Fig. 4).
- Ermatchkov 2006 reprints only the PZ and H2S–PZ tables (Tables 6 and 8, p.1795), so the blend temperature has no published correction. By analogy with the 353 K PZ blocks it is probably about 1.2 K low. That is an inference.
- Other MDEA + PZ data cited on p.2668 were not read or acquired:
  - Bishnoi & Rochelle 2002, IECR 41, 604: 13 P_CO2 points, 4 M MDEA + 0.6 M PZ, 313 and 343 K, ±30 %;
  - Xu et al. 1998, IECR 37, 1473: 11 points at 343 K;
  - Liu et al. 1999, IECR 38, 4032: 80 points, 303–363 K.

## Yi 2024: an eNRTL cross-check

**Model.** Aspen Plus eNRTL with SRK vapor (§2.1, pp.3–4). PZ, AMP and H2O use pure-component reference states with extended Antoine vapor pressures; CO2 is a Henry component.
- The model has fifteen species. PZH2²+ and AMPCOO− are neglected: "the dissociation constant of PZH2²+ is very high [30–32]" (§2.1.2, p.3).
- The zwitterion is uncharged (`ZWITTER` = 1 for HPZCOO in SI sheet "Scalar parameters"). It is nonvolatile (`PLXANT` first coefficient −10²⁰, sheet "T-dependent parameters").
- Its reference state is unsymmetric under the ENRTL-RK method (Table 1, p.2; §2.1.3, p.4).

**PZ data fitted, rejected or compared (Table 3, p.2; SI Table S1).** Every accepted set was fitted. The fit was sequential: binary, then ternary, then quaternary (§2.1.2, p.4). A set was rejected if its AARD exceeded 40 % or if it raised the mean AARD by more than 20 percentage points (§2.2, p.7). Rejected sets have no printed AARD.

| System | Fitted (points, AARD) | Rejected (points) |
|---|---|---|
| PZ–H2O (SI Table S1) | Hartono 2013 PZ vapor pressure (24, 9.0 %); Wilson 1994 DIPPR total pressure (33, 6.2 %); Cp: Hilliard 2008 (34), Xu 2011 (17) | Hilliard 2008 VLE (47); Nguyen 2010 (12); Chen 2010 Cp (44) |
| PZ–CO2–H2O, CO2 partial pressure (Table 3) | Ermatchkov 2006 (52, 7.8 %); Hilliard 2008 (62, 18.0 %); Dash 2011 (314, 26.3 %); Derks 2005 (58, 27.9 %); Xu & Rochelle 2011 (159, 22.3 %); "Xu [37]" (35, 6.2 %); Hartono 2021 (28, 7.8 %) | Aroua 2004 (140); Haghtalab 2014 (7); Safdar 2016 (10); Bougie (64); Nguyen 2010 (2) |
| PZ–CO2–H2O, total pressure (Table 3) | Hartono 2021 (14, 20.4 %) | **Kamps 2003 (92)**; Kadiwala 2010 (42) |
| PZ–CO2–H2O, Cp | Hilliard 2008 (85, 0.1 %) | — |

Notes on the table:
- Ref. [37] for "Xu" is an undated Rochelle committee report, so that set's identity is unresolved.
- Bougie is listed as 2000, but ref. [43] is JCED 56 (2011) 1547.
- Yi's 92 Kamps points equal 95 rows minus 2 unloaded rows minus the precipitation row. That match is an inference; Yi gives no explanation.
- Yi's 47 Hilliard binary points compare with 46 rows counted in App. D.2 by #237. The difference was not resolved.

The SI rejects Hilliard and Nguyen PZ volatility because the PZ partial pressures there were "lower than 1 Pa", at the FTIR detection limit (SI p.1). It adds that Hilliard's values exceed the model below 2.5 m at low temperature, mostly at 0.1–10 Pa. Chen 2010 Cp is rejected as inconsistent with the Rochelle group's values.

**Comparisons not used as fitting targets.**

- *Kamps 2003, SI Fig. S10 (2 m only).* The SI introduction calls some figures comparisons against data "the model was not fitted to, a test set". Visual reading, not digitized:
  - the eNRTL curves lie above the 40 °C points at 1.2–1.55 mol/mol and below the 100–120 °C points near 0.8–1.1 mol/mol;
  - the temperatures are the nominal, uncorrected ones;
  - the lowest 40 °C point (Table 3: 1.452/1.995 = 0.73 mol/mol, 13.3 kPa) is plotted near 0.23 mol/mol, which looks like a plotting error in the figure.
- *NMR.*
  - SI Fig. S11 (1.4 m, 40 °C) cites Ermatchkov 2006. Ermatchkov 2006 shows only the D2O NMR of Ermatchkov 2003, at 298 K (Fig. 4, p.1793). The S11 points are therefore probably the Ermatchkov 2003 D2O series at 313 K (inference).
  - SI Fig. S12 shows Hilliard's 2 m, 27 °C NMR.
  - Yi does not mention the D2O solvent.
- *Absorption heat.* Kim's 2007 NTNU thesis (2.4 m; Fig. 3a and S13) and Hartono 2021 (S14–S16). Absorption heat was also one of the indicators used to tune the model (§2.3, item 3, p.8), so this is not a strict hold-out.

**AMP and the AMP/PZ blend (for the later blend).**
- AMP–CO2–H2O (Table 4, p.5):
  - fitted: Chen 2011 (13), Hartono 2020 (109), Li & Chang 1994 (27), Seo & Hong 1996 (17), Dash 2011 (166), Kundu 2003 (51), plus total pressure from Hartono 2020 (53) and Tong 2012 (45);
  - rejected: Jane 1997 (8) and Silkenbäumer 1998 (66).
- AMP–H2O (SI Table S2): Hartono 2013 (26), Pappa 2006 (27), Belabbaci 2010 (80), and Cp from Chiu 1999 and Chen 2001.
- AMPH+ formation properties come from pKa data and are compared with Hamborg 2009 (§3.2, p.10; Fig. S19). The AMP NMR comparison is Ciftja 2014 (Fig. 5b).
- AMP/PZ (Table 5, p.7):
  - fitted: Bruder 2011 (49), Li 2013 (83), Dash 2011 (160), Hartono 2021 (43), plus total pressure from Tong 2013 (99) and Hartono 2021 (276);
  - rejected: Dash 2012 (136, AARD 61.7 %) and Yang 2010 (145).
- The blend NMR of Li et al. 2014 has amine mass-balance deviations above 20 percentage points (p.11).

**Constants for P1–P5.** Yi does not use ln K laws: "the equilibrium constants are calculated based on Gibbs free energy difference between products and reactants" (§2.1.2, p.4). Its inputs are in SI sheet "Scalar parameters" (J/kmol), as aqueous infinite-dilution formation Gibbs energies and enthalpies, `DGAQFM`/`DHAQFM`:

| Species | `DGAQFM` | `DHAQFM` |
|---|---:|---:|
| PZH+ | 7.82E7 | −1.15E8 |
| PZH2²+ | 4.0368E7 | −1.4389E8 |
| HPZCOO | −2.78596E8 | −5.35204E8 |
| PZCOO− | −2.25605E8 | −4.98090E8 |
| PZ(COO)2²− | −5.73728E8 | −9.36851E8 |

PZ, AMP and HPZCOO also have ideal-gas `DGFORM`/`DHFORM` values. Constant aqueous heat capacities are given as `CPAQ0` (sheet "T-dependent parameters"), for example 1.6E5 J/(kmol K) for PZH+.

Where the values come from:
- PZH+ formation properties were set from PZH+ pKa data at 20–80 °C (§3.1, p.9). SI Fig. S4 compares them with Hamborg 2009 pKa over the same 20–80 °C range, so that agreement is calibration, not a test.
- The zwitterion `DGFORM` and the `CPAQ` of the protonated amines were adjusted while tuning the model (§2.3, item 4, p.8).
- The origin of the carbamate and dicarbamate values is not stated.
- PZH2²+ has formation values although the main text says the species was neglected. Its role in the reaction set was not resolved.
- The values differ from Hilliard's (thesis `DNQUPRMT`, Table 6.2-5, PDF p.174). For example, `DGAQFM` for PZH+ is 7.82E7 against Hilliard's 1.024E8, and for PZCOO− −2.256E8 against −2.164E8. They are not Hilliard's values adopted unchanged.
- The implied ln K were not evaluated. That needs the Aspen databank formation values for H2O, H3O+, CO2 and HCO3− and the PZ reference state, which the SI does not give.

Yi's model is an eNRTL fit to most of the PZ data in the public record. Its agreement with a dataset is calibration evidence for that model only. It is not an independent measurement and is not adopted here.
