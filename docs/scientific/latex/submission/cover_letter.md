Dear Editors of Fluid Phase Equilibria,

Please consider “Electrolyte PC-SAFT with carbamate chemistry for CO₂ in aqueous monoethanolamine: joint calibration and a finite phase test” for publication as a research article.

The manuscript examines an explicit-ion equation of state with carbamate chemistry and a solvation-shell Born term. Sixteen parameters are fitted jointly to 307 pressure, species, integral-heat, density, heat-capacity and binary-equilibrium quantities. The selected fit gives pressure and species average absolute relative deviations of 11.86% and 7.10%, and fitted integral-heat root-mean-square error of 1.83 kJ/mol CO₂. Densities are calculated without volume translation; 44 of 47 fitted loaded densities lie within twice their assigned scale.

The phase test covers only 313.15–333.15 K and loading 10⁻⁵–0.5. An original-bound fit has detected instability at 12 of 73 states; restricting its protonated-amine size and water-interaction bounds removes those detected instabilities in 657 trial phases, while increasing total cost by 0.45% and leaving three density deviations of 0.40–0.53% at 353.15 K. Those density states lie outside the phase-test temperature range. The paper reports the fitted MEAH⁺ dissociation law’s 0.49 pK departure from Bates–Pinching at 313.15 K and discusses parameters at bounds, source-data conflicts and remaining property discrepancies.

Joint multi-property fitting is established in the MEA literature. This work contributes the explicit-ion ePC-SAFT application and its paired property/phase-search comparison. Published model comparisons distinguish fitting roles, parameter counts, domains and error metrics. The conclusions concern local calibration and finite numerical searches; independent validation of phase behavior and process performance remains outstanding.

Processed observations, parameters, retained calculations and presentation code are prepared for a Zenodo deposit, with the DOI to be added at submission. The exact compiled ePC-SAFT Engine package will be provided to referees through restricted access coordinated by the corresponding author. Deposit publication, access arrangements and submission require the author’s final action.

The manuscript and Supplementary Material include the approved funding, competing-interest and generative-AI declarations. This letter is a local submission draft.
