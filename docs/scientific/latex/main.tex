\begin{abstract}
% P:abstract-01
Can one explicit-ion equation of state represent equilibrium, caloric and density properties of 30 wt\% aqueous monoethanolamine (\MEA) without a numerically resolved Gibbs-energy descent, that is, a lower-Gibbs-energy phase arrangement, at specified states, and what does that restriction cost?
A nine-species electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) model with carbamate chemistry is jointly calibrated through 16 adjustable parameters against 307 quantities in eight groups: pressure, species and integral heat in a subset shared with a predecessor fit, additional species observations, amount ratios to total amine for \MEAH, \MEACOO and \ce{HCO3- + CO3^{2-}} at two states, loaded density, \COtwo-free density, \MEA--water equilibrium, \COtwo--water pressure and heat capacity.
The common pressure and species subsets give average absolute relative deviations of 11.86 and 7.10\,\%, and fitted integral-heat root-mean-square error is \SI{1.83}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} \COtwo.
Without empirical volume translation, 44 of 47 fitted loaded densities fall within twice their declared scales.
No descent was numerically resolved in 657 trials at 73 specified states (313.15--\SI{333.15}{\kelvin}, $\alpha$ $10^{-5}$--0.5).
A preceding local fit to the same 307-row objective met all 47 loaded-density requirements but had resolved descent at 12 of these states; the selected endpoint has no resolved descent on this family but three density failures at \SI{353.15}{\kelvin}, 0.40--0.53\,\% below measurement and outside the phase family's temperatures.
Comparisons outside the objective are not independent validation: Arcis heats served as an acceptance check, and the Wagner pressures and 15 and 45 wt\% transfer data were examined during model development.
Carbonate share, the titration bicarbonate pool, unloaded heat capacity at \SI{318.15}{\kelvin} and 45 wt\% pressure transfer remain discrepant.
Two sequential fits, each from one start in sampled bounds, establish a constrained local feasibility result with disclosed density failures, conditional errors and finite phase evidence; they establish neither a global optimum nor physical validation of phase behavior or process performance.
\end{abstract}
