\subsection{Caloric and density calculations}\label{sec:heat-method}
% P:methods-heat-01
Eight Vinjarapu integral heats are fitted at \SI{313.15}{\kelvin} and \SI{1.734}{\mega\pascal}, from initially carbon-free 30 wt\% solvent \cite[Sects.~1.1.1 and 2.2, Table~4]{HemaBhavya2025}.
The signed heat per mole of added \COtwo is
\begin{equation}
 \Delta h=\frac{H_{\mathrm{final}}-H_{\mathrm{initial}}-\Delta n_{\COtwo}h_{\COtwo}^{\mathrm{feed}}}{\Delta n_{\COtwo}},
 \label{eq:addition-heat}
\end{equation}
where both equilibrium liquid enthalpies and the pure-\COtwo feed enthalpy are evaluated at the stated temperature and pressure; negative values denote exothermic absorption.
Fourteen Arcis heats at \SI{322.5}{\kelvin} and \SI{0.51}{\mega\pascal} or \SI{1.03}{\mega\pascal} are compared \cite[Sect.~2.4, Table~5]{arcisEnthalpySolutionCarbon2011}.

% P:methods-heat-02
Total enthalpy follows the same Helmholtz energy and reaction standard states as the equilibrium calculation:
\begin{equation}
 H=PV-RT^2\left(\frac{\partial(A/RT)}{\partial T}\right)_{V,\boldsymbol n},\qquad
 A=A^{\mathrm{ig}}+A^{\mathrm{res}}.
 \label{eq:total-enthalpy}
\end{equation}
Neutral ideal-gas functions supply the caloric reference; the ionic functions are completed from the five reaction laws, including $\Delta_rh^\circ=RT^2d\ln K_r/dT$ and the derivatives of the infinite-dilution reference corrections.
Reaction enthalpies are therefore included once through the standard-state functions.
The neutral ideal-gas coefficients follow NIST-JANAF and Zhang et al.\ \cite{chaseNISTJANAFThermochemicalTables1998,Zhang2011} and are listed in the data deposit; Section~S4 gives the functions and reference convention, including the extrapolation of the water Shomate function below \SI{500}{\kelvin}.
Equilibrium isobaric heat capacity is $C_P=(dH_{\mathrm{eq}}/dT)_{P,\boldsymbol n_{\mathrm{feed}}}$, including the change in speciation.
The objective uses $C_P/n_{\mathrm{feed}}$ per mole of complete analytical feed, with source mass-specific values converted on that same basis.
The mass-specific value is $c_P=C_P/m_{\mathrm{feed}}$, where $m_{\mathrm{feed}}=n_{\MEA}M_{\MEA}+n_wM_w+n_{\COtwo}M_{\COtwo}$ is the complete analytical-feed mass.
The four fitted Hilliard heat capacities use 7 mol \MEA/kg water, calculation pressure \SI{101325}{\pascal} and a $10^{-6}$ loading approximation for the nominally unloaded states \cite[Sects.~4.5--4.6, App.~G.2]{Hilliard2008}.

% P:methods-heat-03
Densities are calculated directly from the equation of state, without volume translation, so the density data constrain the same parameters that set the phase and reaction equilibria.
The fitted loaded observations comprise 15 Amundsen and 32 Hartono densities; five carbon-free densities use exactly zero analytical carbon, and all 67 Han densities are comparisons \cite{Amundsen2009,Hartono2014,hanDensityWater12012}.
Five Amundsen densities at loading 0.4 are excluded after comparison with Hartono values interpolated to the same loading, temperature and solvent composition; all five remain in the complete comparison.
The calculations use \SI{100000}{\pascal} for Amundsen where source pressure is unreported; other pressures follow the individual observations.
The loaded-density scale is
\begin{equation}
 s_{\rho,j}=\left[u_{\rho,j}^2+(g_j u_{\alpha,j})^2\right]^{1/2},
 \label{eq:density-scale}
\end{equation}
where $g_j$ is the density--loading secant between neighboring observations at the same source temperature and solvent composition, using a one-sided secant at the ends.
Section~S5 gives the source-specific scales and qualifications.
The scales combine heterogeneous uncertainty information; $2s$ is not uniformly a 95\,\% interval, and Amundsen's precise high-temperature/loading uncertainty boundary remains unresolved \cite[pp.~3099--3100]{Amundsen2009}.


\subsection{Parameters and fitting}\label{sec:parameters}
% P:methods-04
Parameters were estimated in four fits, each holding the earlier values fixed.
Five pure-water parameters were fitted to IAPWS-95, the \COtwo--water interaction and its temperature slope to Kiepe pressures, and the \MEA dielectric coefficients to unloaded \MEA--water permittivities; 16 parameters were then fitted jointly to the observations of \cref{tab:current-data-inventory}.
\Cref{tab:species-parameters,tab:interaction-matrix} list the species and pair parameters of the reported fit, with fitted values in bold, and \cref{sec:results-water,sec:results-co2-water,sec:results-permittivity,sec:results-joint} report each fit.
A fit with wider bounds on the protonated-amine diameter and its water interaction, the wider-bound fit, is specified in Table~\ref{supp-tab:comparison-fit-parameters}; the constant-interaction Cai calculation is specified in Section~S10.
Calculations for the reported fit use the parameter set listed here.
Rounding the reported parameter record to eight significant figures gives the fitted-data statistics of \cref{tab:residual-summary} at their printed precision.
Table~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-fitted-parameters} gives the joint-fit bounds and conditional standard errors; Section~S1 gives the source or modeling assumption of every fixed input.
The joint bounded least-squares objective is
\begin{equation}
 C(\boldsymbol\theta)=\frac12\sum_jw_jr_j^2,
 \label{eq:fit-objective}
\end{equation}
where $r_j$ is a property-scaled difference or log ratio and $w_j$ sets its weight; Section~S2 defines every residual, scale and weight.
Loaded densities have weight $1/47$, carbon-free densities weight 10, binary \MEA--water quantities unit weight, and \COtwo--water pressures weight $1/39$.
At the reported fit, the binary \MEA--water quantities contribute 61.71\,\% of the objective and loaded densities 0.3798\,\%; these cost shares describe fitting priorities rather than relative physical accuracy.
Section~S2 gives the complete group contributions.
The solver uses bounded Levenberg--Marquardt least squares with exact equation-of-state and implicit equilibrium derivatives.
Software identities are specified in Data and Code Availability.

% P:methods-05
Six parameters reach bounds: the \MEAH--water and \MEAH--\MEACOO interactions, the \MEAH segment diameter, both fitted Born diameters and the R5 enthalpy shift.
Both Born diameters are \SI{5}{\angstrom}, and the fitted unlike-ion interaction $k_{ij}=-1$ doubles the unlike-ion dispersion energy in \cref{eq:dispersion-combining-rule}.
The fitted R5 dissociation p$K$ exceeds Bates--Pinching by 0.49 at \SI{313.15}{\kelvin}, with dissociation enthalpy \SI{63.4}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} rather than \SI{50.5}{\kilo\joule\per\mole}; its total enthalpy shift reaches \SI{+10}{\kilo\joule\per\mole}.
Conditional errors and correlations in Section~S6 describe local sensitivity given the weights, fixed inputs and active bounds; they do not provide prediction intervals.

Agreement is reported as average absolute relative deviation (AARD),
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{AARD}=\frac{100\,\%}{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left|y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}/y_j^{\mathrm{obs}}-1\right|,
 \label{eq:aard}

\subsection{Phase-stability search}\label{sec:methods-phase}
% P:methods-phase-01
After fitting, the reported parameters and the wider-bound fit are searched for additional phases at specified states.
The tangent-plane criterion \cite[Sect.~2]{Michelsen1982} is applied to reacting, charge-neutral trials, with reduced distance
\begin{equation}
 D(\boldsymbol w)=g(T,P,\boldsymbol w)/(RT)-\boldsymbol w^{\mathsf T}B^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\lambda,
 \qquad \sum_iw_i=1,\quad\sum_i z_iw_i=0,\quad w_i>0,
 \label{eq:tpd}
\end{equation}
where $B$ represents conserved component combinations and $\boldsymbol\lambda$ their chemical-potential multipliers in the existing equilibrium.
A resolved instability requires a negative distance and a material-conserving finite transfer that lowers Gibbs energy beyond its numerical-error estimate; Section~S8 gives the method details and the searched states.
