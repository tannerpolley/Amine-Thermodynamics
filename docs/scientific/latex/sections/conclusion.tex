\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-mea
With the equilibrium constants fixed at literature correlations and the Born diameters of the amine ions tied to their segment diameters, an 11-parameter ePC-SAFT calibration describes the pressure, speciation, absorption heat, heat capacity and density of loaded aqueous MEA with one equation of state.
The fitted \COtwo pressures at nominal 30 wt\% deviate by 11.6\,\%, and finite phase-stability searches detect no instability, although stability was not imposed in the fit.
The restrictions cost accuracy outside the fit, and pressures at 15 and 45 wt\% MEA, carbonate shares and absorption heats at high temperature remain inaccurate.

% P:conclusion-transfer
The water, \COtwo and inorganic-ion parameters of that calibration, held fixed, support a fit of DEA, MDEA and AMP with four, eight and nine amine-specific parameters.
The fitted loaded-solution pressures deviate by 28.5\,\% for DEA, 20.6\,\% for MDEA and 10.4\,\% for AMP, and those not fitted by 15.4--84.9\,\%, so the transfer gives approximate rather than close pressures.

% P:conclusion-stability
Phase stability separates the three amines.
No instability is detected for DEA, whose water--DEA excess enthalpies nevertheless have the wrong sign.
For MDEA a calculated liquid--liquid gap of the \COtwo-free solution, found at 14 of 78 binary states after each penalized fit, is absent at the reported parameters, which are interpolated rather than converged; one loaded-solution split remains, at \SI{262.9}{\mega\pascal}.
The AMP model has at least 73 confirmed splits, all above the measured temperature range of its dissociation constant, and the stability-constrained alternative, with none detected, raises the deviation of the fitted pressures to 20.2\,\%.
None of the fits made here gives an AMP parameter set that both describes the fitted pressures that closely and has no calculated split.

% P:conclusion-published
On the 101 \COtwo partial pressures of Ermatchkov et al.\ the MDEA model deviates by 15.4\,\%, against 16.8\,\% for their parameter set I; neither was fitted to these data, but they helped choose the initial MDEA parameters, so this is not a blind test.
On the 28 total pressures of P\'erez-Salado Kamps et al., to which both published sets were fitted and the MDEA model was not, the published sets are far closer.

% P:conclusion-limits
All four models are calibrations against data examined during development and provide neither physical validation nor a basis for absorber design.
