### Every binding bound: physics, evidence and proposed treatment

The mixing rule is $u_{ij}=\sqrt{u_i u_j}(1-k_{ij})$ (Uyan 2015 Eq.7; Wangler 2018 Eq.11). Negative k increases unlike attraction. Positivity of size, Born diameter and u, and k<1 for positive unlike dispersion are physical/model restrictions. None of 3 Å, 7 Å, 500 K, 8 Å or −0.5 is a universal physical boundary. Effective sphere diameter, chain segment diameter, packing diameter, Debye–Hückel diameter and Born diameter are different quantities. Compare excluded-volume scale $m\sigma^3$, not sigma alone, across A/B. Preserve the actual packing conventions: A and carbamates use 0.88 sigma; B uses neutral temperature-dependent packing.

### Stability in the objective and at which states

Adopt the **method**, not MEA's acceptance percentages, from [#255](https://github.com/tannerpolley/Amine-Thermodynamics/issues/255) and [its retained design and results](../mea_parameter_bundle/results/stable-refit-255/README.md). That study moved an unstable wide-bound fit to an available/no-descent candidate with data cost 148.510546 versus 148.883260 on 307 rows, using 1903 enforced states and separate check grids. Its physical results and numerical availability band do not transfer to PZ.

Use the native parameter-fitting interface `reg.stability(parameters, liquid_parent, scale=s, pressure_reference=bubble_problem, pressure_ratio=c)` alongside the existing 294 data observations. It searches Full optimized trials with candidate-dependent bubble reference pressure. The objective is

$$C(u)=\tfrac12\|L^{-1}(y(u)-y_{obs})\|^2+\tfrac12\sum_j[m_j(u)/s]^2.$$

Here $LL^T$ is the approved data covariance; $m_j$ is the Engine's minimum raw reduced TPD on a resolved descending branch, or zero only for a completed available search with no witness.
