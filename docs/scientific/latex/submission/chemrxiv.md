# ChemRxiv submission draft

## Title

Electrolyte PC-SAFT with carbamate chemistry for CO₂ in aqueous monoethanolamine: joint calibration and a finite phase test

## Author and affiliation

Tanner W. Polley; ORCID 0009-0008-5957-4152; tpolley3@byu.edu. Department of Chemical Engineering, Brigham Young University, Provo, UT 84602, United States.

## Abstract

We jointly calibrate a nine-species electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) model of CO₂ absorption in 30 wt% aqueous monoethanolamine (MEA), with explicit carbamate chemistry and a solvation-shell Born term. Sixteen parameters are fitted to 307 quantities: pressures, species fractions, integral heats, densities, heat capacities and binary equilibria. The selected fit gives average absolute relative deviations of 11.86 and 7.10% for 47 fitted pressures and 94 species fractions, and integral-heat root-mean-square error of 1.83 kJ/mol CO₂. Densities are calculated without volume translation; 44 of 47 fitted loaded densities lie within twice their assigned scale. The leading limitation is the phase-test range: 73 states at 313.15–333.15 K and loading 10⁻⁵–0.5, below the 353.15 K density failures. An original-bound fit has numerically resolved instability at 12 states; restricting the protonated-amine size and water-interaction bounds removes all detected instabilities in 657 trials, while increasing total cost by 0.45% and leaving three densities 0.40–0.53% low. The fitted protonated-amine dissociation pK exceeds Bates–Pinching by 0.49 pK at 313.15 K. Carbonate share, titration bicarbonate, unloaded heat capacity and 45 wt% pressure transfer remain discrepant. Heat temperature dependence is untested beyond the fitted and compared states. The results describe local calibration and finite numerical searches; previously examined comparisons and the heat-based selection check provide no independent validation of phase behavior or process performance.
