## Pure-parameter conventions

Razavi's five PC-SAFT numbers are recorded once in [pure-pz-pcsaft.md](pure-pz-pcsaft.md#the-candidate-and-its-origin). Esmaeili prints the same values rounded. Pereira Table 1 p.693 instead gives soft-SAFT m=2.261, σ=3.882 Å, ε/kB=364.0 K, association energy 1600 K and association volume **2000 Å³**, with two donors/two acceptors. Its volume is dimensional and belongs to its soft-SAFT association expression; it is not Razavi's dimensionless κ=0.07275 with a σ³ prefactor. Section 4.1.2/Table 2 gives PZ–water association energy 2643 K and volume 2436 Å³, with the size/energy correction factors fixed to one. Pure and mixture estimation are coupled.

Mota Tables 1–2 give CPA a0=2.25 Pa m⁶ mol⁻², c1=1.29, b=10.61×10⁻⁵ m³ mol⁻¹, association energy 3.57 kJ mol⁻¹ and dimensionless association volume 0.1. These are cubic/association coefficients, not a PC-SAFT chain description. The paper's fusion input 11.70 kJ/mol and melting 379 K differ from Steele's 26.7±0.3 kJ/mol and 384.6 K; its aqueous-solubility fit cannot be imported without resolving that source convention.

Perdomo 2021/2023 uses group-specific sizes, shape factors, repulsive exponents and group-pair energies/volumes (2021 Tables 2–4; 2023 Tables 2–5 and C.13). There is no scalar five-parameter substitution. No calculation converting or comparing numerical predictions has been performed. A different SAFT model's smaller fit deviation is not evidence that its parameters work with the approved Engine equations.

The PZ groups in Perdomo 2023 Table 3 (retained from its refs.87–88), with PZ topology illustrated in Fig.1, are:

Table 4 gives the cCH2–cNH dispersion energy 605.45 K and a combining-rule repulsive exponent; Table 5 gives cNH(e)–cNH(H) association energy 1511.6 K and bonding volume 87.651 Å³. These group parameters represent the PZ molecular groups; they are not a molecular PC-SAFT parameter set. Table C.13 identifies the required group-pair interactions. The 2023 values provide a PDF-verified record of the inherited neutral model even though the 2021 PDF remains unavailable. No new pure-PZ density validation is provided by their reuse.
