# ChemRxiv submission draft

## Title

Thermodynamics of CO₂-loaded aqueous amines with ePC-SAFT: monoethanolamine calibration, transfer to DEA, MDEA and AMP, and phase stability

## Author and affiliation

Tanner W. Polley; ORCID 0009-0008-5957-4152; tpolley3@byu.edu. Department of Chemical Engineering, Brigham Young University, Provo, UT 84602, United States.

## Abstract

Can two calibrations of an equation of state that reproduce the measured pressures of aqueous amines loaded with carbon dioxide (CO₂) about equally well predict different liquid phases? An electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) model of monoethanolamine (MEA) is calibrated first, and its water, CO₂ and inorganic-ion parameters are held fixed while diethanolamine (DEA), methyldiethanolamine (MDEA) and 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol (AMP) are each fitted. Phase-stability searches separate calibrations that the pressure deviations do not. For AMP, two fits deviate by 10.3 and 10.5% from the same fitted total pressures, yet the first gives 73 calculated liquid–liquid splits above 353 K, the highest temperature of the fitted loaded-solution pressures, and the second none. For MDEA, changing one shared MEA interaction lowers the deviation of the fitted total pressures from 20.1 to 11.2% but raises that of the CO₂ partial pressures outside the fit from 20.1 to 33.6%, and its searches confirm calculated splits. The MEA and DEA models have no detected instability, and DEA is a negative result: with the adopted DEA pure-component and association parameters, no tested water–DEA interaction represents the binary excess enthalpies and bubble pressures together. The four models and their phase searches are offered as a testbed for calibration practice, in which a fit is judged by the phases it predicts as well as by its deviations, rather than as a basis for absorber design.

## Keywords

ePC-SAFT; monoethanolamine; diethanolamine; methyldiethanolamine; 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol; parameter transfer; phase stability.

## Data availability

The electronic data comprise processed observations, parameter values, numerical results and the analysis and figure code under the MIT license; third-party measurements remain under the terms of their sources. They will be deposited in Zenodo, and the DOI will be added at submission. A software index in the deposit lists the versions, checksums and commands needed to repeat the calculations. The MEA calculations were made with version 1.3.0 of the epcsaft package, and the DEA, MDEA and AMP calculations with version 1.3.1. Referees will receive access to the compiled epcsaft package through the corresponding author.
