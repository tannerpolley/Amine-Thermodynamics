## What changed in this revision

1. **Pressure-tracking relation in the methods.** New Eq. 9 (§2.3) gives (∂G*/∂P)_{T,n} − V_EOS = RT Σ nᵢ (∂bᵢ/∂P_src) dP_src/dP, with R1–R3 tracking and R4–R5 at 0.1 MPa. §2.4 states the caloric consistency check. Sizes and the 204.054 MPa reference test are in S3.
2. **MDEA has two confirmed splits.** The grid split is at 204.054 MPa and a targeted split at 262.862 MPa, both from the reported model. The abstract, §4.3, the conclusions and S7.1 now state both. The 25 measured-state descents were reclassified for the reported model: all point toward a vapor, below the model bubble pressure.
3. **AMP claim narrowed.** At the six measured states where the first fit split, the AMP model confirms none, but four stay unresolved. The removal is credited to the refit as performed.
