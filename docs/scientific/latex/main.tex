\begin{abstract}
% P:abstract-01
Can one electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) Helmholtz energy describe the equilibrium, caloric and density properties of loaded aqueous monoethanolamine with literature reaction constants and fewer adjustable parameters, without predicting an additional liquid?
A joint calibration with eleven adjustable parameters, fixed literature reaction constants and amine-ion Born diameters tied to their segment diameters describes these properties, and finite phase searches detect no additional liquid although no stability penalty entered the fit.
For nominal 30 wt\% monoethanolamine, fitted carbon-dioxide pressure average absolute relative deviation is 11.64\,\%, integral absorption-heat root-mean-square error is \SI{1.98}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} carbon dioxide, and fitted loaded-density deviation is 0.152\,\% without volume translation.
The restrictions have a measured cost: on the Ma'mun observations, which are not fitted, pressure deviation rises from 19.43\,\% for a 16-parameter calibration that also shifts two of those constants and fits separate amine-ion Born diameters to 33.47\,\%.
An unpenalized comparison fit instead predicts an ion-rich, water-poor liquid through a coupled energy balance with a prominent dispersion change; no unique microscopic cause is identified.
Pressures at 15 and 45 wt\% monoethanolamine, carbonate shares and high-temperature absorption heat remain inaccurate.
All comparisons use data seen during development, so they describe a calibration rather than independent validation, and parameter uncertainty is conditional on the fit.
Part I supplies the equations and shared inputs for the companion study of other amines.

\end{abstract}
