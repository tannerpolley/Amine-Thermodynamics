\section{Data and parameter estimation}\label{sec:data-methods}
\subsection{Experimental data}\label{sec:data-roles}
% P:methods-01
Loading is $\alpha=n_{\COtwo,\mathrm{added}}/n_{\mathrm{amine,total}}$, and the amine content is the amine mass fraction of the unloaded solution, written in wt\%.
\emph{Fitted} means that an observation enters the objective of the reported fit, and \emph{not fitted} that it is outside that objective.
\emph{Comparison only} marks a different quantity or different conditions from those the model was built for, and \emph{development} data are observations outside the objective that informed a choice of parameters or of an initial parameter set.
Every observation was examined during model development, so no comparison in this paper is an independent validation or a blind test.
\Cref{tab:calibration-data} lists, for each amine, the data in the objective, the data fitted beforehand and the development data.
The objectives contain 307 observations for MEA, 140 for DEA, 281 for MDEA and 214 for AMP; Tables~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-mea-comparison} and \ref{supp-tab:supplement-transfer-data} give every other set that is compared.

% P:methods-selection
The fitted sets were chosen amine by amine, not by one rule.
The MEA objective contains \COtwo partial pressures only at nominal 30 wt\% and 313--\SI{333}{\kelvin}; the pressures at 15 and 45 wt\% and at higher temperatures are compared but not fitted.
The DEA objective has three loaded-pressure sources, 30 pressures of Lawson and Garst, 16 of Seo and Hong and 44 of Lal et al.; the cell of Lawson and Garst also contained methane, which the calculation omits (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-corrections}), and their seven pressures at \SI{394}{\kelvin} are not fitted.
For MDEA the loaded total pressures of Kuranov et al.\ at \SI{413}{\kelvin} were set aside as an extrapolation.
For AMP all loaded total pressures of Hartono et al.\ and of Tong et al., 313--\SI{393}{\kelvin}, are fitted; a first AMP fit used only those of Hartono et al.\ at 313--\SI{353}{\kelvin}, and \cref{sec:results-phase} reports what that restriction caused.
The fitted loaded pressures of MDEA and AMP are total pressures, and their \COtwo partial pressures are not fitted.

% P:methods-range
A calculation is attempted for every observation, including those outside the measured temperature range of an equilibrium constant (\cref{tab:reaction-equilibrium-constants}).
For MEA the reacting calculations span 293.15--\SI{393.15}{\kelvin}, so R4 and R5 are extrapolated above \SI{323.15}{\kelvin}; for DEA, MDEA and AMP, Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-ranges} reports the deviations inside and outside each range separately.

% P:methods-02
Each observable follows its experimental definition: a sum reported by nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) is compared with the same calculated sum \cite{Bottinger2008}, and bubble calculations omit carrier gas, so calculated $y_{\COtwo}P$ is compared with a measured \COtwo partial pressure and calculated $P$ with a measured total pressure.
Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-corrections} gives the conventions of the individual sources and the excluded observations.

\subsection{Calculated quantities}\label{sec:heat-method}
% P:methods-heat-01
Pressures are reactive bubble pressures at the measured temperature and apparent composition, and species fractions are those of a single liquid at an assigned pressure.
The integral absorption heat per mole of added \COtwo is
\begin{equation}
 \Delta h=\frac{H_{\mathrm{final}}-H_{\mathrm{initial}}-\Delta n_{\COtwo}h_{\COtwo}^{\mathrm{feed}}}{\Delta n_{\COtwo}},
 \label{eq:addition-heat}
\end{equation}
with both equilibrium liquids and the pure-\COtwo feed at the same temperature and pressure; negative values denote exothermic absorption.
The semi-differential heats of Kim and Svendsen are calculated over each finite addition \cite{Kim2007}.

\subsection{Objective, deviations and conditional standard errors}\label{sec:parameters}
% P:methods-04
Estimation is sequential.
The water parameters, the \COtwo--water interaction, fitted to the pressures of Kiepe et al.\ \cite{kiepeExperimentalDeterminationPrediction2002}, the permittivity coefficients and the two salt-fitted water--anion interactions (\cref{sec:species}) are determined first and remain fixed.
Eleven MEA parameters are then estimated jointly (\cref{tab:species-parameters,tab:interaction-matrix}), and with all shared parameters fixed, four DEA, eight MDEA and nine AMP parameters are fitted (\cref{tab:transfer-fitted}).
Parameters were also set beforehand: the two permittivity coefficients of each amine, the DEA--water interaction and the five pure-AMP parameters (\cref{tab:transfer-species}, Table~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-transfer-parameters}); with these, 7 quantities are adjusted for DEA, 10 for MDEA and 16 for AMP.
Each fit minimizes, by bounded least squares,
\begin{equation}
 C(\boldsymbol\theta)=\frac12\sum_jw_jr_j^2,\qquad
 r_j=\frac{y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}-y_j^{\mathrm{obs}}}{s_j}
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 r_j=\frac{\ln(y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}/y_j^{\mathrm{obs}})}{s_j},
 \label{eq:fit-objective}
\end{equation}
with the logarithmic form for pressures.

% P:methods-weights
Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-objective} gives the scale $s_j$ and weight $w_j$ of each data set.
Several are assigned without a statistical derivation and they differ between the amines, so an objective value sets fitting priorities within one fit and does not compare accuracy between amines.
Table~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-objective-contributions} gives the contribution of every data set to its objective.
The loaded-solution pressures contribute 94.0\,\% of the DEA objective and 97.1\,\% of the AMP objective.
In the MDEA objective each group enters as the mean of its squared standardized residuals, so that the many densities do not dominate.
There the loaded total pressures contribute 32.9\,\% and the 24 densities of the \COtwo-free solution 11.0\,\%.
In the MEA objective the 50 MEA--water observations of Cai et al.\ \cite{caiBinaryIsobaricVaporLiquid1996} contribute 59.9\,\% of the objective value, 152.96, but respond only to the MEA--water interaction and its temperature coefficient, so they determine those two parameters and leave the weighting among the loaded-solution data unchanged.

% P:methods-starts
Each fit is started from two initial parameter sets, three for MDEA, and the converged solution with the lower objective value is reported; Table~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-fit-starts} lists every start.
The two starts reach the same objective value within a relative $10^{-6}$ for MEA and $10^{-10}$ for DEA and AMP, and two of the three MDEA starts end within 1.2\,\% of each other, at 17.401 and 17.600, the third far higher, at 148.906.
These are local solutions: no wider search over starting sets has been made, and the two MEA starts descend from the 16-parameter calibration described next.

% P:methods-restrictions
For MEA the joint fit is also made with fewer restrictions.
The 16-parameter calibration shifts the R2 and R5 correlations, fits separate Born diameters for the two MEA ions and the water--bicarbonate interaction, and is fitted with the stability penalty of \cref{sec:methods-phase}.
The \emph{MEA model} used throughout fixes R2 and R5 at their literature forms, ties the Born diameters to the segment diameters and fixes the two water--anion interactions from the salt data; its eleventh fitted parameter is the \MEAH--bicarbonate interaction, and its final optimization used no stability penalty.

% P:methods-06
Agreement is reported as average absolute relative deviation (AARD), relative bias and root-mean-square error (RMSE),
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{AARD}=\frac{100\,\%}{N}\sum_j\left|\frac{y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}}{y_j^{\mathrm{obs}}}-1\right|,
 \qquad \operatorname{bias}=\frac{100\,\%}{N}\sum_j\left(\frac{y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}}{y_j^{\mathrm{obs}}}-1\right),
 \qquad \operatorname{RMSE}=\sqrt{\frac1N\sum_j(y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}-y_j^{\mathrm{obs}})^2},
 \label{eq:aard}
\end{equation}
where $N$ is the number of observations in the set, restricted for AARD and bias to nonzero observations.
For the parameters that are not on a bound, the local covariance is
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{cov}(\boldsymbol\theta_I)=s_C^2(J_I^{\mathsf T}J_I)^{-1},\qquad
 s_C^2=\frac{\sum_{j\in\mathcal R}w_jr_j^2}{N_{\mathrm{resp}}-|I|},
 \label{eq:conditional-covariance}
\end{equation}
where $J_I$ is the weighted residual Jacobian and $\mathcal R$ is the set of the $N_{\mathrm{resp}}$ observations in the objective whose calculated value depends on at least one fitted parameter; a parameter on a bound is held fixed and has no standard error.
Observations that respond to no fitted parameter are left out of both the sum and the count: the 39 Kiepe pressures of the MEA objective, which leaves 268 observations and 259 residual degrees of freedom, and three pure-water pressures of the MDEA objective, which leaves 278 observations and 271 residual degrees of freedom.
These standard errors are conditional on the model form, the equilibrium-constant correlations, the fixed shared parameters, the scales, the weights and the local linearization; they exclude model discrepancy and systematic differences between sources and do not define prediction intervals.

\subsection{Phase-stability search and stability penalty}\label{sec:methods-phase}
% P:methods-phase-01
Each fitted parameter set is searched for a phase split with the tangent-plane criterion \cite{Michelsen1982}, with electroneutral trial phases \cite{Perdomo2025} and reaction-equilibrium constraints \cite{Tsanas2022,Ascani2022}:
\begin{equation}
 D(\boldsymbol w)=g(T,P,\boldsymbol w)/(RT)-\boldsymbol w^{\mathsf T}B^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\lambda,
 \quad \sum_iw_i=1,\quad\sum_i z_iw_i=0,\quad w_i>0.
 \label{eq:tpd}
\end{equation}
Here $\boldsymbol w$ is the composition of a trial phase, restricted to the species that can form from the feed and, for a vapor, to \COtwo, water and the molecular amine; $B$ is the conservation matrix, whose rows are the independent conserved groups of the true species, so that $B\boldsymbol\nu_r=0$ for every reaction; and $\boldsymbol\lambda$ holds the Lagrange multipliers of the corresponding balances at the searched state, divided by $RT$, so that $B^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\lambda$ is the tangent plane of that state.
A negative distance that is confirmed by a finite, mass-conserving transfer lowering the Gibbs energy by more than its numerical error estimate is a \emph{detected instability}, classed as toward a second liquid or toward a vapor from the density of the trial phase.
A liquid--liquid split is \emph{confirmed} when a separate equilibrium calculation at the same feed, temperature and pressure returns two distinct liquids of lower Gibbs energy.
The searched states are grids of temperature, amine content and loading, mostly liquids at 1.05 and 3 times the calculated bubble pressure, and, for DEA, MDEA and AMP, also the measured states (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-phase} gives the grids, the starting compositions and the acceptance tests).
A search that does not finish is counted as not completed, never as stable, and a finite search gives no proof of global stability.

% P:methods-exceptions
Stability enters a fit in two places only.
The 16-parameter MEA calibration adds a penalty on the most negative tangent-plane distance $\tau_k$ found at each searched state $k$, zero when the completed search finds none:
\begin{equation}
 C_{\mathrm{pen}}=C+\frac12\sum_k\left(\frac{\tau_k}{s_{\mathrm{stab}}}\right)^2,
 \quad s_{\mathrm{stab}}\in\{10^{-2},3\times10^{-3}\},
 \label{eq:stability-penalty}
\end{equation}
with unit weight and the two scales applied in succession; the MEA, DEA and AMP models were fitted without it.
For MDEA one interaction is bounded.
Only the MDEA--water interaction acts on the \COtwo-free solution, and a fit without a bound, made with the MEA-fitted shared set, converged where that solution has a calculated liquid--liquid gap at searched states between 393 and \SI{423}{\kelvin}.
Its value at \SI{423}{\kelvin}, the highest temperature tested, is therefore bounded above at $-0.0671$, $10^{-4}$ below the largest tested value without a gap at \SI{1.466}{\mega\pascal}; the bound was fixed before fitting (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-mdea-bound}).
The loaded MDEA states are not constrained.
