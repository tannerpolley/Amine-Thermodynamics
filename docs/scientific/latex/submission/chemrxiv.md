# ChemRxiv submission draft

## Title

Modeling CO₂ absorption in aqueous monoethanolamine with ePC-SAFT

## Author and affiliation

Tanner W. Polley; ORCID 0009-0008-5957-4152; tpolley3@byu.edu. Department of Chemical Engineering, Brigham Young University, Provo, UT 84602, United States.

## Abstract

We examine whether one explicit-ion electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) formulation with carbamate chemistry can represent the equilibrium, caloric and density properties of loaded aqueous monoethanolamine (MEA). After separate water, CO₂–water and dielectric fits, sixteen parameters are fitted jointly to pressure, species, integral absorption heat, density, heat capacity and binary equilibrium data. For nominal 30 wt% MEA, one parameter set thus represents CO₂ pressure, the amine and carbamate fractions, integral heat at 313.15 K and loaded density through 353.15 K. For nominal 30 wt% MEA, pressure average absolute relative deviations are 11.86% over 47 fitted observations at 313.00–333.15 K and 20.55% over 159 observations at 313.00–393.15 K, including 112 not fitted but examined during model development. The shared subset of 94 fitted species fractions gives 7.10% at 293.15–333.15 K. The fitted integral-heat root-mean-square error is 1.84 kJ/mol CO₂; the average absolute relative density deviation is 0.18% over 47 fitted loaded densities, calculated without volume translation, with 44 within twice the assigned scale and three accepted misses at 353.15 K. The 159-pressure deviation lies within the range of pressure deviations reported for related MEA models, which use different data sets and fitting roles. A fit with wider bounds on the protonated-amine diameter and its water interaction matches the properties similarly but predicts a second liquid phase at some states; narrowing these bounds removes the detected instabilities in a finite search at 162 states between 313.15 and 393.15 K, with a 0.45% higher objective value. Carbonate shares, titration bicarbonate, the 0.51 MPa Arcis integral heats, loaded densities above 353.15 K and the unloaded heat capacity at 318.15 K remain discrepant, and pressure deviations reach 29.5 and 56.0% at 15 and 45 wt% MEA. The available comparisons do not yet establish suitability across absorber and stripper temperatures.
