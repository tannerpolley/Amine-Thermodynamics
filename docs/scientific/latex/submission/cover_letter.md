Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript "Electrolyte PC-SAFT with carbamate chemistry for CO2 in aqueous
monoethanolamine: the role of the Born term and of speciation data" for publication in
*Fluid Phase Equilibria*.

The manuscript continues the electrolyte PC-SAFT (ePC-SAFT) line that Held, Sadowski and
co-workers developed for reactive aqueous methyldiethanolamine (MDEA), which forms no carbamate,
and extends it to monoethanolamine (MEA), the reference solvent for CO2 capture, whose carbamate
chemistry changes the ion population with loading. A nine-species ePC-SAFT model with
Debye–Hückel and Born contributions is fitted through five binary interaction parameters to 47
CO2 partial pressures at 40 and 60 °C and 94 NMR and titration species measurements in 30 wt%
MEA, with average absolute relative deviations (AARD) of 13.81 % and 7.89 %. On Wagner et al.
pressures near 80 and 119 °C (353 and 392 K), which entered no fitting or selection step, it
gives 23.11 % and 16.23 %; the other data at 80–120 °C informed fixed reaction shifts, so they
are not presented as independent temperature tests.

To our knowledge, this is the first application of electrolyte PC-SAFT with both Debye–Hückel
and Born contributions to aqueous MEA–CO2 with explicit carbamate chemistry, and the first
simultaneous estimation of liquid-phase ePC-SAFT parameters against both CO2 partial pressures
and measured liquid speciation for loaded MEA. Within the declared bounds, the refit without the
Born term reaches a fitted-pressure AARD of 80.25 %, and a decomposition of the calculated
pressure shows that the refitted interactions offset the loading-dependent Born contribution at
low loading but not at high loading. The solvation-shell and dielectric-saturation Born term of
Figiel et al. and the original Born term calibrate comparably, and which has the lower cost
depends on whether titration bicarbonate data are fitted. A refit with literature reaction
temperature laws matches the fit at or below 60 °C but gives 54.98 % at 100–120 °C, so
independent temperature-sensitive data are needed to constrain the high-temperature response.

The manuscript states its scope in one place: the model is a phase- and speciation-equilibrium
description of 30 wt% MEA, with conditional parameter uncertainties, and loaded-solution density
and transfer to other amine concentrations are reported as limitations. The parameter files,
retained calculations and figure data are public at the repository commit cited in the Data and
Code Availability statement.
