\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-question
Fits of an ePC-SAFT model that reproduce measured pressures about equally well need not predict the same liquid phases.
With the water, \COtwo and inorganic-ion parameters of an MEA calibration held fixed, two AMP fits deviate by 10.3 and 10.5\,\% from the same 47 fitted total pressures; the first gives 73 confirmed liquid--liquid splits at 363--\SI{393}{\kelvin}, above the fitted loaded-pressure range, and the second gives none in 666 finite searches.
The calculated splits disappear when either the cation--water interaction or the AMP dissociation constant is held at its value at the upper end of the fitted pressure range.

% P:conclusion-transfer
The choice made for a shared parameter matters downstream.
Fixing the shared water--bicarbonate interaction at 0.1325 instead of 0.7650 raises the refitted MEA objective by 1.023, within the range of increases found in the MEA bound refits, but lowers the deviation of the 66 fitted MDEA total pressures from 20.1 to 11.2\,\%, lowers that of 54 outside-fit total pressures from 38.7 to 33.0\,\%, raises that of 121 outside-fit \COtwo partial pressures from 20.1 to 33.6\,\% and introduces two confirmed splits.
\emph{Transfer} in this work therefore means that DEA, MDEA and AMP could be fitted with the shared parameters held fixed, not that those parameters are determined by MEA.

% P:conclusion-models
The MEA model, an 11-parameter calibration with its equilibrium constants fixed at literature correlations, deviates by 11.6\,\% from 47 fitted \COtwo partial pressures and shows no detected instability in 2646 searches; its final optimization used no stability penalty, although its starts descended from a penalized calibration.
Its pressures at 15 and 45 wt\% MEA, carbonate shares and absorption heats at high temperature remain inaccurate, and the cause of the carbonate deficit is unresolved.
The DEA model deviates by 28.5\,\% from 30 fitted \COtwo partial pressures and gives the wrong sign of the water--DEA excess enthalpy; a refit that corrects the enthalpy describes the binary and the unfitted loaded-solution pressures less closely.
The MDEA model deviates by 20.1\,\% from 66 fitted total pressures; a bound keeps its \COtwo-free solution free of a calculated gap through \SI{423}{\kelvin}, no second liquid is confirmed at the measured states at 0.100--\SI{4.190}{\mega\pascal}, and one two-liquid solution is confirmed at \SI{262.862}{\mega\pascal}, far above process conditions.
Unlike those of MEA, the carbamate-hydrolysis constants of DEA and AMP are fitted, not fixed at their literature correlations.

% P:conclusion-limits
All four models are calibrations against data examined during development, with phase behaviors calculated by finite searches; they provide neither physical validation nor a basis for absorber design.
