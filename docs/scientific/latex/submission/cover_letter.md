Dear Editor,

Please consider “Electrolyte PC-SAFT with carbamate chemistry for CO2 in aqueous monoethanolamine: joint calibration and a finite phase test” for *Fluid Phase Equilibria*.

The manuscript asks whether one explicit-ion equation of state can jointly represent pressure, speciation, caloric and density observations while showing no resolved phase descent at specified states. A nine-species ePC-SAFT model with carbamate chemistry is calibrated through 16 coordinates against 307 quantities in eight groups. Common-subset pressure/species deviations are 11.86/7.10%, and fitted integral-heat RMSE is 1.83 kJ/mol CO2.

The central comparison is between two local endpoints. A6 has resolved descent at 12 of 73 specified states; no descent was numerically resolved for accepted A7 in 657 trials at 73 specified states (313.15–333.15 K, α 10⁻⁵–0.5). A7 retains three density failures at 353.15 K, with 44 of 47 fitted loaded densities within twice their declared scales, without empirical volume translation. The warm-density failures lie outside the phase family's temperatures. This comparison establishes no minimum density penalty, isolated Born or heat effect, global optimum or physical phase validation.

The paper gives equal visibility to the carbonate-share and titration-pool discrepancies, unloaded heat capacity at 318.15 K and 45 wt% pressure transfer. Arcis heat is an acceptance check, and Wagner and transfer data were previously accessed. Two sequential fits each use one start, and parameter errors are conditional on weights, fixed inputs, active bounds and local linearization. No accuracy ranking against published models or absorber/stripper performance claim is made.

The A7 data release DOI is pending; the locally prepared release identifies the selected parameter hash, Engine commit and wheel hash. The older F1 deposit is identified only as predecessor evidence. Independent manuscript reviews and owner approval precede submission.
