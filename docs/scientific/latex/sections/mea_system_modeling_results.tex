\subsection{Phase stability of the present model}\label{sec:phase-method}
% P:results-phase-opening
Stability is checked because ePC-SAFT ion-parameter choices can predict inconsistent liquid--liquid demixing in aqueous salt solutions \cite[Sect.~2.4]{Held2014}.
For loaded aqueous MEA, B\"ottinger et al.\ observe a homogeneous liquid in their measured subset \cite[Sect.~3]{Bottinger2008}, although that observation does not establish homogeneity over the entire numerical search domain.
With the searches of \cref{sec:methods-phase}, the present model gives no detected instability at 1908 liquid-grid states, 576 intermediate liquid states and 162 liquid--vapor states, with no unavailable or incomplete search in these sets.
Trial compositions taken from Gibbs decreases resolved for other MEA parameter sets also give no negative distance for the present model; 6121 such compositions are evaluated at every liquid--vapor state.
No stability penalty entered the fit of the present model.

\subsubsection{Absorption heat and heat capacity}\label{sec:results-heat}
% P:results-heat-01
The fitted integral heats and the Arcis comparison probe different temperature and pressure conditions (\cref{fig:heat}).
The calculated Arcis absorption is less exothermic than measured, with a larger discrepancy in the lower-pressure series.
The measurements themselves differ between those series at nearby loadings \cite[Table~5]{arcisEnthalpySolutionCarbon2011}; neither a pressure-only explanation nor a unique model contribution is isolated by this comparison.

% P:results-heat-kim
The Kim--Svendsen comparison deteriorates at \SI{393.15}{\kelvin}, where its finite-interval heat RMSE exceeds that of the Polley--Hedengren parameters on the common intervals (\cref{tab:same-data}).
Both calculations include the inconsistent printed intervals in this comparison; Table~\ref{supp-tab:kim-alternatives} reports alternative source exclusions.
Extrapolation of the equilibrium-constant correlations, approximated pressure histories and missing chemistry can affect the calculated heat, but this result does not identify a unique cause.
The discrepancy prevents interpreting the common enthalpy formulation as demonstrated high-temperature caloric accuracy.

\subsection{Cost of the parameter restrictions and conditional uncertainty}\label{sec:results-joint}
% P:results-joint-01
\Cref{tab:choice-cost} gives the measured cost of each cumulative restriction defined in \cref{sec:parameters}.
From the 16-parameter calibration to the present model, five fewer adjustable parameters leave the objective value and the fitted pressure and density deviations nearly unchanged and reduce the number of parameters at bounds, while the deviation of the pressures that are not fitted rises slightly.
The present model is the most restricted set whose fitted pressure and density deviations do not exceed those of the 16-parameter calibration; every further restriction in \cref{tab:choice-cost} has larger fitted and not-fitted pressure deviations than the present model.
The cumulative changes quantify the cost of the selected assumptions, not the isolated physical effect of each restriction.

\input{tables/choice_cost}

% P:results-parameter-interpretation
Only the \MEAH--\MEACOO interaction is at a bound for the present model; its lower-bound value doubles the unlike-pair dispersion energy under \cref{eq:dispersion-combining-rule}.
The other parameters have conditional standard errors listed beside their values in Table~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-fitted-parameters}.
The water--bicarbonate interaction and the opposing amine-ion size changes are weakly determined; their large conditional errors preclude identifying the fitted values as unique molecular properties.
Correlated covariance propagation describes local pressure sensitivity, while inward bound scans test short feasible changes with the other parameters refitted.
Neither calculation includes model discrepancy, uncertainty of the equilibrium-constant correlations, correlated errors within one experimental source or uncertainty of a phase boundary.

\subsection{Comparison with published models}\label{sec:results-literature}
% P:results-literature-01
\Cref{tab:same-data} compares the present model with the 2026 Polley--Hedengren parameters on identical observations \cite{polleyReactiveEPCSAFTModeling2026}.
The Polley--Hedengren equation-of-state calorics are calculated here from their published thermodynamic specification; their empirical column enthalpy correlations are not substituted.
The comparison shows gains in pressure and density agreement alongside remaining and worsened quantities, including high-temperature heat and the more concentrated pressure series.
Because the fitting roles differ, these values do not define an across-role model ranking.
Their published fitting account does not establish whether every pressure and species observation was fitted; the mixed-role labels in \cref{tab:same-data} preserve that uncertainty.

% P:results-literature-matched
On the 19 identical Ma'mun observations (\cref{tab:matched-pressure}) \cite{Mamun2005}, the present model has a larger pressure deviation than the 16-parameter calibration and than the PC-SAFT calculation of Baygi and Pahlavanzadeh, and a smaller one than the SAFT-HR calculation of Najafloo and Zarei.
Zhang fitted these data, whereas Baygi and Najafloo describe ternary calculations outside their fitting objectives \cite[Table~9]{Zhang2011}\cite[Table~5]{Baygi2015,Najafloo2018}.
Najafloo's detailed methods and conclusion differ in their fitting description, and equivalence of its pressure AAD formula is inferred rather than explicitly defined.
Published aggregate errors on other datasets are not converted into comparisons on common observations when their observation selection or property basis cannot be reconstructed.

\subsection{Scope and limitations}\label{sec:limits}
% P:results-limits
These results support the present model as a calibration, with calculations at fixed parameters, against data seen during development; no comparison establishes independent validation.
The present model also has measured weaknesses: on the 19 Ma'mun observations, the restrictions raise the pressure AARD from 19.43\,\% for the 16-parameter calibration to 33.47\,\% for the present model, and at \SI{393.15}{\kelvin} the Kim--Svendsen heat RMSE of the present model, \SI{31.77}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} \COtwo, exceeds the \SI{29.49}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} \COtwo of the Polley--Hedengren parameters.
The parameter uncertainty is conditional on this fit and therefore excludes model discrepancy and the uncertainty of the equilibrium-constant correlations.
The absence of an additional liquid rests on finite searches rather than a global proof.
The R1--R3 temperature range is Austgen's correlation range, not demonstrated measurement coverage, and R4, R5 and the unloaded dielectric law are extrapolated beyond their direct source constraints.
Pressures at other MEA concentrations, carbonate shares and the highest-temperature heats remain the weakest parts of the calibration, and missing pressure histories, source inconsistencies, observable approximations, omitted carrier gas and omitted oxazolidone further limit interpretation.
No absorber or stripper operating prediction is qualified by the property comparisons alone.
