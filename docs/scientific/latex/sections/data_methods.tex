\section{Data and parameter estimation}\label{sec:data-methods}
\subsection{Experimental data}\label{sec:data-roles}
% P:methods-01
Loading is $\alpha=n_{\COtwo,\mathrm{added}}/n_{\mathrm{amine,total}}$, and the amine content is the amine mass fraction of the unloaded solution, written in wt\%.
\emph{Fitted} means that an observation enters the objective of the reported fit; \emph{not fitted} means that it is outside that objective; \emph{comparison only} means a different quantity or different conditions from those the model was built for, never used to choose a fit.
Every observation had been examined during model development, and some observations that are not fitted informed a choice of parameters or of an initial parameter set; these are \emph{development} data.
No comparison in this paper is therefore an independent validation or a blind test.
\Cref{tab:calibration-data} lists, for each amine, the data in the objective, the data fitted beforehand and the development data.
The objectives contain 307 observations for MEA, 80 for DEA, 281 for MDEA and 156 for AMP; Tables~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-mea-comparison} and \ref{supp-tab:supplement-transfer-data} give every other set that is compared.

% P:methods-range
A calculation is attempted for every observation, including those outside the measured temperature range of an equilibrium constant (\cref{tab:reaction-equilibrium-constants}).
For DEA, MDEA and AMP such observations are marked in Table~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-transfer-data} and in the pressure figures, and Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-ranges} reports them separately.
For MEA the reacting calculations span 293.15--\SI{393.15}{\kelvin}, so R4 and R5 are extrapolated above \SI{323.15}{\kelvin}.
The calculated states of every amine extend above its R5 range, and no R4 range establishes the validity of a fitted R4 correlation.

% P:methods-02
Each observable follows its experimental definition: a sum reported by nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) is compared with the same calculated sum \cite{Bottinger2008}, and bubble calculations omit carrier gas, so calculated $y_{\COtwo}P$ is compared with a measured \COtwo partial pressure and calculated $P$ with a measured total pressure.
Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-corrections} gives the conventions of the individual sources and the excluded observations.

\subsection{Calculated quantities}\label{sec:heat-method}
% P:methods-heat-01
Pressures are reactive bubble pressures at the measured temperature and apparent composition, and species fractions are those of a single liquid at an assigned pressure.
The integral absorption heat per mole of added \COtwo is
\begin{equation}
 \Delta h=\frac{H_{\mathrm{final}}-H_{\mathrm{initial}}-\Delta n_{\COtwo}h_{\COtwo}^{\mathrm{feed}}}{\Delta n_{\COtwo}},
 \label{eq:addition-heat}
\end{equation}
with both equilibrium liquids and the pure-\COtwo feed at the same temperature and pressure; negative values denote exothermic absorption.
The semi-differential heats of Kim and Svendsen are calculated over each finite addition \cite{Kim2007}.

\subsection{Objective, deviations and conditional standard errors}\label{sec:parameters}
% P:methods-04
Estimation is sequential: the water parameters, the \COtwo--water interaction, fitted to the pressures of Kiepe et al.\ \cite{kiepeExperimentalDeterminationPrediction2002}, and the permittivity coefficients are determined first and remain fixed.
The 39 Kiepe pressures nevertheless remain in the MEA objective, to which they contribute 0.0007 without responding to any of its parameters (\cref{eq:fit-objective}, Table~\ref{supp-tab:objective-groups}).
Eleven MEA parameters are then estimated jointly (\cref{tab:species-parameters,tab:interaction-matrix}); one of them, the water--bicarbonate interaction, belongs to the shared species.
With all shared parameters fixed, four DEA, eight MDEA and eight AMP parameters are fitted (\cref{tab:transfer-fitted}); for AMP the temperature coefficient of the \ce{AMPH+}--water interaction, $b_{\mathrm{AMPH^+,w}}$, is fixed at \SI{-150}{\kelvin}, a value chosen from a scan of fixed values (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-amp-slope}).
Parameters were also set beforehand: the two permittivity coefficients of each amine, the DEA--water interaction and the five pure-AMP parameters (\cref{tab:transfer-species}, Table~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-transfer-parameters}); with these and the scanned slope, 7 quantities are adjusted for DEA, 10 for MDEA and 16 for AMP.
Each fit minimizes, by bounded least squares,
\begin{equation}
 C(\boldsymbol\theta)=\frac12\sum_jw_jr_j^2,\qquad
 r_j=\frac{y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}-y_j^{\mathrm{obs}}}{s_j}
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 r_j=\frac{\ln(y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}/y_j^{\mathrm{obs}})}{s_j},
 \label{eq:fit-objective}
\end{equation}
with the logarithmic form for pressures.
Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-objective} gives the scale $s_j$ and weight $w_j$ of each data set; several are assigned without a statistical derivation, so an objective value sets relative fitting priorities and does not compare accuracy between amines, and two objective values can be compared only on the same weights.
In the reported MEA objective each of the 50 MEA--water observations of Cai et al.\ \cite{caiBinaryIsobaricVaporLiquid1996} has unit weight, with scales of \SI{1}{\kelvin} and 0.01 mole fraction.
They contribute 60.8\,\% of the objective value, 150.76, but respond only to the MEA--water interaction and its temperature coefficient, so they determine those two parameters and leave the weighting among the loaded-solution data unchanged.
With the two Cai observables instead weighted so that each contributes half its mean squared residual, the reported parameters give an objective value of 62.797 and a refit gives 62.656; the fitted \COtwo partial pressures then deviate by 11.61 instead of 11.64\,\% (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-objective}).

% P:methods-starts
Each fit is started from two initial parameter sets, three for MDEA, and the converged solution with the lower objective value is reported; these are local solutions, not a global search.
The final 11-parameter MEA optimization used no stability penalty.
Its two starts descended from an earlier stability-penalized calibration, the 16-parameter calibration described next, and they converge to objective values of 150.763 and 150.767.

% P:methods-restrictions
For MEA the joint fit is also made with fewer restrictions.
The 16-parameter calibration shifts the R2 and R5 correlations, fits separate Born diameters for the two MEA ions and is fitted with the stability penalty of \cref{sec:methods-phase}.
Fixing R2 and R5 at their literature forms and tying the Born diameters to the segment diameters gives the 11-parameter \emph{MEA model} used throughout.

% P:methods-06
Agreement is reported as average absolute relative deviation (AARD), relative bias and root-mean-square error (RMSE),
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{AARD}=\frac{100\,\%}{N}\sum_j\left|\frac{y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}}{y_j^{\mathrm{obs}}}-1\right|,
 \qquad \operatorname{bias}=\frac{100\,\%}{N}\sum_j\left(\frac{y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}}{y_j^{\mathrm{obs}}}-1\right),
 \qquad \operatorname{RMSE}=\sqrt{\frac1N\sum_j(y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}-y_j^{\mathrm{obs}})^2},
 \label{eq:aard}
\end{equation}
where $N$ is the number of observations in the set, restricted for AARD and bias to nonzero observations.
For the parameters that are not on a bound, the local covariance is
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{cov}(\boldsymbol\theta_I)=s_C^2(J_I^{\mathsf T}J_I)^{-1},\qquad
 s_C^2=\frac{\sum_{j\in\mathcal R}w_jr_j^2}{N_{\mathrm{resp}}-|I|},
 \label{eq:conditional-covariance}
\end{equation}
where $J_I$ is the weighted residual Jacobian and $\mathcal R$ is the set of the $N_{\mathrm{resp}}$ observations in the objective whose calculated value depends on at least one fitted parameter; a parameter on a bound is held fixed and has no standard error.
An observation that responds to no fitted parameter carries no information on the parameters, so it is left out of both the sum and the count: the 39 Kiepe pressures of the MEA objective, which leaves 268 observations and 258 residual degrees of freedom, and three pure-water pressures of the MDEA objective, which leaves 278 and 272.
Compared with counting every observation, this raises the MEA standard errors by 7.3\,\% and those of MDEA by 0.5\,\%; all DEA and AMP observations respond.
These standard errors are conditional on the model form, the equilibrium-constant correlations, the fixed shared parameters, the scales, the weights and the local linearization.
They exclude model discrepancy and systematic differences between sources, and they do not define prediction intervals.

\subsection{Phase-stability search and stability penalty}\label{sec:methods-phase}
% P:methods-phase-01
Each fitted parameter set is searched for a phase split with the reacting tangent-plane criterion, which tests charge-neutral trial phases in reaction equilibrium \cite{Michelsen1982,Tsanas2022,Ascani2022,Perdomo2025}:
\begin{equation}
 D(\boldsymbol w)=g(T,P,\boldsymbol w)/(RT)-\boldsymbol w^{\mathsf T}B^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\lambda,
 \quad \sum_iw_i=1,\quad\sum_i z_iw_i=0,\quad w_i>0.
 \label{eq:tpd}
\end{equation}
Here $\boldsymbol w$ is the composition of a trial phase, restricted to the species that can form from the feed and, for a vapor, to \COtwo, water and the molecular amine; $B$ is the conservation matrix, whose rows are the independent conserved groups of the true species, so that $B\boldsymbol\nu_r=0$ for every reaction; and $\boldsymbol\lambda$ holds the Lagrange multipliers of the corresponding balances at the searched (parent) state, divided by $RT$, so that $B^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\lambda$ is the tangent plane of the parent.
A negative distance that is confirmed by a finite, mass-conserving transfer lowering the Gibbs energy by more than its numerical error estimate is a \emph{detected instability}, classed as toward a second liquid or toward a vapor from the density of the trial phase.
The parent states are grids of temperature, amine content and loading, mostly liquids at pressures above the calculated bubble pressure, and, for DEA, MDEA and AMP, also the measured states (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-phase}).
A search that does not finish is counted as not completed, never as stable.

% P:methods-penalty
For the 16-parameter calibration the searched states enter a penalized objective:
\begin{equation}
 r_k^{\mathrm{stab}}=\sqrt{w_{\mathrm{stab}}}\frac{\tau_k}{s_{\mathrm{stab}}},\qquad
 C_{\mathrm{pen}}=C+\frac12\sum_k(r_k^{\mathrm{stab}})^2,
 \quad w_{\mathrm{stab}}=1,
 \quad s_{\mathrm{stab}}\in\{10^{-2},3\times10^{-3}\}.
 \label{eq:stability-penalty}
\end{equation}
The two scales are applied in succession; $\tau_k$ is the most negative tangent-plane distance found at state $k$, or zero when the completed search finds none.

% P:methods-exceptions
The MEA and DEA models were fitted without the penalty; of the MEA fits, only the 16-parameter calibration used it.
For MDEA no penalty is used, and only the MDEA--water interaction acts on the \COtwo-free solution.
Its linear temperature law is written about \SI{423}{\kelvin}, the highest temperature tested, and its value there is bounded above at $-0.0671$, $10^{-4}$ below the value at which a liquid--liquid gap first appears in the \COtwo-free solution at \SI{1.466}{\mega\pascal}.
At \SI{1.466}{\mega\pascal} the fitted interaction lies below that onset value at each of the 33 tested temperatures from 290 to \SI{423}{\kelvin}.
The bound was fixed before fitting, from the onset at \SI{423}{\kelvin} and a margin of $10^{-4}$ (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-mdea-bound}).
The loaded MDEA states are not constrained, so a split found there would be reported as a limitation.
For AMP neither the penalty nor a bound of this kind is used: the \emph{AMP model} fits the other eight parameters with $b_{\mathrm{AMPH^+,w}}$ fixed at \SI{-150}{\kelvin}.
