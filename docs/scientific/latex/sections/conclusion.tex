\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-01
One explicit-ion ePC-SAFT formulation was fitted jointly to selected equilibrium, caloric and density observations for nominal 30 wt\% MEA, with property-dependent agreement.
Bicarbonate and carbonate fractions, the \SI{0.51}{\mega\pascal} Arcis heats, the unloaded heat capacity at \SI{318.15}{\kelvin} and loaded densities at 373.15--\SI{393.15}{\kelvin} remain discrepant.
It calculates the five target properties from one thermodynamic formulation, including loaded density without volume translation.
Pressure AARD is 11.86\,\% over 47 fitted observations at 313.00--\SI{333.15}{\kelvin} and 20.55\,\% over the 159-pressure comparison extending to \SI{393.15}{\kelvin}; pressures above \SI{333.15}{\kelvin} were not fitted.
The 159-pressure deviation lies within the range reported for related models on different data sets.
At 15 and 45 wt\% MEA the pressure deviations rise to 29.5 and 56.0\,\%, so the parameters do not transfer across solvent concentration.
The available comparisons do not yet establish its suitability across an absorber's operating range.

% P:conclusion-02
The phase comparison shows that similar property agreement can accompany additional calculated liquids.
Narrowing two parameter bounds removes the detected instabilities at the specified states, at a small fitting-cost increase and with three of the 47 fitted loaded densities outside $2s$.
This result covers 162 searched states at six temperatures between 313.15 and \SI{393.15}{\kelvin} and loadings $10^{-5}$--0.6.
The wider-bound fit has detected instabilities at 54 of these states, while the reported parameters have none; this finite search is not a global-stability certificate or physical validation.
Further work must address concentration dependence and bicarbonate and carbonate speciation, alongside the independent phase-behavior comparisons and broader caloric tests needed before process use.
