# ChemRxiv submission draft

## Title

Thermodynamics of CO₂-loaded aqueous monoethanolamine with ePC-SAFT. Part I: phase equilibria, speciation, absorption heat, density and phase stability

## Author and affiliation

Tanner W. Polley; ORCID 0009-0008-5957-4152; tpolley3@byu.edu. Department of Chemical Engineering, Brigham Young University, Provo, UT 84602, United States.

## Abstract

A thermodynamic description of reactive absorption must combine equilibrium, caloric and density properties of the loaded solvent. Part I jointly estimates sixteen ePC-SAFT parameters for aqueous monoethanolamine (MEA) using pressure, speciation, integral absorption heat, density, heat capacity and binary equilibrium data. For nominal 30 wt% MEA, pressure average absolute relative deviations are 11.79% for 47 fitted pressures and 20.27% over 159 fitted and non-fitted pressures at 313.00–393.15 K. For 94 of the 101 fitted species fractions the deviation is 7.11%; fitted integral-heat root-mean-square error is 1.84 kJ/mol CO₂, and fitted loaded-density deviation is 0.162% without volume translation. A fit without a phase-stability requirement gives similar property agreement but has detected liquid-phase instability at 54 of 162 searched states. Penalizing tangent-plane instability during fitting gives a parameter set with no detected instability at the 1903 penalized states or at 738 states checked outside the fit. Six parameters lie on bounds; conditional covariance gives pressure standard errors of 5.41–16.00% at the 47 fitted pressure states. Pressure deviations at 15 and 45 wt% are 33.89 and 44.53%, respectively; bicarbonate/carbonate and higher-temperature caloric discrepancies are reported with their source conflicts and model assumptions. These comparisons describe calibration and fixed-parameter predictions; the finite searches are numerical verification of phase behavior. A companion Part II examines transfer of the shared parameters to other amine classes.
