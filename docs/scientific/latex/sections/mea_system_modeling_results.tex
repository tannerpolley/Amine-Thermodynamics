\subsubsection{Parameters and their uncertainty}\label{sec:results-parameters}
% P:results-design
The MEA model has \res{mea-jointly-fitted-parameter-count-results} jointly fitted parameters: the MEA--water interaction and its temperature coefficient, the diameters and dispersion energies of \MEAH and \MEACOO, their interactions with water, one with a temperature coefficient, and the interactions of \MEAH with \MEACOO and with bicarbonate.
\Cref{tab:species-parameters,tab:interaction-matrix} list them, and \cref{tab:mea-data} gives the deviations from every fitted data set, beside those of the fit with the MEA-fitted shared set and of the model of Polley and Hedengren on identical observations \cite{polleyReactiveEPCSAFTModeling2026}.

% P:results-parameter-interpretation
\res{mea-active-interactions-count-results} interactions are on bounds.
The \MEAH--\MEACOO interaction is at $\res{mea-active-interaction-lower-value-results}$, which doubles the unlike-pair dispersion energy under \cref{eq:dispersion-combining-rule}, and the \MEAH--bicarbonate interaction is at $\res{mea-active-interaction-upper-value-results}$, which removes it; in fits with the R1--R3 references at the system pressure, holding either one slightly inside its bound raised the objective value by at most \res{mea-inward-bound-profile-maximum-objective-increase} (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-uncertainty}).
The MEA--water interaction has a small conditional standard error (\cref{tab:interaction-matrix}), and the calculated bubble temperatures follow both isobars of Cai et al.\ \cite{caiBinaryIsobaricVaporLiquid1996} with an RMSE of \SI{\res{mea-cai-bubble-temperature-rmse}}{\kelvin} (Fig.~\ref{supp-fig:mea-water}).
The diameters of the two MEA ions have a conditional correlation of $\res{mea-ion-segment-diameter-conditional-correlation}$ and are not separately identified, so the fitted values are not unique molecular properties.
Propagated to the \res{mea-reported-covariance-volatilization-pressure-count-results} fitted \COtwo partial pressures, the parameter covariance gives relative standard errors of \res{mea-reported-fitted-pressure-minimum-relative-se-results}--\res{mea-reported-fitted-pressure-maximum-relative-se-results}\,\%, of the same size as the deviations themselves.

\subsubsection{Pressure and speciation at 30 wt\%}\label{sec:results-pressure}
% P:results-pressure-01
At nominal 30 wt\% MEA the bubble curves follow the measured \COtwo partial pressures over the broad logarithmic pressure range, while differences between sources remain visible (\cref{fig:pressure}).
The \res{mea-legacy-pressure-count-results} fitted pressures, at 313--\SI{333}{\kelvin}, deviate by \res{mea-fitted-pressure-aard-mea-results}\,\%.
The higher-temperature pressures are calculated without further fitting, and the \res{mea-added-pressure-count-results} pressures of the same \res{mea-canonical-observation-source-count-results} sources that are not fitted deviate by \res{mea-added-same-sources-pressure-aard-results}\,\% (\cref{tab:mea-data}; Table~\ref{supp-tab:pressure-sources} gives the deviation for each source).

% P:results-species-01
The species curves show molecular MEA being replaced by protonated MEA and carbamate as loading increases, followed by increasing bicarbonate (\cref{fig:speciation}).
Agreement with the NMR amine sum does not independently establish the separate molecular and protonated fractions.
The carbonate share of dissolved carbon remains far below the \res{mea-carbonate-share-observation-count-results} observations of Jakobsen et al.\ \cite{Jakobsen2005}, with an AARD of \res{mea-carbonate-share-aard-results}\,\%: the geometric mean of the calculated-to-observed ratios is \res{mea-carbonate-share-geometric-mean-mea-results}, against \res{mea-historical-shared-carbonate-share-geometric-mean-results} with the MEA-fitted shared set (Fig.~\ref{supp-fig:jakobsen-carbonate-share}).
Jakobsen et al.\ obtain that share from the chemical shift of a merged \ce{^{13}C} NMR signal and state that their carbonate is most likely overpredicted, which limits the accuracy of these observations.
Changes to the Born diameters of the two anions, to the permittivity and to the solvation factors, tested with the MEA-fitted shared set, do not remove the deficit (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-carbonate}).
Its cause is unresolved, and the fitted pressures do not determine this minor-species partition.

\subsubsection{Absorption heat, heat capacity and density}\label{sec:results-heat}
% P:results-heat-01
The integral heats of Vinjarapu et al.\ at \SI{313.15}{\kelvin} are fitted \cite{HemaBhavya2025}; those of Arcis et al.\ at \SI{322.5}{\kelvin} are not, although they were used to select the parameters \cite{arcisEnthalpySolutionCarbon2011}.
The calculated absorption at the Arcis conditions is less exothermic than measured, with the larger discrepancy in the lower-pressure series (\cref{fig:heat}).
The measurements themselves differ between the two series at nearby loadings, so this comparison isolates neither a pressure effect nor a model contribution.

\begin{figure}[pos=!htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.75\linewidth]{heat-of-absorption.pdf}
\caption{Integral absorption heat per mole of added \COtwo in nominal 30 wt\% MEA against loading: fitted Vinjarapu observations and Arcis observations, which are not fitted \cite{HemaBhavya2025,arcisEnthalpySolutionCarbon2011} (symbols), and the MEA model at the labeled temperature and pressure (lines). At \SI{322.5}{\kelvin}, open and filled squares are \SI{0.51}{\mega\pascal} and \SI{1.03}{\mega\pascal}.}
\label{fig:heat}
\end{figure}

% P:results-heat-kim
The semi-differential heats of Kim and Svendsen deviate most at \SI{393.15}{\kelvin}, with an RMSE of \SI{\res{mea-kim-svendsen-hot-interval-rmse-results}}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} of \COtwo over all \res{mea-kim-svendsen-hot-interval-count-results} intervals, including the \res{mea-kim-hot-flagged-interval-count-results} flagged for source problems that Table~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-temperature-bands} reports separately.
Extrapolated equilibrium constants, pressure histories that had to be approximated and chemistry missing from the model may all contribute, so caloric accuracy at high temperature is not demonstrated.
Heat capacities outside the fitted states \cite{weilandHeatCapacityAqueous1997,concepcionHeatCapacitiesDifferent2023} deviate by about \res{mea-heat-capacity-outside-weiland-concepcion-maximum-aard}\,\% or less, \res{mea-weiland-outside-heat-capacity-aard}\,\% for the \res{mea-weiland-outside-heat-capacity-count} of Weiland et al.\ (Table~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-mea-comparison}).

% P:results-density-01
Without volume translation, the model describes the fitted loaded densities of Amundsen et al.\ and Hartono et al.\ more closely than the full ranges of those sources and the densities of Han et al., which are not fitted (Fig.~\ref{supp-fig:density-curves}) \cite{Amundsen2009,Hartono2014,hanDensityWater12012}.

\subsubsection{Other MEA concentrations}\label{sec:results-concentration}
% P:results-transfer
At 15 and 45 wt\% MEA, where no pressure is fitted and no concentration-specific parameter is estimated, the pressures of Aronu et al.\ deviate by \res{mea-aronu-lower-concentration-pressure-aard-results} and \res{mea-aronu-higher-concentration-pressure-aard-results}\,\%, against \res{mea-aronu-nominal-pressure-aard-results}\,\% for their pressures at 30 wt\%, which are not fitted either (\cref{tab:mea-data}, Fig.~\ref{supp-fig:concentration-pressure}).
Aronu et al.\ fitted an extended UNIQUAC model to their own data at 15--60 wt\% and report 24.3\,\% for all their \COtwo partial pressures together \cite{Aronu2011}, so the two are not compared on identical observations.
The concentration dependence of the pressure is a weakness of the calibration.

\subsubsection{Effect of the restrictions and of the salt-fitted interactions}\label{sec:results-restrictions}
% P:results-joint-01
Two steps lead from the \res{mea-unrestricted-results-coordinate-count}-parameter calibration to the MEA model; the sequence was computed with the R1--R3 references at the system pressure (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-restrictions}).
The first fixes R2 and R5 at their literature forms and ties the Born diameters, which gives the \res{mea-third-restriction-results-coordinate-count}-parameter fit with the MEA-fitted shared set.
It leaves the objective value and the fitted pressure and density deviations nearly unchanged and lowers the number of parameters on bounds from \res{mea-restriction-unrestricted-active-bound-count} to \res{mea-restriction-tied-born-active-bound-count}.
The second fixes the two water--anion interactions from the salt data.
On the same \res{mea-objective-observations-electrostatics} observations it raises the objective value from \res{mea-restriction-mea-shared-set-objective} to \res{mea-restriction-salt-set-tracking-objective}, and the \res{mea-added-pressure-count-restriction-comparison} pressures not fitted deviate by \res{mea-restriction-salt-set-tracking-unfitted-aard} instead of \res{mea-restriction-mea-shared-set-not-fitted-pressure-aard}\,\%.

% P:results-joint-02
Both steps cost accuracy at high temperature.
On the \res{mea-mamun-assessment-pressure-count-mea-results} \COtwo partial pressures of Ma'mun et al.\ \cite{Mamun2005} near \SI{393}{\kelvin}, which no fit includes, the deviation is \res{mea-mamun-16-coordinate-reference-comparison-aard}\,\% for the \res{mea-unrestricted-mamun-comparison-coordinate-count}-parameter calibration, \res{mea-mamun-mea-shared-reference-comparison-aard}\,\% with the MEA-fitted shared set and \res{mea-mamun-restricted-salt-shared-reference-comparison-aard}\,\% for the restricted model in that sequence; the MEA model with the \SI{100}{\kilo\pascal} references deviates by \res{mea-mamun-pressure-aard-mea-results}\,\% (\cref{tab:published-models}).
In return the restricted model keeps the literature equilibrium constants that the other amines share, needs no stability penalty, and no longer takes a shared interaction from MEA data that do not determine it.
With the water--bicarbonate interaction fixed, the conditional standard errors of the carbamate--water and \MEAH--water interactions fall from \res{mea-shared-carbamate-water-conditional-se-before-fixing} and \res{mea-shared-protonated-mea-water-conditional-se-before-fixing} to \res{mea-salt-shared-carbamate-water-conditional-se-results} and \res{mea-salt-shared-protonated-mea-water-conditional-se-results}.

% P:results-expanded
Fitting the hot pressures does not remove this cost.
A sensitivity fit, made with the R1--R3 references at the system pressure, adds the \res{mea-added-pressure-count-weighting-sensitivity} pressures not fitted here to the MEA objective at full weight; it lowers the deviation of the \res{mea-expanded-weight-hot-wagner-pressure-count-results} hot pressures of Wagner et al.\ from \res{mea-tracking-legacy-hot-wagner-pressure-aard} to \res{mea-expanded-weight-hot-wagner-pressure-aard-results}\,\% but raises that of the \res{mea-legacy-pressure-count-weighting-sensitivity} pressures fitted here from \res{mea-tracking-legacy-pressure-aard-weighting-context} to \res{mea-expanded-weight-legacy-pressure-aard-results}\,\% (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-restrictions}).
The sources disagree at these temperatures: at \SI{393}{\kelvin} and 30 wt\% MEA, Jou et al.\ report \SI{46.8}{\kilo\pascal} at a loading of 0.349 and Ma'mun et al.\ \SI{83.6}{\kilo\pascal} at 0.350.
A different weighting therefore moves the error from one campaign to another, and the MEA model remains the fit to the \res{mea-legacy-pressure-count-weighting-interpretation} pressures; with the \SI{100}{\kilo\pascal} references it deviates by \res{mea-selected-hot-wagner-pressure-aard}\,\% from the \res{mea-selected-hot-wagner-pressure-row-count} Wagner pressures.
The MEA model does not reproduce the absorption heats at \SI{393}{\kelvin}: for the finite additions of Kim et al.\ \cite{Kim2014}, it gives \res{mea-kim-hot-heat-predicted-min-results}--\SI{\res{mea-kim-hot-heat-predicted-max-results}}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} against \res{mea-kim-hot-heat-measured-min-results}--\SI{\res{mea-kim-hot-heat-measured-max-results}}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} measured (Table~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-mea-heat-sensitivity}).
