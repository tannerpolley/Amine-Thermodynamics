\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-base
On one base set without amine data, the parameters of water, \COtwo and the inorganic ions, the inorganic equilibrium constants and the water--bicarbonate and water--carbonate interactions from sodium-salt osmotic coefficients, ePC-SAFT with explicit ions describes the \COtwo solubility in aqueous MEA, DEA, MDEA and AMP, each amine through a fit of its own species: the fitted loaded-solution pressures deviate by \res{mea-fitted-pressure-aard-conclusion}\,\% for MEA at 30 wt\%, \res{dea-fitted-pressure-aard-conclusion}\,\% for DEA, \res{mdea-fitted-pressure-aard-conclusion}\,\% for MDEA and \res{amp-fitted-pressure-aard-conclusion}\,\% for AMP, on \res{mea-legacy-pressure-count-conclusion}, \res{dea-fitted-partial-pressure-count-conclusion}, \res{mdea-fitted-kuranov-pressure-count-conclusion} and \res{amp-reported-loaded-pressure-count-conclusion} observations.
The same base thus gives the \COtwo pressures of a primary, a secondary, a tertiary and a hindered amine from one Helmholtz energy, with DEA and AMP each holding two parameters at their MEA values.

% P:conclusion-mea
The MEA model, with \res{mea-jointly-fitted-parameter-count-conclusion} jointly fitted parameters and literature equilibrium constants, also deviates by about \res{mea-fitted-heat-cp-density-maximum-aard-rounded}\,\% or less from its fitted heats, heat capacities and densities.
\Cref{sec:limits} states, property by property, the ranges and deviations within which the four models supply thermodynamic properties for absorber calculations.

% P:conclusion-phase
The phase-stability searches find no confirmed second liquid with any of the four reported models.
For DEA, AMP and MDEA one change in the fitted data decides whether a second liquid is predicted.
Fitted without the pressures of Lawson and Garst, the DEA model predicts a second liquid; adding the pressures of Tong et al.\ and those of Hartono et al.\ above \SI{353}{\kelvin} to an AMP calibration with other water--anion and carbamate inputs and only the loaded Hartono et al.\ pressures to \SI{353}{\kelvin} removes every detected instability at the states searched; and weighting the \COtwo-free MDEA pressures at the scale of the loaded pressures removes the calculated liquid--liquid gap in the \COtwo-free solution.
Fits to single-liquid data can therefore predict a second liquid, and a phase-stability search tests a fitted electrolyte model in a way that its deviations from the fitted data do not.
