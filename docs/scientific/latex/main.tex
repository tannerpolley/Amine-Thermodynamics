\begin{abstract}
% P:abstract-01
A thermodynamic description of reactive absorption must combine equilibrium, caloric and density properties of the loaded solvent.
Part I jointly estimates sixteen ePC-SAFT parameters for aqueous monoethanolamine (MEA) using pressure, speciation, integral absorption heat, density, heat capacity and binary equilibrium data.
For nominal 30 wt\% MEA, pressure average absolute relative deviations are 11.79\,\% for 47 fitted pressures and 20.27\,\% over 159 fitted and non-fitted pressures at 313.00--\SI{393.15}{\kelvin}.
For 94 of the 101 fitted species fractions the deviation is 7.11\,\%; fitted integral-heat root-mean-square error is \SI{1.84}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} \ce{CO2}, and fitted loaded-density deviation is 0.162\,\% without volume translation.
A fit without a phase-stability requirement gives similar property agreement but has detected liquid-phase instability at 54 of 162 searched states.
Penalizing tangent-plane instability during fitting gives a parameter set with no detected instability at the 1903 penalized states or at 738 states checked outside the fit.
Six parameters lie on bounds; conditional covariance gives pressure standard errors of 5.41--16.00\,\% at the 47 fitted pressure states.
Pressure deviations at 15 and 45 wt\% are 33.89 and 44.53\,\%, respectively; bicarbonate/carbonate and higher-temperature caloric discrepancies are reported with their source conflicts and model assumptions.
These comparisons describe calibration and fixed-parameter predictions; the finite searches are numerical verification of phase behavior.
A companion Part II examines transfer of the shared parameters to other amine classes.


\end{abstract}
