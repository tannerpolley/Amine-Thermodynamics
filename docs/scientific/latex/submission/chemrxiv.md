# ChemRxiv submission draft

## Title

Modeling CO₂ solubility in aqueous alkanolamine solutions with ePC-SAFT

## Author and affiliation

Tanner W. Polley; ORCID 0009-0008-5957-4152; tpolley3@byu.edu. Department of Chemical Engineering, Brigham Young University, Provo, UT 84602, United States.

## Abstract

Equation-of-state models of carbon dioxide (CO₂) solubility in aqueous alkanolamines are calibrated amine by amine and judged by their deviations from measured pressures, but a fit to single-liquid data does not require the calculated liquid to be stable. An electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) model with explicit ions describes CO₂ solubility in four aqueous amines calibrated on one base set without amine data: water, CO₂, the inorganic ions and reactions, and water–anion interactions fixed from sodium-salt osmotic coefficients. Monoethanolamine (MEA) is fitted jointly to pressures, species fractions, absorption heats, heat capacities and densities. Diethanolamine (DEA), methyldiethanolamine (MDEA) and 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol (AMP) are each fitted through their own species, DEA and AMP each holding two parameters at their MEA values. The fitted CO₂ partial pressures of MEA at 30 wt% and of DEA deviate by 11.3 and 14.7%, and the fitted total pressures of MDEA and AMP by 4.2 and 10.8%. Reactive phase-stability searches at measured and gridded states find no confirmed second liquid in the four models. That outcome depends on the fitted data. A DEA fit without the Lawson–Garst pressures predicts one. Weighting the CO₂-free MDEA pressures like the loaded ones removes a calculated liquid–liquid gap from that solution. An AMP calibration with other water–anion and carbamate inputs and no loaded pressures above 353 K predicts a second liquid, and adding the remaining loaded pressures alone removes it. A phase-stability search thus tests a fitted electrolyte model beyond its deviations from the fitted data.

## Keywords

## Data availability

The electronic data comprise processed observations, parameter values, numerical results and the analysis and figure code under the MIT license; third-party measurements remain under the terms of their sources. They will be deposited in Zenodo, and the DOI will be assigned at submission. The reported values were calculated with a development build of the epcsaft package after release 0.1.0, whose source is at https://github.com/tannerpolley/epcsaft. The deposit contains that wheel, the retained inputs, the retained calculation records and the commands that regenerate every table and figure of the article and the supplement from them. The retained inputs are the processed observations with their source-row transformations, the caloric inputs and the full parameter vectors of the four models.
