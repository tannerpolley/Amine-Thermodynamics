\section{Born-diameter refit at the historical 160-target objective}\label{sec:s-diameter}
% P:supplement-01
Uniform scaling of all Born diameters at fixed interactions changed the historical SSM+DS cost more than the Original Born cost, which raised the question of whether the inherited diameters explain the SSM+DS penalty on the 160-target objective.
The Born diameters of \ce{MEAH+}, \ce{MEACOO-} and \ce{HCO3-} were changed from 3.533, 3.541 and \SI{3.000}{\angstrom} to 5.000, 2.041 and \SI{3.302}{\angstrom}, within a search envelope of 2--\SI{5}{\angstrom} chosen from the 2.784--\SI{4.985}{\angstrom} Born diameters reported for other non-proton ions \cite[Tables~2--3]{Figiel2025}, not from measured diameters of the \ce{MEA}-derived ions, and the five interaction coordinates were then refitted.
That refit converged after five optimizer updates and lowered the historical SSM+DS cost from 47.625767 to 43.950877, a reduction of 3.674890.
The test set in advance was a reduction of at least 3.71, half the 7.42 cost gap to Original Born, so the result is inconclusive: the diameters affect the cost, but this change does not account for the gap.
Table~\ref{tab:s-diameter-refit} lists the diameters and group costs.
The refitted coordinates are \(k_{\ce{MEACOO-},\ce{H2O}}=0.0351\), \(k_{\ce{MEAH+},\ce{H2O}}=-0.2533\) at \SI{313.15}{\kelvin}, \(k_{\ce{HCO3-},\ce{H2O}}=+0.5\) at its upper bound, \(k_{\ce{MEAH+},\ce{MEACOO-}}=-0.5759\) and \(b=\SI{119.96}{\kelvin}\).

\section{Experimental data inventory for near-30 wt\% aqueous MEA}\label{sec:s-data-inventory}
% P:supplement-02
To place the observations of the main text among published measurements, we inventoried experimental studies of \ce{CO2} in aqueous \ce{MEA} near 30 wt\%: vapor--liquid equilibrium, liquid speciation, density, heat of absorption, heat capacity, pH and reaction constants.
The search is not exhaustive.
The inventory holds 165 entries, of which 88 were identified from bibliographic metadata alone; their coverage is unverified, and they are not tabulated.
\Cref{tab:s4-data-inventory} lists the 34 publications whose near-30 wt\% coverage was confirmed from a primary passage, table or database record, with the concentration basis, conditions and observation counts each source reports.
Several entries reuse the same experimental campaign and are not independent measurements.
Extracting values from a source does not admit them to the fit; the fitted and assessment roles of the main text are those of its observation-role table.
Dilute-solution reaction-constant measurements are outside the scope of \Cref{tab:s4-data-inventory}.
