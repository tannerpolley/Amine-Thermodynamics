% P:nomenclature-conventions
Subscripts $i,j$ identify species, $r$ reactions, $w$ water and $k$ searched states; superscripts res, ig, hc, disp, assoc, DH and Born identify residual, ideal-gas, hard-chain, dispersion, association and electrostatic terms.
Superscripts calc and obs mean calculated and observed; pure and $\infty,w$ mean pure solvent and infinite dilution in water; $m^\circ$ is the standard molality and $P^\circ$ the ideal-gas standard pressure.

% P:nomenclature-acronyms
AARD: average absolute relative deviation; RMSE: root-mean-square error; MEA: monoethanolamine; DEA: diethanolamine; MDEA: methyldiethanolamine; AMP: 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol; NMR: nuclear magnetic resonance; eNRTL: electrolyte non-random two-liquid; PC-SAFT: perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory; ePC-SAFT: electrolyte PC-SAFT; SAFT-HR: Huang--Radosz statistical associating fluid theory.
