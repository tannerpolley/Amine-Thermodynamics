\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-mea
An ePC-SAFT model of \COtwo-loaded aqueous MEA with 11 jointly fitted parameters and literature equilibrium constants deviates by 11.34\,\% from its 47 fitted \COtwo partial pressures at 30 wt\% and by about 3\,\% or less from its fitted heats, heat capacities and densities.
Its pressures at 15 and 45 wt\% MEA and at stripper temperatures, its carbonate shares and its absorption heats at high temperature remain inaccurate, and the cause of the carbonate deficit is unresolved.

% P:conclusion-transfer
With the water, \COtwo and inorganic-ion parameters of that model held fixed, DEA, MDEA and AMP could each be fitted: the fitted loaded-solution pressures deviate by 42.75\,\% for DEA, 4.39\,\% for MDEA and 10.82\,\% for AMP, on 90, 66 and 105 observations.
Every reaction reference is at \SI{100}{\kilo\pascal}, so equilibrium, density and enthalpy follow from one Gibbs energy; at one fitted state of each amine the finite-difference pressure derivative of that Gibbs energy agrees with the equation-of-state volume within the stated numerical error, which is 10.5\,\% for MEA and 8.9\,\% for DEA.
Each fit is a local solution: for MEA, DEA and AMP three distinct starts reach the same objective, while the MDEA fit is provisional because only one of 64 drawn starts could be evaluated and the reported endpoint comes from the inherited start, and no endpoint meets the strict projected-gradient criterion.
\emph{Transfer} means no more than this.
DEA and AMP each fit a carbamate-hydrolysis correlation, MDEA needed its loaded-solution pressures weighted against its many density and heat data, and for DEA no tested water--DEA interaction represents the binary excess enthalpies and bubble pressures together.

% P:conclusion-shared
The shared water--bicarbonate and water--carbonate interactions are fixed from sodium-salt osmotic coefficients, so that no amine data determine them.
They are adopted for that reason, not because the amine data are fitted better.

% P:conclusion-phase
Phase-stability searches of the fitted models detect no instability for MEA or at the 666 AMP states scaled to the bubble pressure.
For DEA they confirm liquid--liquid splits at four states of measured density: the single-liquid calculation matches those densities while the model's own phase calculation favors two liquids, contrary to treating the samples as one liquid.
For MDEA a bound keeps the \COtwo-free solution free of a calculated liquid--liquid gap only up to \SI{423}{\kelvin}, and no split is confirmed for the provisional MDEA fit.
A first AMP fit, whose loaded-solution pressures ended at \SI{353}{\kelvin}, gave calculated liquid--liquid splits at 363--\SI{393}{\kelvin}; the refit that added 18 pressures of Hartono et al.\ at 373--\SI{393}{\kelvin} and 40 of Tong et al.\ at 313--\SI{393}{\kelvin} confirms none in the 666-state screen; at the six measured-pressure controls, where the first fit confirms six splits, it confirms none, and four of its descents point toward a vapor below its bubble pressure.
A phase-stability search is therefore a useful check on where a fit has been extrapolated or fails.
The four models are calibrations against data examined during development; \cref{sec:limits} states what they do not establish.
