\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-01
One explicit-ion ePC-SAFT formulation represents the selected equilibrium, caloric and density observations for nominal 30 wt\% MEA.
It calculates the five target properties from one thermodynamic formulation, including loaded density without volume translation.
Pressure AARD is 11.86\,\% over 47 fitted observations and 20.55\,\% over the 159-pressure comparison.
Its pressure deviations are within the range reported for related models on different data sets.
At 15 and 45 wt\% MEA the pressure deviations rise to 29.5 and 56.0\,\%, so the parameters do not transfer across solvent concentration.
The separately fitted parameters match IAPWS-95 saturation properties within 0.2\,\% and heat capacity within 1.5\,\%, the Kiepe \COtwo--water pressures with 2.67\,\% AARD but up to 11.3\,\% low at \SI{393.2}{\kelvin}, and unloaded permittivities with RMSE 0.30.
The available comparisons do not yet establish its suitability across an absorber's operating range.

% P:conclusion-02
The phase comparison shows that similar property agreement can accompany additional calculated liquids.
Narrowing two parameter bounds removes the detected instabilities at the specified states, at a small fitting-cost increase and with three fitted density limits missed.
This result covers 162 searched states at six temperatures between 313.15 and \SI{393.15}{\kelvin} and loadings $10^{-5}$--0.6.
The comparison fit has detected instabilities at 54 of these states, while the reported parameters have none; this finite search is not a global-stability certificate or physical validation.
Independent phase-behavior comparisons and caloric tests over a broader temperature range remain necessary before process use.
