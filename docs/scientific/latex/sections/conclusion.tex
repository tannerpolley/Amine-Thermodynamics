\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-01
One explicit-ion ePC-SAFT formulation with carbamate chemistry represents the selected equilibrium, caloric and density observations for nominal 30 wt\% MEA.
It provides the five target properties from one thermodynamic formulation, with loaded density calculated without volume translation.
Pressure AARD is 11.86\,\% over 47 fitted observations and 20.55\,\% over the 159-pressure comparison; these values lie within the published range for related models using different data sets and fitting roles.
The reported parameters support comparisons within the measured domain, while the pressure, species, heat and density discrepancies identify where further assessment is needed.
They do not support transfer across solvent concentrations or establish suitability across an absorber's operating range.

% P:conclusion-02
The phase comparison shows that similar property agreement can accompany additional calculated liquids.
Narrowing two parameter bounds removes the detected instabilities at the specified states, at a small fitting-cost increase and with three fitted density limits missed.
This result is confined to the finite search at 313.15--\SI{333.15}{\kelvin} and loading $10^{-5}$--0.5; it does not establish stability at the warmer property states.
Independent phase-behavior comparisons and caloric tests over a broader temperature range remain necessary before process use.
