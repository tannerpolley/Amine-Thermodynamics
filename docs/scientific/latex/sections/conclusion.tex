\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-01
A nine-species ePC-SAFT model with explicit carbamate chemistry jointly represents several equilibrium, caloric and volumetric properties of 30 wt\% aqueous MEA through 16 fitted parameters and 307 quantities.
The leading limitation is that its finite phase test covers only 313.15--333.15 K and loading $10^{-5}$--0.5, below the three density failures at 353.15 K.
The restricted-bound fit gives pressure/species AARDs of 11.86/7.10\,\% and fitted integral-heat RMSE of 1.83 kJ/mol \COtwo.
Densities are calculated without volume translation; 44 of 47 fitted loaded densities lie within $2s$.

% P:conclusion-02
Restricting the protonated-amine size and water-interaction bounds removes all instabilities detected for the original-bound fit at the specified states, while increasing total cost by 0.45\,\% and leaving three densities 0.40--0.53\,\% low.
This is a comparison of local solutions under chosen bounds, rather than a minimum density penalty or a global optimum.
The fitted R5 law departs from Bates--Pinching, and heat temperature dependence remains untested beyond the fitted and compared states.
Carbonate share, titration bicarbonate, unloaded heat capacity and pressures at 45 wt\% MEA limit further use.
The evidence supports calibration and finite numerical phase searches; independent phase-behavior and absorber/stripper validation remain outstanding.
