\begin{abstract}
% P:abstract-01
The equilibrium \COtwo partial pressure over loaded aqueous monoethanolamine (\MEA) depends on liquid speciation, and titration and NMR measurements disagree on bicarbonate.
Electrolyte PC-SAFT (ePC-SAFT) with explicit ions has been applied to aqueous methyldiethanolamine, which forms no carbamate, and recent Born-term modifications were parameterized on salt solutions.
We fit five interaction coordinates of a nine-species ePC-SAFT model with explicit carbamate chemistry to 48 \COtwo partial pressures (40 and \SI{60}{\degreeCelsius}) and 94 species targets (20--\SI{60}{\degreeCelsius}) in 30 wt\% \MEA, with average absolute relative deviations (AARD) of 13.53\,\% and 8.28\,\%.
With identical coordinates and data, the ranking of the solvation-shell and dielectric-saturation (SSM+DS) and Original Born terms reverses with the role of 18 titration bicarbonate observations: fitting them favors Original Born; leaving them report-only favors SSM+DS by 4.6\,\% in weighted cost.
At fixed interactions, scans of solvation factors, ion-region permittivity and Born diameters raise the 142-target cost from 32.99 to as much as \pending{SCAN_MAX_C142}; a refit without the Born term reaches \pending{BORNOFF_C142}.
Electrolyte-term comparisons must therefore state the speciation measurement method and its fitting role.
Outside the present fit, the pressure AARD is 9.24\,\% for 11 Jou 1995 targets at \SI{80}{\degreeCelsius}, whose data informed fixed inputs, 26.44\,\% for 57 rows at 100--\SI{120}{\degreeCelsius}, and 75.95 and 46.15\,\% for 33 and 37 rows at 15 and 45 wt\% \MEA.
The bicarbonate--water coefficient lies at its bound, density and heat capacity are not qualified, and no physical validation is claimed.
\end{abstract}
