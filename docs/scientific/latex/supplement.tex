\section{Complete model inputs and fixed-parameter provenance}\label{sec:s-parameters}
% P:supplement-18
Neutral MEA parameters are the 2B values of Baygi and Pahlavanzadeh, except the association volume described below \cite[Table~2]{Baygi2015}.
\COtwo segment count, diameter and dispersion energy are from Gross and Sadowski \cite[Table~1]{Gross2001}.
Bicarbonate, carbonate and hydroxide sizes and dispersion energies follow Uyan et al.; their hydrogen-ion parameters are transferred here to hydronium \cite[Table~4]{Uyan2015}.
Ion segment count one and the diameter ties are modeling assumptions.
Fixed Born diameters for bicarbonate, carbonate, hydronium and hydroxide, and the fixed water interactions with carbonate, hydronium and hydroxide, are model inputs without independent MEA-specific parameter measurements.
Their exact values are tabulated; they are not attributed to the cited size/dispersion source.
Water's solvation factor and ion-region permittivity follow Figiel et al.; unit solvation factors for the other components are assumptions \cite[Table~2, Eqs.~5--10]{Figiel2025}.

% P:supplement-association-01
The \COtwo--water cross-association energy is \SI{878.325}{\kelvin}, half the fitted water self-association energy, with the same association volume as water.
The topology follows Schick et al.\ and Pabsch et al.\ \cite[Table~1, Sect.~2.4.2]{Schick2023}\cite{Pabsch2020}.
The association strength here carries $\sigma_{ij}^3$, whereas Baygi and Pahlavanzadeh report their MEA association volume, 0.037470, for a $d_{ij}^3$ strength \cite[Table~2]{Baygi2015}.
The value used here, 0.036305439, is the constant fitted by least squares, with the ePC-SAFT library's fitter, to the vapor pressures and saturated-liquid densities of their own model at 21 temperatures from 293.15 to \SI{393.15}{\kelvin}.

% P:supplement-19
The main text reports the fits of the five water parameters to IAPWS-95 \cite{internationalassociationforthepropertiesofwaterandsteamRevisedReleaseIAPWS2018}, the \COtwo--water interaction to Kiepe pressures \cite{kiepeExperimentalDeterminationPrediction2002} and the \MEA dielectric coefficients to permittivities.
In the water fit, the two exponential terms in $\sigma_w(T)=\sigma_0+a_1\exp(b_1T)+a_2\exp(b_2T)$, with $a_1=10.11$, $b_1=-0.01775$, $a_2=-1.417$ and $b_2=-0.01146$, were fixed while $\sigma_0$ was fitted; diameters are in angstrom and temperature in kelvin.
The objective scales were relative 0.0004885 for saturated density, 0.006187 for saturation pressure and 0.03 for heat capacity, with heat-capacity weight 0.1 relative to the other property groups.

% P:supplement-20
In the permittivity fit, water coefficients, 0.3777 L/mol and 1403 K, follow Yang et al.; the \COtwo dielectric volume $10^{-8}$ L/mol and temperature 1403 K are assumptions \cite[Table~6]{Yang2024}.
At 298.15 K the quadratic composition dependence gives two coefficient pairs with identical permittivities at any density: $(d_V,d_T)=(0.24645\ \mathrm{L/mol},2436.44\ \mathrm{K})$ or $(0.73099\ \mathrm{L/mol},1019.08\ \mathrm{K})$.
The larger-volume solution is used here; the permittivity data alone do not distinguish these solutions, so that selection is a model assumption.
Temperature and loaded-solution extrapolation of this fit remain untested by these permittivity data.

\section{Objective and data roles}\label{sec:s-objective}
% P:supplement-objective-00
Eight disjoint groups contain 307 fitted quantities.
The shared pressure--species--heat group contains 47 pressures, 94 species fractions and eight integral heats; seven additional species observations and six Jakobsen ratios enter separately.
The remaining groups are 47 loaded densities, five carbon-free densities, 50 Cai binary quantities, 39 Kiepe pressures and four heat capacities.

% P:supplement-objective-01
The bounded least-squares problem minimizes
\begin{equation}
 C(\boldsymbol\theta)=\frac12\sum_jw_jr_j^2,\qquad
 r_j=\begin{cases}(y_{c,j}-y_{o,j})/s_j,&\text{difference},\\[4pt]
 \ln(y_{c,j}/y_{o,j})/s_j,&\text{log ratio}.
 \end{cases}\label{eq:fit-residuals}
\end{equation}
Here $y_{c,j}$ and $y_{o,j}$ denote calculated and observed values.
Loaded densities have weight $1/47$, so their group contributes a mean scaled squared error rather than a sum growing with its observation count.
They contribute 0.565 of the total cost 148.9, or 0.38\,\%; the separate $2s$ check prevents their small cost share from hiding individual failures.
Carbon-free densities have scale \SI{0.5}{\kilogram\per\cubic\metre} and weight 10; Cai temperature and vapor-fraction scales are \SI{1}{\kelvin} and 0.01 with unit weight, and Kiepe \COtwo--water pressures have weight $1/39$ \cite{kiepeExperimentalDeterminationPrediction2002}.
These weights set fitting priorities; the heat scales are not measured standard uncertainties.
\Cref{tab:objective-groups} specifies every group.

\section{Conditional parameter errors and correlations}\label{sec:s-uncertainty}
% P:supplement-covariance-01
For the ten parameters free of active bounds, the weighted residual Jacobian $J_I$ gives
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{cov}(\boldsymbol\theta_I)=s_C^2(J_I^{\mathsf T}J_I)^{-1},\qquad s_C^2=\frac{2C}{307-10}.
 \label{eq:conditional-covariance}
\end{equation}
The Jacobian has rank ten, 297 degrees of freedom and variance factor 1.003.
The six parameters at bounds have no symmetric standard error.
These local errors condition on the weights, fixed inputs, bounds and linearization; dependence between measurements from the same experiment is not propagated.
\Cref{tab:supplement-correlations} gives the parameter order and correlations.

% P:supplement-covariance-02
Six of the 16 parameters are at bounds: \MEAH--water and \MEAH--\MEACOO interactions, \MEAH segment diameter, both fitted Born diameters and total R5 enthalpy shift.
The free bicarbonate--water coefficient is $0.727$ with conditional standard error $1.27$; the R2 total shift is approximately $-1.4\pm5.3$~\si{\kilo\joule\per\mole}, and the \MEAH--water slope is $-41.6$~\si{\kelvin} with conditional standard error \SI{60.3}{\kelvin}.
Both Born diameters reach their 5~\si{\angstrom} upper bound, and the unlike-ion interaction $k_{ij}=-1$ doubles the unlike-ion dispersion energy under the dispersion combining rule.
These values indicate compensation among solvation, dispersion and reaction terms, rather than measured ion properties.
The fitted R5 differs from Bates--Pinching by 0.49 pK at 313.15 K, with dissociation enthalpy 63.4 rather than 50.5~\si{\kilo\joule\per\mole}; its total enthalpy shift reaches the +10~\si{\kilo\joule\per\mole} bound.
These local uncertainties and the correlations below limit physical interpretation of individual coefficients.
They do not identify unique ion dimensions or reaction enthalpies, and they do not supply prediction intervals for pressures or process duties.

\section{Phase-search definition and confirmed splits}\label{sec:s-phase}
% P:supplement-05
The complete 162-state table for both fits is supplied as CSV in the data deposit.
The grid has six temperatures, 313.15, 318.15, 333.15, 353.15, 373.15 and \SI{393.15}{\kelvin}, and nine loadings, $10^{-5}$, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.256, 0.3, 0.4, 0.5 and 0.6.
At each temperature and loading, the reference pressure $P_*$ is the bubble pressure of the reported fit (\Cref{tab:phase-reference-pressures}), and the three test states are at $P/P_*=0.50$, 0.80 and 0.95.
Both fits are searched at the same 162 states.
Phase calculations use 9.7292821 mol analytical MEA, 76.9751925 mol water and $9.7292821\alpha$ mol \COtwo.
All extensive Gibbs differences and phase amounts below refer to that feed, rather than the one-mole total-amine property basis.

% P:supplement-phase-summary-01
Nine trial phases per state give 1458 trials for each fit; every trial optimization completes, and none is unresolved.
No descent is resolved for the reported parameters, and all 162 of their existing equilibria are liquid--vapor at these pressures, which lie below their bubble pressure.
The comparison fit has resolved descents at 54 states, with 143 descending trials: ten states each at 313.15 and \SI{318.15}{\kelvin}, nine each at 333.15, 353.15 and \SI{373.15}{\kelvin} and seven at \SI{393.15}{\kelvin} (\Cref{tab:supplement-phase-family}).
The 81 states at 353.15--\SI{393.15}{\kelvin} lie above the temperatures of the fitted \COtwo pressures.

% P:supplement-phase-confirmation-01
At each descent state, descending trials are grouped by phase kind, supported species and composition, with maximum composition difference $10^{-6}$ within a group, and one representative of each group seeds one split calculation of at most 400 iterations.
A split is confirmed when it converges to the requested tolerance with negative $\Delta G/(RT)$, raw residuals at most $10^{-10}$, positive reduced-Hessian minimum, positive phase amounts and positive pressure--density derivatives.
The 91 attempts confirm 51 distinct two-liquid paths at 32 of the 54 descent states, at every temperature (\Cref{tab:phase-splits}).
The water mole fraction is 0.40--0.74 in the water-poor liquid and 0.68--0.97 in the water-rich liquid.
The 40 unsuccessful attempts end with vanished or coincident phases or a nonpositive reduced Hessian; failure to confirm does not remove a resolved descent.
These splits have no experimental confirmation in the cited homogeneous-liquid data \cite[Sect.~4.2]{neumannNewModelCombining2022}.
The tables below give the reference pressures, the counts by temperature and fit, and the confirmed splits.

\input{tables/phase_reference_pressures}

% P:supplement-06
Four liquid starts and five vapor starts are used at each converged equilibrium state.
The liquid starts are a uniform positive composition and three compositions with 0.99 assigned to \COtwo, MEA or water, distributing 0.01 among the other species in proportion to an existing full-support phase.
The uniform composition is charge-balanced by multiplying positive-ion amounts by $\sqrt{q_-/q_+}$ and dividing negative-ion amounts by the same factor, then normalizing; $q_+$ and $q_-$ are the positive and absolute negative charge sums.
This reconstruction is also applied after forming each nearly pure start, followed by an arithmetic correction on the largest positive and negative ion entries.
Vapor starts are uniform, nearly pure \COtwo, nearly pure MEA, nearly pure water, and the ideal-gas estimate $w_i\propto\exp(\pi_i-s_i)$, where $\pi_i=(B^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\lambda)_i$ and $s_i$ is the dimensionless standard chemical potential in the density representation.
Only the three neutral species enter a vapor start.
Each start obtains a density on its specified liquid or vapor branch before the reacting constrained tangent-plane minimization.

% P:supplement-07
Reacting equilibrium and trial calculations use logarithmic positive species fractions and packing fraction, phase amounts, and Lagrange multipliers for the independent balances.
A Newton procedure and the shared Ipopt minimization use exact first and second derivatives, with MA57 linear algebra and a tolerance of $10^{-10}$ on the recomputed residuals used to decide convergence, after the solver's row scaling.
When an initial two-phase solve fails, up to two converged neighboring states at the same temperature provide ordered starts; if needed, the bubble solution initializes liquid fractions 0.5 and 0.9, with at most 400 iterations per attempt and 800 total.
The final state must satisfy material and charge balances, positive compressibility and nonnegative reduced-Hessian curvature.
A separate single-liquid/bubble calculation distinguishes a liquid from liquid--vapor equilibrium at the fixed test pressure.

% P:supplement-08
For each optimized trial, the tangent-plane distance $D$ is Eq.~\ref{eq:tpd-supp}; the multipliers refer to the existing reacting equilibrium.
A donor phase $d$ with positive amounts of every supported trial species has transfer capacity $\beta_{\max}=\min_i(N_dx_{di}/w_i)$.
The donor with greatest capacity is used.
The transfer slope is
\begin{equation}
 S=g_w/(RT)-\sum_iw_i\mu_{di}/(RT)+r_{P,d},\qquad
 r_{P,d}=1-v_d F_{v,d}-Pv_d/(RT),\qquad F=A^{\mathrm{res}}/(RT),
 \label{eq:donor-slope}
\end{equation}
where $v_d$ is molar volume and $F$ is evaluated on a one-mole phase basis.
The term $r_{P,d}$ corrects for the small pressure residual of the existing phase.
For completeness, the distance is
\begin{equation}
 D=g_w/(RT)-\sum_iw_i\pi_i,\qquad \pi_i=(B^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\lambda)_i.
 \label{eq:tpd-supp}
\end{equation}
A negative $D$ and $S<-E_S$ trigger finite transfers at $\beta=\beta_{\max}/2,\ldots,\beta_{\max}/512$.
Each transfer conserves every species amount: $x'_{di}=(N_dx_{di}-\beta w_i)/(N_d-\beta)$, leaving the other phases unchanged.
Both phase densities are closed at the specified pressure.
A trial is a resolved instability only if $\Delta G/(RT)<-E_{\Delta G}$ for an admitted transfer; a negative value within that error estimate remains unresolved as a decrease.

% P:supplement-09
The numerical-error estimate combines association-solver derivative error, the Euler identity defect, floating-point summation and the correction applied to restore the material balance.
For a one-mole phase define
\begin{align}
 E(w)&=(1+|v|)f+\left|F-vF_v-\sum_iw_iF_{n_i}\right|+\gamma_{2N+4}M,\\
 E_S&=E(w)+(1+|v_d|)f_d+E_{\mathrm{feas}}+\gamma_{N+3}M_S,\\
 E_{\Delta G}&=(N_d-\beta)E(x'_d)+\beta E(w)+N_dE(x_d)+E_{\mathrm{feas}}+\gamma_{N+3}M_G.
 \label{eq:phase-error}
\end{align}
Here $f$ is the reported residual-Helmholtz gradient error estimate in the SI volume and amount columns, $N=9$, $\gamma_m=m\epsilon/(1-m\epsilon)$ and $\epsilon$ is machine precision.
The sum magnitude is $M=|F|+\sum_i|w_i[\ln(w_i/v)-1+s_i]|+|Pv/(RT)|$.
$M_S=|g_w/(RT)|+\sum_i|w_i\mu_{di}/(RT)|+|r_{P,d}|$ and $M_G$ sums absolute values of the three extensive Gibbs terms in the transfer difference.
The feasibility estimate is $E_{\mathrm{feas}}=\sum_{p,i}(|\delta n_{pi}|+|\delta n^{\mathrm{intended}}_{pi}-\delta n_{pi}|)|\mu_{pi}/(RT)|$, where the correction solves the balance and charge defects with amount-weighted linear algebra.
No uncertainty in the supplied standard-state functions is included in this numerical estimate.
Finite-transfer compositions must normalize and be charge-neutral to $10^{-12}$; species and conserved-quantity amount errors must be at most $10^{-12}\max(1,n_{\mathrm{feed}})$ mol.
These are numerical resolution rules, not experimental uncertainty bounds or a global stability proof.

% P:supplement-phase-criterion-01
The distance in Eq.~\ref{eq:tpd-supp} is minimized over positive trial fractions satisfying $\sum_i w_i=1$ and $\sum_i z_iw_i=0$; $B$ represents conserved component combinations and $\boldsymbol\lambda$ their multipliers in the reacting equilibrium \cite[Sect.~2]{Michelsen1982}.
Liquid trials support all nine species, and vapor trials support only \COtwo, MEA and water.
A two-phase classification includes liquid--vapor equilibrium and alone does not establish a second liquid.

\section{Comparison fit and bound restriction}\label{sec:s-probes}
% P:supplement-bounds-01
The comparison fit and reported fit use the same 16 adjustable parameters and 307 quantities.
The reported fit is the parameter set reported here.
The restriction changes the \MEAH segment-diameter bounds from 2--\SI{5.8}{\angstrom} to 5.35--\SI{5.8}{\angstrom}, and the \MEAH--water interaction bounds from $[-0.5,0.5]$ to $[-0.5,-0.161]$; all other physical bounds, observations, scales and weights are unchanged.
\Cref{tab:comparison-fit-parameters} lists the comparison-fit values that differ from the reported fit, so that both parameter sets are fully specified.
\Cref{tab:supplement-fitted-parameters} gives the selected values, bounds and conditional standard errors.
Fixed inputs include earlier pure-water, binary and permittivity estimates, documented with their sources in Section~\ref{sec:s-parameters}.

\input{tables/comparison_fit_parameters}

% P:supplement-bounds-02
The fit uses Ceres bounded Levenberg--Marquardt least squares with dense QR, affine parameter scaling and exact equation-of-state and implicit equilibrium derivatives.
The deposited starting parameter vectors and affine transformations specify the numerical scaling.
Function, parameter and gradient tolerances are $10^{-6}$, $10^{-10}$ and $10^{-8}$, respectively, with a maximum of 180 iterations.
Two sequential restricted fits were made, each from one start.
The first used a \MEAH size lower bound of \SI{5.49}{\angstrom}; the second started from its solution and lowered that bound to \SI{5.35}{\angstrom}, stopping on function tolerance after eight iterations.
The restrictions were chosen from fixed-parameter probes: smaller diameters raise density but can overshoot the colder Hartono observations.
Phase searches were applied to the resulting parameter sets, rather than imposed as constraints at every optimizer iteration.

% P:supplement-11
Hartono et al.\ report apparent \COtwo component fraction $x_3$ as well as loading \cite[Table~3, footnote~a]{Hartono2014}.
A separate fit on the loading basis $\alpha_{x_3}=x_3(1+W)/(1-x_3)$, where $W$ is the water-to-total-amine molar ratio, left this ambiguity unresolved and does not replace the reported fit.
