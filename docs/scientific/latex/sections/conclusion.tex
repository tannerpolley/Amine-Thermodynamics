\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-01
A nine-species ePC-SAFT model with explicit \MEAH and \MEACOO chemistry and an SSM+DS Born term was fitted to 48 \COtwo pressures at 40 and \SI{60}{\degreeCelsius} and 94 species measurements at 20--\SI{60}{\degreeCelsius} in 30 wt\% aqueous \MEA.
The fitted pressure and species AARDs are 13.53 and 8.28\,\%.
At \SI{80}{\degreeCelsius}, outside the current fit, the model reproduces the 11 Jou 1995 targets with an AARD of 9.24\,\% and all 21 canonical rows with 20.27\,\%.
These are tests outside the present (142-target, five-coordinate) fit, not data unseen during model development: inherited reaction shifts used two of the 11 targets, and the fixed \COtwo dispersion energy was chosen against a set that included \SI{80}{\degreeCelsius} data.

% P:conclusion-02
The comparison of Born forms depends on the observations fitted.
With Matin's titration bicarbonate included, Original Born has the lower cost, 40.20 against 47.63.
With those 18 observations report-only, SSM+DS is lower, 32.99 against 34.58, a 4.6\,\% advantage relative to Original Born.
Those authors report bicarbonate above NMR values, and the selected model departs further from that bicarbonate, with a signed relative mean of \(-58.84\,\%\).
In this system the Born-form selection depends on the measurement method of the fitted bicarbonate, which must therefore be reported with the fit.

% P:conclusion-03
Pressure transfer to 15 and 45 wt\% \MEA gives AARDs of 75.95 and 46.15\,\%, and extrapolation to 100--\SI{120}{\degreeCelsius} gives 26.44\,\%.
The \HCOthree--water coefficient lies at its bound, the calculated carbonate share ranges from 0.42 to 1.33 times the NMR values, and density and heat capacity remain unqualified.
Two Born probe calculations remain unavailable, with undetermined cause.
All evidence is numerical verification and assessment on existing measurements; no physical validation at new conditions is claimed.
The working domain of the record is \COtwo pressure and speciation in 30 wt\% \MEA at 40--\SI{80}{\degreeCelsius}; the evidence does not support other concentrations, higher temperatures or absorber and stripper design.
