\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-01
An ePC-SAFT calibration with literature reaction laws and tied amine-ion Born diameters describes pressure, speciation, heat, heat capacity and density through one Helmholtz energy without detected additional-liquid instability in the specified finite searches.
The reduced parameter freedom has a measured cost, including worse agreement with the Ma'mun pressures, while concentration transfer, carbonate partition and high-temperature absorption heat remain inaccurate.
The false split of the unpenalized comparison fit reflects opposing energy terms with a prominent dispersion change; the evidence does not identify one microscopic cause.

% P:conclusion-limits
These are calibration and fixed-parameter comparisons against data seen during development, not independent validation.
Local uncertainty remains conditional on the fitted model and inputs, and finite searches do not establish global stability.
The common thermodynamic property calculation is useful for consistent assessment, but its remaining discrepancies preclude an unqualified process-use claim.

% P:conclusion-shared
Table~\ref{tab:shared-parameters} specifies exactly the shared coefficient set held fixed in Part II; the associated fixed fields, topology and standard states are defined in the model section and supplement.
It includes water, \COtwo, hydronium, hydroxide, bicarbonate and carbonate, their shared interactions and R1--R3 laws.
The poorly determined water--bicarbonate interaction, assumed dielectric behavior in loaded solutions and source-correlation extrapolation are the shared inputs most likely to limit transfer.
Part II assesses the consequences with other amines rather than assuming that MEA agreement establishes their accuracy.
