\begin{abstract}
% P:abstract-01
An electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) model with explicit ions is calibrated for aqueous monoethanolamine (MEA) loaded with carbon dioxide (\COtwo) and transferred to diethanolamine (DEA), methyldiethanolamine (MDEA) and 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol (AMP).
The MEA parameters are fitted jointly to pressures, species fractions, absorption heats, heat capacities and densities, with the equilibrium constants taken from the literature.
The parameters of water, \COtwo and the inorganic ions are then held fixed, with the water--anion interactions taken from sodium-salt osmotic coefficients, and each further amine is described by a small fit of its own species.
One set of shared parameters thus serves four classes of amine: the fitted \COtwo partial pressures of MEA at 30 wt\% and of DEA deviate by \res{mea-fitted-pressure-aard-abstract} and \res{dea-fitted-pressure-aard-abstract}\,\%, and the fitted total pressures of MDEA and AMP by \res{mdea-fitted-pressure-aard-abstract} and \res{amp-fitted-pressure-aard-abstract}\,\%.
Within the ranges of their data the models can be used in absorber calculations at these accuracies.
A reactive phase-stability search then tests each fitted model for a second liquid, which the deviations do not show.
None of the four models predicts a second liquid at its measured states.
Two fits made on the way did.
An MDEA fit without a bound converged inside a calculated liquid--liquid gap of the \COtwo-free MDEA--water solution, so the MDEA--water interaction of the MDEA model is bounded to keep that solution a single liquid up to \SI{423}{\kelvin}.
A first AMP fit, whose loaded-solution pressures ended at \SI{353}{\kelvin}, predicted a second liquid above those temperatures, and the AMP model therefore fits the pressures up to \SI{393}{\kelvin}.

\end{abstract}
