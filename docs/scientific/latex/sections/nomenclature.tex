% P:nomenclature-conventions
Subscripts $i,j$ identify species, $r$ reactions, $w$ water and $k$ polynomial or search indices; superscripts res, ig, hs, hc, disp, assoc, DH and Born identify residual, ideal-gas, hard-sphere, hard-chain, dispersion, association and electrostatic terms.
Superscripts calc and obs mean calculated and observed; pure and $\infty,w$ mean pure solvent and infinite dilution in water; $\circ$ denotes the source standard state.
% P:nomenclature-acronyms
AARD: average absolute relative deviation; RMSE: root-mean-square error; MEA: monoethanolamine; MDEA: N-methyldiethanolamine; NMR: nuclear magnetic resonance; VLE: vapor--liquid equilibrium; eNRTL: electrolyte non-random two-liquid; PC-SAFT: perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory; SSM+DS: solvation-shell model with dielectric saturation.
