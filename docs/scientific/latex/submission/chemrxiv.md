# ChemRxiv submission draft

## Title

Thermodynamics of CO₂-loaded aqueous amines with ePC-SAFT: monoethanolamine calibration, transfer to DEA, MDEA and AMP, and phase stability

## Author and affiliation

Tanner W. Polley; ORCID 0009-0008-5957-4152; tpolley3@byu.edu. Department of Chemical Engineering, Brigham Young University, Provo, UT 84602, United States.

## Abstract

An electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) model of aqueous monoethanolamine (MEA) loaded with carbon dioxide (CO₂) is calibrated with its reaction equilibrium constants fixed at literature correlations, to test whether its water, CO₂ and inorganic-ion parameters can be held fixed for diethanolamine (DEA), methyldiethanolamine (MDEA) and 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol (AMP), and whether finite phase-stability searches detect a second liquid. Eleven MEA parameters are estimated jointly from pressure, speciation, absorption-heat, heat-capacity, density and binary equilibrium data; DEA, MDEA and AMP need four, eight and eight more. For MEA, the fitted CO₂ pressures at nominal 30 wt% have an average absolute relative deviation of 11.6% and the 112 not fitted 24.9%; no instability is detected, although the fit did not impose stability. Pressures at 15 and 45 wt% MEA, carbonate shares and high-temperature absorption heats remain inaccurate. Fitted loaded-solution pressures of the other amines deviate by 10.5% (AMP) to 28.5% (DEA); no instability is detected for DEA, but its water–DEA excess enthalpies have the wrong sign. For MDEA, no liquid–liquid gap of the CO₂-free solution is detected at the reported parameters, interpolated toward a stability-penalized fit; one loaded-solution split remains, at 262.9 MPa, far above process pressures. For AMP, with the cation–water temperature coefficient set from a scan, no instability is detected; fitting it as well gives splits above 353 K at a similar deviation. The models are calibrations against data examined during development, with conditional standard errors; they provide neither validation nor a basis for absorber design.

## Keywords

ePC-SAFT; monoethanolamine; diethanolamine; methyldiethanolamine; 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol; parameter transfer; phase stability.

## Data availability

The electronic data comprise processed observations, parameter values, numerical results and the analysis and figure code under the MIT license; third-party measurements remain under the terms of their sources. They will be deposited in Zenodo, and the DOI will be added at submission. A software index in the deposit lists the versions, checksums and commands needed to repeat the calculations. The MEA calculations were made with version 1.3.0 of the epcsaft package, and the DEA, MDEA and AMP calculations with version 1.3.1. Referees will receive access to the compiled epcsaft package through the corresponding author.
