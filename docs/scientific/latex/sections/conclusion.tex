\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-mea
An ePC-SAFT model of \COtwo-loaded aqueous MEA with \res{mea-jointly-fitted-parameter-count-conclusion} jointly fitted parameters and literature equilibrium constants deviates by \res{mea-fitted-pressure-aard-conclusion}\,\% from its \res{mea-legacy-pressure-count-conclusion} fitted \COtwo partial pressures at 30 wt\% and by about \res{mea-fitted-heat-cp-density-maximum-aard-rounded}\,\% or less from its fitted heats, heat capacities and densities.
Within the ranges of its data it can supply these properties to absorber calculations at the accuracies reported here; \cref{sec:limits} states the ranges of use of all four models.

% P:conclusion-transfer
With the water, \COtwo and inorganic-ion parameters of that model held fixed, and the water--bicarbonate and water--carbonate interactions fixed from sodium-salt osmotic coefficients so that no amine data determine them, DEA, MDEA and AMP could each be described by a small fit of its own species: the fitted loaded-solution pressures deviate by \res{dea-fitted-pressure-aard-conclusion}\,\% for DEA, \res{mdea-fitted-pressure-aard-conclusion}\,\% for MDEA and \res{amp-fitted-pressure-aard-conclusion}\,\% for AMP, on \res{dea-fitted-partial-pressure-count-conclusion}, \res{mdea-fitted-kuranov-pressure-count-conclusion} and \res{amp-reported-loaded-pressure-count-conclusion} observations.
One set of shared parameters thus gives the pressures, speciation, absorption heats and densities of a primary, a secondary, a tertiary and a hindered amine from one Helmholtz energy.
\emph{Transfer} means no more than this: DEA and AMP each fit a carbamate-hydrolysis correlation, which for DEA lies well below the measured constant, and MDEA needed its loaded-solution pressures weighted against its many density and heat data.

% P:conclusion-phase
In the phase-stability searches none of the four models predicts a second liquid at its measured states.
Two fits made on the way did: an MDEA fit without a bound converged inside a calculated liquid--liquid gap of the \COtwo-free MDEA--water solution, which a bound on the MDEA--water interaction now excludes up to \SI{423}{\kelvin}, and a first AMP fit predicted a second liquid above the temperatures of its fitted pressures, which the AMP model, fitted to \SI{393}{\kelvin}, does not.
Both were converged fits of their objectives, so a phase-stability search tests a fitted electrolyte model in a way that its pressure and density deviations do not.
