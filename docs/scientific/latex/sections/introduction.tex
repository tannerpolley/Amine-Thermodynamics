\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}
% P:introduction-00
Can one equation of state describe the equilibrium, caloric and density properties of loaded aqueous monoethanolamine (MEA) with literature reaction constants and fewer adjustable parameters, without predicting an additional liquid?
This question matters because absorption pressure, species concentrations, heat release and liquid density enter different parts of an absorber calculation but describe the same reacting solvent \cite{Zhang2011,Freguia2003,Amundsen2009}.
A common Helmholtz energy relates these properties through composition, temperature and volume derivatives.
Agreement with one property alone does not establish agreement with the others or the correct number of phases.

\subsection{Models of loaded aqueous MEA}
% P:introduction-03
Activity-coefficient models combine reaction equilibria with a description of nonideal liquid activities.
B\"ottinger et al.\ fitted a Pitzer model to species observations while keeping the Bates--Pinching MEA dissociation law \cite[Sects.~4--6, Table~5]{Bottinger2008}.
Zhang et al.\ jointly estimated electrolyte non-random two-liquid (eNRTL) parameters from pressure, heat, heat capacity and nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) data, and compared additional pressure sources separately \cite[Sect.~3.3, Tables~9--11]{Zhang2011}.
These studies establish multi-property fitting and explicit accounting of data use as relevant precedents.

% P:introduction-05
Statistical associating fluid theory (SAFT) provides an equation of state for both phases and calculates density directly.
Baygi and Pahlavanzadeh combined perturbed-chain SAFT (PC-SAFT) with ideal-activity reaction equilibria after fitting pure-MEA and binary parameters \cite[Sects.~3.1--3.3]{Baygi2015}.
Najafloo and Zarei used Huang--Radosz SAFT with the same broad separation between molecular phase properties and ideal reaction activities \cite[Sects.~2--3.3]{Najafloo2018}.
Reactive-association approaches instead represent reaction products through bonded fractions, including soft-SAFT and group-contribution SAFT-\(\gamma\) Mie \cite{lloretConsistentTransferableThermodynamic2017,perdomoPredictiveGroupcontributionFramework2023}.
Their species definitions and fitting roles differ from explicit-ion reaction models.

% P:introduction-08
Electrolyte PC-SAFT (ePC-SAFT) adds ionic electrostatics to the molecular equation of state \cite{Cameretti2005,Held2014}.
Applications to N-methyldiethanolamine (MDEA) estimate subsystem inputs before calculating reacting-mixture pressure and speciation \cite[Sects.~4.1--4.3]{Uyan2015}\cite[Sects.~6.1--6.3]{Wangler2018}.
Cleeton et al.\ extend this treatment to competitive acid-gas absorption and discuss its concentration-dependent discrepancies \cite[Sects.~3--4]{Cleeton2020}.
MDEA does not form carbamate, so this experience does not determine the MEA-ion parameters.

% P:introduction-prior-mea
Polley and Hedengren already describe the explicit MEA ions, including carbamate, with ePC-SAFT \cite[Sect.~2.2, App.~B]{polleyReactiveEPCSAFTModeling2026}.
Their parameters were estimated in separate steps, and their column calculations used empirical caloric properties.
The present question concerns joint property estimation and phase assessment with one thermodynamic property calculation, rather than the first introduction of explicit MEA ions.
A joint fit can also shift reaction constants or fit separate amine-ion Born diameters; such freedom can lower the objective, but shifted constants depart from the source reaction laws, and the \COtwo hydrolysis constant is shared with other amines.

\subsection{Property constraints and phase stability}
% P:introduction-10
Pressure, species and caloric measurements constrain different combinations of reaction equilibria and physical interactions.
NMR may report molecular and protonated MEA as a sum, while titration and carbon-balance estimates give different information about bicarbonate and carbonate \cite[Table~3]{Bottinger2008}\cite[Eqs.~18--19b]{matinFacileMethodDetermination2012}.
Direct calorimetry also differs from heat inferred from the temperature dependence of pressure \cite{Kim2007}.
A joint fit must preserve these observable definitions rather than treat every reported value as an interchangeable species fraction or heat.

% P:introduction-12
A single-liquid property objective contains no requirement that the calculated liquid be stable against formation of another phase.
Michelsen's tangent-plane criterion tests whether an additional phase can lower Gibbs energy \cite[Sect.~2]{Michelsen1982}.
For reacting electrolytes the trial must also satisfy charge neutrality and the chemical constraints \cite{Tsanas2022,Ascani2022,Perdomo2025}.
Phase searches therefore supply a separate numerical check on a calibration, with a finite search giving no global proof.

\subsection{Aim and scope of the series}
% P:introduction-13
This work finds that fixed literature reaction laws and Born diameters tied to the amine-ion segment diameters permit a reduced joint calibration with no detected additional-liquid instability in the specified searches.
The restrictions leave the objective value and the fitted pressure and density deviations of a less restricted 16-parameter calibration nearly unchanged, but they worsen some pressure comparisons outside the fit and leave discrepancies in pressures at other MEA concentrations, minor species and high-temperature heats.
The paper reviews the ePC-SAFT contributions, reports the cost of each parameter restriction, and compares the adopted parameters with the Polley--Hedengren (2026) parameters and published models on identical observations.
Because all comparison data were seen during development, these comparisons describe a calibration and calculations at fixed parameters, not independent validation.
Part I establishes the MEA formulation and the exact shared inputs; Part II examines their use with other amines.
