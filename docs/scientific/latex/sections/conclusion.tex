\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-base
With explicit ions, ePC-SAFT gives the \COtwo solubility in aqueous MEA, DEA, MDEA and AMP, a primary, a secondary, a tertiary and a hindered amine, from one Helmholtz energy and shared parameters determined without amine measurements.
The base holds the parameters of water, \COtwo and the inorganic ions, the inorganic equilibrium constants and the water--bicarbonate and water--carbonate interactions from sodium-salt osmotic coefficients.
Each amine is fitted through its own species, DEA and AMP each holding two parameters at their MEA values.
The fitted loaded-solution pressures deviate by \res{mea-fitted-pressure-aard-conclusion}\,\% for MEA at 30 wt\%, \res{dea-fitted-pressure-aard-conclusion}\,\% for DEA, \res{mdea-fitted-pressure-aard-conclusion}\,\% for MDEA and \res{amp-fitted-pressure-aard-conclusion}\,\% for AMP, on \res{mea-legacy-pressure-count-conclusion}, \res{dea-fitted-partial-pressure-count-conclusion}, \res{mdea-fitted-kuranov-pressure-count-conclusion} and \res{amp-reported-loaded-pressure-count-conclusion} observations.

% P:conclusion-mea
The MEA model, with \res{mea-jointly-fitted-parameter-count-conclusion} jointly fitted parameters and literature equilibrium constants, also deviates by about \res{mea-fitted-heat-cp-density-maximum-aard-rounded}\,\% or less from its fitted heats, heat capacities and densities.
The four models supply thermodynamic properties for absorber calculations within the ranges and deviations that \cref{sec:limits} states property by property.

% P:conclusion-phase
The phase-stability searches, with liquid and vapor trial phases at the measured states and on grids of temperature, amine content and loading, find no confirmed second liquid with any of the four models; \res{mdea-measured-states-unclassified-conclusion} heat-measurement states of MDEA are unclassified.
For DEA, MDEA and AMP the fitted data and their weights decide whether a second liquid is predicted.
A DEA fit without the pressures of Lawson and Garst predicts a second liquid.
Weighting the \COtwo-free MDEA pressures at the scale of the loaded pressures removes the calculated liquid--liquid gap in the \COtwo-free solution.
An AMP fit without the pressures of Tong et al.\ and those of Hartono et al.\ above \SI{353}{\kelvin} predicts a second liquid at \res{amp-without-hot-minimum-split-temperature-conclusion}--\SI{\res{amp-without-hot-maximum-split-temperature-conclusion}}{\kelvin}.
The AMP model, which fits those pressures, has no detected instability at its searched states; at the \res{amp-measured-control-states-conclusion} measured total pressures at which both AMP calibrations were also searched, it detects none at \res{amp-reported-measured-pressure-control-no-descent-conclusion}, and at \res{amp-reported-liquid-vapor-qualified-controls-conclusion} its equilibrium is liquid plus vapor, and the search does not decide whether a second liquid also forms.
Fits to single-liquid data can therefore predict a second liquid, and a phase-stability search tests a fitted electrolyte model in a way that its deviations from the fitted data do not.
