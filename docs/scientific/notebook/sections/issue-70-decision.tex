\section{Issue 70 parameter decision}

Issue 70 ends with a supported negative result. The retained diagnostic
candidate evaluated 119 equilibrium states and 257 comparison quantities with
no omitted final comparison row, but its parameter optimizer terminated with
\texttt{NO\_CONVERGENCE}.  The active scaled Jacobian had condition number
4745.95 and smallest singular value $4.27\times10^{-4}$.  Rows formerly marked
reserved were opened during model selection, so no untouched replacement
partition remains for independent validation.  The candidate calculation also
used an Engine wheel whose SHA-256 differs from the frozen Engine lock.

The multiproperty comparisons remain useful diagnostics, not validation.  In
particular, the Wong molecular-CO2 challenge contains sodium perchlorate beyond
the nine-species chemistry, and the calorimetry comparison lacks an accepted
reference-state derivative and covariance model.  These competing explanations
prevent attribution of the residual improvements to one predictive parameter
set.

The machine-readable decision record and generated parameter, reaction,
comparison, and downstream-property tables are retained in the Issue 70
results directory.  The associated refusal record prohibits predictive export
and absorber-column mapping.  The fixed-parameter manuscript boundary
therefore remains in force.
