\section{Data and methods}\label{sec:data-methods}

\subsection{Observations}
% P:methods-01
All solutions are 30 wt\% \MEA on a \COtwo-free basis unless stated otherwise.
Supplementary Section~S4 places the observations below within an inventory of published measurements near 30 wt\% \MEA, and Supplementary Section~S3 lists the corrections made to the source data before fitting.
The pressure comparisons use 162 measured \COtwo partial pressures at 40--\SI{120}{\degreeCelsius} from six studies \cite{Aronu2011,Hilliard2008,idrisSpeciationMEACO2Adducts2014,Jou1995,Mamun2005,Xu2011}.
Each row keeps its source table and row, the measured temperature, the loading and the pressure.
Hilliard and Xu and Rochelle report the amine concentration as molality, and their rows are calculated at the reported molality and measured temperature rather than at a nominal composition and series temperature \cite[App.~D.4]{Hilliard2008}\cite[Table~1]{Xu2011}.
Three Xu and Rochelle rows, at 120.4--\SI{121.8}{\degreeCelsius}, lie above \SI{393.15}{\kelvin}, the upper limit of the temperature domain declared for the reaction correlations, and are not evaluated.
Idris et al.\ report a combined standard uncertainty for each pressure \cite[Table~2]{idrisSpeciationMEACO2Adducts2014}, and the other five studies give no row-level uncertainty that we retained.
The 23 \COtwo partial pressures of Wagner et al.\ near 30 wt\% \MEA entered none of the fitting or selection steps described in \cref{sec:parameters} \cite{wagnerSolubilityCarbonDioxide2013}; each row is calculated at its reported temperature and \COtwo-free \MEA mass fraction, 29.93--30.52 wt\% near \SI{353}{\kelvin} and 30.28--30.95 wt\% near \SI{392}{\kelvin}.

% P:methods-02
The liquid species data come from two methods.
B\"ottinger et al.\ measured species by online \textsuperscript{1}H and \textsuperscript{13}C NMR spectroscopy \cite[Sect.~3]{Bottinger2008}.
Fast proton exchange makes only the sum \ce{MEA + MEAH+} observable, and the authors report carbonate as practically absent, so their bicarbonate value is compared with the modeled \ce{HCO3- + CO3^2-} pool.
Each B\"ottinger state is calculated at the reported unloaded \MEA mass fraction, which is 0.31 at \SI{80}{\degreeCelsius} \cite[Table~3]{Bottinger2008}.
B\"ottinger et al.\ also report 2-oxazolidone, which the nine-species model does not contain; their \SI{80}{\degreeCelsius} state at loading 0.51, where this byproduct becomes quantifiable, is not compared.
Matin et al.\ liberated the dissolved \COtwo with phosphoric acid and stripped it with nitrogen to a gas analyzer, and derived the species from acid and base titrations and material balances without equilibrium constants \cite[pp.~6614--6615]{matinFacileMethodDetermination2012}.
Their method reports \MEA, \MEAH and \MEACOO separately and assigns carbonate to bicarbonate, so their bicarbonate value is also compared with the \ce{HCO3- + CO3^2-} pool \cite[Eqs.~18--19b]{matinFacileMethodDetermination2012}.
All 95 numerical values in the 19-row Matin transcription were checked against Table~S.1 of the supporting information \cite[Table~S.1, Supplementary Material]{matinFacileMethodDetermination2012}.
The source figures label the measurements at \SI{21}{\degreeCelsius}; that temperature is treated as equivalent to \SI{20}{\degreeCelsius} in these calculations.
Jakobsen et al.\ report bicarbonate and carbonate separately and print two loadings for each sample \cite[Tables~A1--A2]{Jakobsen2005}; their observations enter only the carbonate-share comparison, at the printed maximum loading.
Every species target is a true-species liquid mole fraction.

% P:methods-03
\Cref{tab:mea-data-inventory} summarizes the fitted target sets and comparison groups; these groups overlap, and their counts are not additive.
The 141 fitted targets belong to 83 states: 47 pressure states from Hilliard and Jou et al.\ and 36 species states from B\"ottinger et al.\ and Matin et al.
The fitted pressure states span loadings 0.0563--0.6679 and \COtwo partial pressures 0.00147--\SI{282}{\kilo\pascal}; the fitted species states span loadings 0.0749--0.5309.
Loading is expressed as mol \COtwo per mol total amine, and the unloaded-solvent mass fraction is \(w_{\MEA}=m_{\MEA}/(m_{\MEA}+m_{\HtwoO})\), rather than a mass fraction of the loaded liquid.
The 159 evaluated pressures in the six-study set span loadings 0.00323--0.68891 and 0.00147--\SI{2804}{\kilo\pascal}; these combine fitted observations with comparisons that informed earlier calibration.
The 23 Wagner pressure states span loadings 0.10259--0.51594 and 1.58--\SI{78.7}{\kilo\pascal} and provide a prediction comparison because they entered no fitting or selection step.
The 70 concentration-transfer pressure states span loadings 0.01690--0.56491 and 0.0008--\SI{16.0024}{\kilo\pascal} and also entered no calibration step.
A Hilliard measurement at \SI{60.106}{\degreeCelsius} lies above \SI{333.15}{\kelvin}, the upper limit of the fitted temperature range, and is compared but not fitted.
Two B\"ottinger states at \SI{40}{\degreeCelsius} and the Matin state at loading 0.455 are also compared but not fitted, because the parameter set that started the fitting had no converged solution at these three states with an earlier software build.
Except for the Wagner et al.\ rows, the pressure and species data at 80--\SI{120}{\degreeCelsius} informed fixed values of the model (\cref{sec:parameters}), so these comparisons are outside the present fit but not independent of model development.

\subsection{Parameters}\label{sec:parameters}
% P:methods-04
\Cref{tab:full-ionic-ssm-ds-parameters,tab:binary-interaction-parameters,tab:reaction-equilibrium-constants} list every parameter of the selected SSM+DS parameter set, F1, and \cref{tab:parameter-accounting} states how each was obtained.
The final fit re-estimates five parameters: \(k_{\MEACOO,\HtwoO}\), \(k_{\MEAH,\HtwoO}\) at \(T_{\mathrm{ref}}\), its reciprocal-temperature slope \(b\), \(k_{\HCOthree,\HtwoO}\) and \(k_{\MEAH,\MEACOO}\), against the 141 targets of \cref{tab:mea-data-inventory}.
The other values are fixed, and some of them were estimated from data before the present fits.
The segment diameters, dispersion energies and Born diameters of \MEAH and \MEACOO were fitted to liquid speciation alone: NMR values of B\"ottinger et al.\ and Jakobsen et al.\ and titration values of Matin et al.\ at 20--\SI{60}{\degreeCelsius}~\cite{Bottinger2008,Jakobsen2005,matinFacileMethodDetermination2012}.
The \MEA--water and \COtwo--water interactions were fitted to binary VLE data at 363--\SI{432}{\kelvin} and 313--\SI{393}{\kelvin} \cite{caiBinaryIsobaricVaporLiquid1996,kiepeExperimentalDeterminationPrediction2002}.
The \HCOthree and \COthree Born diameters are fixed at \SI{3.0}{\angstrom}; they were examined against bicarbonate and carbonate data during model development but are neither independently determined ion properties nor uniquely identified fitted values.
The \MEA permittivity of 32 is an assumed fixed value for which no source is retained, and the water permittivity polynomial of \cref{eq:water-permittivity} is the software's approximation of the Archer--Wang water permittivity~\cite{archerDielectricConstantWater1990}, referenced to \SI{298.15}{\kelvin}.

% P:methods-05
The fixed R2 and R5 reaction-enthalpy shifts, the R5 coefficient \(A_5\) and the \COtwo dispersion energy were also calibrated before the final five-parameter fit.
Their calibration used pressures at 40--\SI{120}{\degreeCelsius}, species at 20--\SI{80}{\degreeCelsius} and heat-of-absorption data, as detailed in Supplementary Section~S5.
These are calibration values, not independently determined physical reaction enthalpies or molecular properties.
Pressure and species comparisons at 80--\SI{120}{\degreeCelsius} are therefore not independent of model development; among the pressure comparisons at \SI{80}{\degreeCelsius} and above, only the Wagner et al.\ rows entered none of these steps.
The 15 and 45 wt\% pressures and the loaded densities also entered none of them, whereas the Jakobsen et al.\ data entered the fit of the \MEAH and \MEACOO parameters.

\subsection{Objective and estimation}
% P:methods-06
The fit minimizes \(C=\tfrac12\sum_jr_j^2\) over the 141 fitted targets, with
\begin{equation}
    r_{P,j}=\frac{\ln\left(p_{\COtwo,j}^{\mathrm{calc}}/p_{\COtwo,j}^{\mathrm{obs}}\right)}{0.3},
    \qquad
    r_{x,j}=\frac{x_j^{\mathrm{calc}}-x_j^{\mathrm{obs}}}{0.1\,x_j^{\mathrm{obs}}+0.001}.
    \label{eq:fit-residuals}
\end{equation}
These scales are the declared fitting weights, not measured standard deviations, and every target has unit weight within its family.
The bounds are \(\pm0.5\) for the three ion--water coefficients, \(\pm1\) for \MEAH--\MEACOO and \(\pm\SI{1000}{\kelvin}\) for \(b\), with parameter scales of 0.01 and \SI{10}{\kelvin}.

% P:methods-07
Six fits use this objective, the same five parameters and the same bounds.
F1 is the selected SSM+DS fit, and F2 fits the original Born form.
F3 removes the Born term, leaving the Debye--H\"uckel term as the only electrostatic contribution.
F4 and F5 repeat F1 and F2 with Matin's 18 pool targets added to the objective, 159 targets in all.
F6 is an SSM+DS fit in which R2 and R5 return to their source correlations, R4 already being at its source correlation, and the \COtwo dispersion energy returns to the published \SI{169.21}{\kelvin}~\cite{Gross2001}.
The 18 Matin pool targets are not fitted in F1--F3 and F6, for the reason given in \cref{sec:results-born}, and they are compared for F1, F2 and F6.
The 100--\SI{120}{\degreeCelsius} pressures are not fitted.
SSM+DS is the selected form: it was declared the primary form before the final fits, and it has the lower cost on the 141 base targets, 32.057 against 60.008; this is a local comparison, not a statistical test.
F2 is reported as the matched alternative.

% P:methods-08
Each fit is a bounded Levenberg--Marquardt least-squares problem with exact implicit derivatives of the equilibrium states with respect to the fitted parameters.
Each was started from two prescribed parameter vectors; all twelve runs converged to matching costs (Supplementary Section~S5).
The lower-cost start is reported; this is a local optimum, not evidence of a global one.
For F1 and F2, the covariance of the interior parameters is \(s^2(J_I^{\mathsf{T}}J_I)^{-1}\), where \(J_I\) holds the columns of the weighted residual Jacobian for the parameters not at a bound, in physical units, and \(s^2=2C/(n-p)\) with \(n=141\) and \(p\) interior parameters.
These standard errors are conditional on the active bounds and the declared residual scales, and they treat residuals as independent, although species derived from one titration are not; they are indicative only.
A parameter at a bound has no symmetric interval.

\subsection{Numerical verification and metrics}
% P:methods-09
In every equilibrium calculation, the conserved \COtwo-equivalent carbon of the feed equals the reported loading, \(\alpha=(n_{\mathrm{C}}-2n_{\mathrm{am}})/n_{\mathrm{am}}\), where \(n_{\mathrm{C}}\) is the total carbon of the feed, including the small ion amounts that start the solution, and \(n_{\mathrm{am}}\) is the total amine, each \MEA unit carrying two carbons.
Re-evaluation reproduces the fitted costs and satisfies material balance, electroneutrality and stationarity; Supplementary Section~S5 gives the exact numerical residuals.
All 1,749 retained evaluation states, including fitted-state replays and comparisons outside the fit, meet the numerical checks.
These checks verify the numerical solutions, not the physical model.

% P:methods-10
Agreement is reported as
\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{AARD}=\frac{100\,\%}{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left|\frac{y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}}{y_j^{\mathrm{obs}}}-1\right|,
    \label{eq:aard}
\end{equation}
with the mean of \(\ln(y^{\mathrm{calc}}/y^{\mathrm{obs}})\) reported as signed logarithmic bias.
Each value is computed from the retained calculations and rounded for display.
