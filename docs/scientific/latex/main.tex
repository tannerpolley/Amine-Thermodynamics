\begin{abstract}
% P:abstract-01
Equation-of-state models of carbon dioxide (\COtwo) in aqueous amines are calibrated amine by amine and judged by their deviations from measured pressures, but a fit to single-liquid data does not require the calculated liquid to be stable.
Here an electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) model with explicit ions is calibrated for four amines on one base set without amine data: the parameters of water, \COtwo and the inorganic ions, the equilibrium constants of the inorganic reactions, and water--anion interactions fixed from sodium-salt osmotic coefficients.
Monoethanolamine (MEA) is fitted jointly to pressures, species fractions, absorption heats, heat capacities and densities.
Diethanolamine (DEA), methyldiethanolamine (MDEA) and 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol (AMP) are each fitted through their own species, DEA and AMP taking two carbamate or cation parameters each from the MEA fit.
The fitted \COtwo partial pressures of MEA at 30 wt\% and of DEA deviate by \res{mea-fitted-pressure-aard-abstract} and \res{dea-fitted-pressure-aard-abstract}\,\%, and the fitted total pressures of MDEA and AMP by \res{mdea-fitted-pressure-aard-abstract} and \res{amp-fitted-pressure-aard-abstract}\,\%.
Reactive phase-stability searches at the measured states and on grids of temperature, amine content and loading find no confirmed second liquid with any of the four models.
For two amines that outcome rests on the data of the fit.
An AMP fit to fewer pressures, none above \SI{353}{\kelvin}, predicted a second liquid at higher temperatures, and adding the remaining pressures alone removes it.
Weighting the \COtwo-free MDEA pressures like the loaded ones removes a calculated liquid--liquid gap in the \COtwo-free solution.

\end{abstract}
