\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-mea
With the equilibrium constants fixed at literature correlations and the Born diameters of the amine ions tied to their segment diameters, an 11-parameter ePC-SAFT calibration describes the pressure, speciation, absorption heat, heat capacity and density of loaded aqueous MEA with one equation of state.
The fitted \COtwo pressures at nominal 30 wt\% deviate by 11.6\,\%, and finite phase-stability searches detect no instability, although stability was not imposed in the fit.
The restrictions cost accuracy outside the fit, and pressures at 15 and 45 wt\% MEA, carbonate shares and absorption heats at high temperature remain inaccurate.

% P:conclusion-transfer
The water, \COtwo and inorganic-ion parameters of that calibration, held fixed, support a fit of DEA, MDEA and AMP with four, eight and eight amine-specific parameters, and one AMP coefficient set from a scan.
The fitted loaded-solution pressures deviate by 28.5\,\% for DEA, 20.1\,\% for MDEA and 10.5\,\% for AMP, and those not fitted by 17.9--85.4\,\%, so the transfer gives approximate rather than close pressures.

% P:conclusion-stability
Phase stability separates the three amines.
No instability is detected for DEA, whose water--DEA excess enthalpies nevertheless have the wrong sign.
For MDEA the local minimum without a constraint, from one initial set, has a calculated liquid--liquid gap of the \COtwo-free solution: a bound on the MDEA--water interaction removes it and raises the fitted objective from 479.4 to 564.5.
No loaded-solution split is found among the searches that finish, but the search at the state of an earlier split does not finish.
The AMP model, with its cation--water temperature coefficient fixed at a value chosen from a scan, has no detected instability, and fitting that coefficient as well brings splits above \SI{353}{\kelvin}.

% P:conclusion-published
On the 101 \COtwo partial pressures of Ermatchkov et al.\ the MDEA model deviates by 17.9\,\%, against 16.8\,\% for their parameter set I; neither was fitted to these data, but they helped choose the initial MDEA parameters, so this is not a blind test.
On the 28 total pressures of P\'erez-Salado Kamps et al., to which both published sets were fitted and the MDEA model was not, the published sets are far closer.

% P:conclusion-limits
All four models are calibrations against data examined during development and provide neither physical validation nor a basis for absorber design.
