# ChemRxiv submission draft

## Title

Thermodynamics of CO₂-loaded aqueous amines with ePC-SAFT: monoethanolamine calibration, transfer to DEA, MDEA and AMP, and phase stability

## Author and affiliation

Tanner W. Polley; ORCID 0009-0008-5957-4152; tpolley3@byu.edu. Department of Chemical Engineering, Brigham Young University, Provo, UT 84602, United States.

## Abstract

An electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory model is calibrated for aqueous monoethanolamine (MEA) loaded with carbon dioxide (CO₂), and its water, CO₂ and inorganic-ion parameters, with water–anion interactions from sodium-salt osmotic coefficients, are held fixed while diethanolamine (DEA), methyldiethanolamine (MDEA) and 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol (AMP) are each fitted. With every reaction reference at 100 kPa, equilibrium, density and enthalpy follow from one Gibbs energy, whose pressure derivative agrees with the volume within the stated numerical error (10.5% for MEA, 8.9% for DEA). Fitted CO₂ partial pressures of the 11-parameter MEA model at 30 wt% deviate by 11.34%, more at other concentrations and stripper temperatures. Fitted CO₂ partial pressures deviate by 42.75% for DEA, and fitted total pressures by 10.82% for AMP and 4.39% for MDEA. The MDEA fit, from an inherited start because only one of 64 drawn starts was evaluable, is provisional and deviates by 29.21% from unfitted CO₂ partial pressures. Phase-stability searches find no instability for MEA or at 666 bubble-pressure-scaled AMP states, and no confirmed MDEA split. For DEA they confirm splits at four states whose measured densities the single-liquid calculation matches; these are failures of the DEA model. A first AMP fit, with pressures to 353 K, split at 363–393 K; a refit adding 58 pressures to 393 K confirms none, also at six measured-pressure controls, four unresolved, where the first fit splits. The searches show where a fit extrapolates or fails; the models are calibrations, not design tools.

## Keywords

ePC-SAFT; monoethanolamine; diethanolamine; methyldiethanolamine; 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol; parameter transfer; phase stability.

## Data availability

The electronic data comprise processed observations, parameter values, numerical results and the analysis and figure code under the MIT license; third-party measurements remain under the terms of their sources. They will be deposited in Zenodo, and the DOI will be added at submission. A software index in the deposit lists the versions, checksums and commands needed to repeat the calculations. The four reported models were calculated with version 1.3.1 of the epcsaft package, and the MEA fits with the MEA-fitted shared set with version 1.3.0; the current release, 1.3.2, was not used for them. Referees will receive access to the compiled epcsaft package through the corresponding author.
