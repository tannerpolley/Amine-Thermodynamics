\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-mea
An ePC-SAFT model of \COtwo-loaded aqueous MEA with 11 jointly fitted parameters and literature equilibrium constants deviates by 11.3\,\% from its 47 fitted \COtwo partial pressures at 30 wt\% and by about 3\,\% or less from its fitted heats, heat capacities and densities.
Its pressures at 15 and 45 wt\% MEA and at stripper temperatures, its carbonate shares and its absorption heats at high temperature remain inaccurate, and the cause of the carbonate deficit is unresolved.

% P:conclusion-transfer
With the water, \COtwo and inorganic-ion parameters of that model held fixed, DEA, MDEA and AMP could each be fitted: the fitted loaded-solution pressures deviate by 36.6\,\% for DEA, 10.1\,\% for MDEA and 10.9\,\% for AMP, on 30, 66 and 105 observations.
\emph{Transfer} means no more than this.
DEA and AMP each need a fitted carbamate-hydrolysis constant, and for DEA no tested water--DEA interaction represents the binary excess enthalpies and bubble pressures together.

% P:conclusion-shared
The shared water--bicarbonate and water--carbonate interactions are fixed from sodium-salt osmotic coefficients, so that no amine data determine them.
They are not better for every amine than an interaction fitted to the MEA data: the deviation of the 66 fitted MDEA total pressures falls from 20.1 to 10.1\,\%, but that of 101 unfitted MDEA \COtwo partial pressures rises from 17.9 to 38.4\,\% and that of the fitted DEA pressures from 28.5 to 36.6\,\%.

% P:conclusion-phase
Phase-stability searches of the fitted models detect no instability for MEA, DEA and AMP.
For MDEA a bound keeps the \COtwo-free solution free of a calculated liquid--liquid gap only up to \SI{423}{\kelvin}, and one calculated split remains at a loaded state far outside the measured conditions.
A first AMP fit, whose loaded-solution pressures ended at \SI{353}{\kelvin}, gave calculated liquid--liquid splits at 363--\SI{393}{\kelvin}, and fitting the measured pressures at those temperatures removed them.
A phase-stability search is therefore a useful check on where a fit has been extrapolated.
The four models are calibrations against data examined during development; \cref{sec:limits} states what they do not establish.
