\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-01
One nine-species ePC-SAFT parameterization with explicit carbamate chemistry can be calibrated jointly to \COtwo partial pressure, speciation, integral heat, loaded and \COtwo-free density, heat capacity and \MEA--water binary data for 30 wt\% \MEA, subject to disclosed failures; this is a feasibility result for one constrained local fit, not an optimum.
The 16-coordinate, 307-row calibration gives common-subset pressure/species AARDs of 11.86/7.10\,\% and Vinjarapu integral-heat RMSE of \SI{1.83}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} \COtwo.
Native densities require no empirical volume translation, but only 44 of the 47 fitted loaded rows meet the declared $2s$ requirement.
The three misses, one Amundsen state at loading 0.30 and Hartono states at loadings 0.29 and 0.39, are all low at \SI{353.15}{\kelvin}.

% P:conclusion-02
No descent was numerically resolved in 657 trials at 73 specified states (313.15--\SI{333.15}{\kelvin}, $\alpha$ $10^{-5}$--0.5).
Compared with A6, this endpoint exchanges resolved descent at some specified states for poorer agreement at three warm-density states outside the phase family's temperatures.
The comparison neither establishes a minimum density penalty nor isolates a Born or heat effect.
No stability claim is made above \SI{333.15}{\kelvin}, above loading 0.5 or at the density failures.

% P:conclusion-03
The carbonate share, titration bicarbonate/carbonate pool, unloaded \SI{318.15}{\kelvin} heat capacity and 45 wt\% pressure transfer remain substantial limitations.
Two sequential single-start local fits in sampled bounds and conditional parameter errors provide no global optimum, parameter uniqueness or prediction intervals.
Fitted agreement is calibration; the finite phase search and the re-evaluation of the selected parameter file are numerical verification; comparisons examined during model development are not independent validation.
This evidence does not validate phase behavior or absorber/stripper performance.
