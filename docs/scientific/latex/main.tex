\begin{abstract}
% P:abstract-01
Equation-of-state models of carbon dioxide (\COtwo) solubility in aqueous alkanolamines are calibrated amine by amine and judged by their pressure deviations, but fits to single-liquid data do not require a stable calculated liquid.
An electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) model with explicit ions describes four aqueous amines on shared parameters determined without amine measurements: water, \COtwo, inorganic ions and reactions, and water--anion interactions from sodium-salt osmotic coefficients.
Monoethanolamine (MEA) is fitted to pressures, species, heats, heat capacities and densities, and diethanolamine (DEA), methyldiethanolamine (MDEA) and 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol (AMP) through their own species, DEA and AMP each holding two parameters at MEA values.
The fitted \COtwo partial pressures of MEA at 30 wt\% and of DEA deviate by \res{mea-fitted-pressure-aard-abstract} and \res{dea-fitted-pressure-aard-abstract}\,\%, and the total pressures of MDEA and AMP by \res{mdea-fitted-pressure-aard-abstract} and \res{amp-fitted-pressure-aard-abstract}\,\%.
Reactive phase-stability searches with liquid and vapor trials at measured and gridded states confirm no second liquid in any model, with \res{mdea-measured-states-unclassified-abstract} MDEA heat-measurement states unclassified.
This outcome depends on the fitted data.
A DEA fit without the Lawson--Garst pressures predicts one.
Weighting the \COtwo-free MDEA pressures like the loaded ones removes a calculated liquid--liquid gap.
An AMP fit without the loaded Tong pressures and Hartono pressures above \SI{353}{\kelvin} predicts a second liquid; the AMP model, fitting them, detects no instability at \res{amp-reported-measured-pressure-control-no-descent-abstract} of \res{amp-measured-control-states-abstract} measured pressures and a vapor at \res{amp-reported-liquid-vapor-qualified-controls-abstract}, where \res{amp-third-liquid-trial-count-abstract} liquid trials find no second liquid.
Phase-stability searches test fitted electrolyte models beyond the deviations of their fits.

\end{abstract}
