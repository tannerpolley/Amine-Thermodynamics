\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-01
A nine-species ePC-SAFT model with explicit \MEAH and \MEACOO chemistry and an SSM+DS Born term was fitted to 48 \COtwo pressures at 40 and \SI{60}{\degreeCelsius} and 94 species measurements at 20--\SI{60}{\degreeCelsius} in 30 wt\% aqueous \MEA.
The fitted pressure and species AARDs are 13.53 and 8.28\,\%.
At \SI{80}{\degreeCelsius}, outside the current fit, the model reproduces the 11 Jou 1995 targets with an AARD of 9.24\,\%, all 21 canonical rows with 20.27\,\% and the 19 rows not used for inherited reaction shifts with 21.83\,\%.
These comparisons are tests outside the fit, not predictions: inherited reaction shifts used two of the 11 targets, and the fixed \COtwo dispersion energy was chosen against a set that included \SI{80}{\degreeCelsius} data.
On Wagner rows not used at any earlier stage, the record gives 24.51\,\% on 11 rows near \SI{353}{\kelvin} and 25.45\,\% on 12 rows near \SI{392}{\kelvin}.
A refit with source reaction constants to the same targets at or below \SI{60}{\degreeCelsius} keeps the low-temperature cost, 33.01 against 32.99, but gives 29.33\,\% on the 21 canonical rows and 52.30\,\% on the 11 Wagner rows at \SI{80}{\degreeCelsius}, with calculated pressures too low.
Data at or below \SI{60}{\degreeCelsius} therefore do not determine the temperature response, and independent temperature information is required.

% P:conclusion-02
The comparison of Born forms depends on the observations fitted.
On the historical 160-target objective, Original Born has the lower cost, 40.20 against 47.63.
On the current 142-target objective, which excludes the 18 Matin bicarbonate-pool targets, SSM+DS has the lower cost, 32.99 against 34.58, a 4.6\,\% advantage relative to Original Born.
Matin et al.\ report bicarbonate above NMR values, and the selected model departs further from that bicarbonate, with a signed relative mean of \(-58.84\,\%\).
Within this comparison, the Born-form ranking reverses when Matin's bicarbonate-pool observations are excluded from the objective.
It also depends on inherited Born inputs: with interactions fixed, Original Born reaches 32.18, below the 32.99 of SSM+DS, when the \MEAH, \MEACOO and \HCOthree Born diameters are scaled by 0.8 or \(\varepsilon_{\MEA}\) is set to 24.
The ranking is therefore not a property of either Born form on these data, and the speciation measurement method, its fitting role and the inherited Born inputs must be reported with any such comparison.

% P:conclusion-04
The Born term itself is needed: a refit without it reaches a cost of 98.79, with a fitted-pressure AARD of 53.90\,\%, and two interaction coordinates move to their bounds.
Refits with one interaction coordinate fixed at zero raise the cost by 134.32 for \MEAH--\MEACOO, 75.87 for \MEAH--water, 11.93 for \HCOthree--water and 9.85 for \MEACOO--water, so the two \MEAH interactions carry most of the fit; removing the temperature slope adds only 0.38 on these 20--\SI{60}{\degreeCelsius} data.
At the historical 160-target SSM+DS optimum, more than 99\,\% of each tested Born-input column of the weighted Jacobian lay in the span of the interaction columns, so the interactions compensate locally for small Born-input changes; they do not compensate for removal of the Born term.
These cost changes are local and conditional on the chemistry, bounds and fixed inputs, not statistical significance tests.

% P:conclusion-03
Pressure transfer gives AARDs of 75.95\,\% on 33 Aronu rows at 15 wt\% \MEA and 46.15\,\% on 37 Aronu rows at 45 wt\%; the 57 canonical pressure rows at 100--\SI{120}{\degreeCelsius} give 26.44\,\%.
The \HCOthree--water coefficient lies at its bound, and density and heat capacity remain unqualified.
The calculated carbonate share is 0.42--1.33 times the observed share over nine Jakobsen points at 20 and \SI{40}{\degreeCelsius}, excluding the \SI{40}{\degreeCelsius} point at loading 0.21.
Two Born probe calculations remain unavailable, with undetermined cause.
All evidence is numerical verification and assessment on existing measurements; no physical validation at new conditions is claimed.
The working domain of the record is \COtwo pressure and speciation in 30 wt\% \MEA at 40--\SI{80}{\degreeCelsius}; the evidence does not support other concentrations, higher temperatures or absorber and stripper design.
