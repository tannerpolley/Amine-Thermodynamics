# Static relative permittivity of aqueous MEA

The sign of the experimental loading dependence remains **undecided**. At 298.15 K the reported fit A reproduces the CO₂-free calibration data with 0.45% AARD and +0.12% relative bias. At 293.15–353.15 K it predicts a decrease with CO₂ loading. The Hajj et al. (2024) Table 1 estimates increase, but the measurements leave the low-frequency dielectric plateau of the loaded solutions unresolved.

## Measurements and frequency coverage

A static liquid value is the low-frequency limit of the bulk real permittivity after the dipolar relaxations have reached their low-frequency plateaus. Fast processes can contribute a constant over the measuring band. For conducting solutions, the conductivity term in the apparent loss and any electrode polarization also need to be accounted for. A finite-frequency value or an extrapolation from a band that cannot identify the slow modes has a different evidentiary use.

The search covered microwave regeneration, capacitance and dielectric-relaxation literature on MEA, other alkanolamines, piperazine and their salts, then hydroxyalkylammonium, carbonate and zwitterionic analogs. The piperazine/AMP regeneration study describes stripping and other fluid properties in its accessible abstract, with dielectric spectra unverified. The Ono and Shikata water/carbobetaine paper is a further dipolar-ion lead (1 MHz–20 GHz and a slow dipolar mode per its abstract, with full methods and total permittivity unavailable). Searches found no assessable static loading series for piperazine or an alkanolamine, and no assessable spectra for composition-controlled aqueous MEAH⁺ salts or MEA carbamate salts. [Palaian Premalalitha and Balraj (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.iecr.3c03671), [Ono and Shikata (2006)](https://doi.org/10.1021/jp0608170).

## Interpretation and materiality

In fit A, CO₂ reactions transfer neutral MEA to MEAH⁺ and MEACOO⁻, changing the number density and composition of the neutral set. The resulting decrease in calculated permittivity follows from the modeled neutral amounts and volume. The reported coefficients therefore provide no direct fitted ionic response. In a liquid, ions can reduce the water contribution through hydration and orientational constraints and through kinetic depolarization. A dipolar ion pair or zwitterion can add its own rotational response, as in the resolved glycine mode. The choline and carbonate data illustrate solvent decrement with salt. The real balance between these contributions in loaded MEA is unknown. [Reported liquid equation](../../../../../../../docs/scientific/latex/sections/epc_saft_equation_of_state.tex#L72), [Hajj §3.3.1](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molliq.2024.124819), [Shaukat pp. 10972–10974](https://doi.org/10.1039/C9CP01016E), [Capewell Table 1 and Fig. 1](https://doi.org/10.1039/A900812H), [Kondo Fig. 2](https://doi.org/10.1038/SREP45398), [Rahman §4.2, Eqs. (6)–(9)](https://doi.org/10.1021/jp207504d).

The historical ion-dependent joint refit reduced the Hajj contribution from 34.12 to 7.07 and raised the Part I objective from 150.76 to 151.79. The carbamate dielectric coefficient and carbamate–MEAH⁺ dispersion coordinate hit bounds. The historical single-liquid stability check also found descent away from the permittivity boundary. With the refitted parameters, held-out six-source pressure RMSE moved from 84.0 to 108.0 kPa, Kim heat RMSE from 7.61 to 8.65 kJ/mol CO₂, and Matin speciation-pool RMSE from 0.00289 to 0.00294 mol/mol. Arcis heat RMSE moved from 9.03 to 9.00 kJ/mol CO₂. These changes demonstrate sensitivity of reportable values to that joint model change. They cannot identify a correction due to the sign of the dielectric trend alone, since the joint estimation moved multiple interaction parameters. No new fit was performed. [Historical refit and held-out comparisons](../../../../../../mea_parameter_bundle/results/permittivity-refit/notebook.qmd), [older Born and permittivity comparisons](https://github.com/tannerpolley/Amine-Thermodynamics/blob/archive/pre-rebuild-3c85ad4fc/analyses/reactive_epcsaft_parameter_evidence/README.md).

The paper can state the CO₂-free calibration agreement and the computed decrease for the Hajj compositions and temperatures. It can report that the microwave-based fit estimates rise and explain the incomplete low-frequency coverage. A conductivity-separated broadband experiment with a low-frequency real plateau demonstrated at several loadings at fixed stock composition and temperature would determine the sign. Companion experiments with speciated MEAH⁺ and carbamate mixtures and controlled counterions could help distinguish the mechanisms.
