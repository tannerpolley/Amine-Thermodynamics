# PZ source and data map

Research snapshot for [#237](https://github.com/tannerpolley/Amine-Thermodynamics/issues/237), 2026-11-02, updated for [#239](https://github.com/tannerpolley/Amine-Thermodynamics/issues/239) the same day: Kamps 2003 read, and Yi 2024 used as an index and an eNRTL cross-check ([source note](sources/kamps-2003-yi-2024.md)). It replaces the PZ rows of [#227's inventory](../amp_pz_scoping/data-map.md). Those rows were CESAR-1 filename leads; these are paper-checked blocks. No measurement table is copied here. Counts are rows printed in the source table, counted from the PDF text. They are not independent experiments.

CESAR-1 (`~/Workspaces/External/CESAR-1`, commit `f24611b95a0fa62bd41b99c1bfd8d700fc6ddd2a`) was read only for filenames and column headers, to find these sources. Nothing from it was copied or run.

Stage key, as in #227: (a) pure PC-SAFT; (b) unloaded VLE; (d) reaction constants; (e) loaded pressure; (f) speciation; (g) absorption heat; (i) loaded heat capacity; (s) solid–liquid equilibrium (new for PZ).

## Proposed roles (not frozen)

The owner must approve these before any fit. The principle, from #220/#227: no row informs the law that is then tested on it, and hold-outs are whole campaigns chosen before fitting.

| Role | Proposal | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed input | P1/P2 from Hamborg Table 16, after transcription and a fit to Eq.21 with uncertainty; Hetzer laws as a cross-check over 293–323 K | Independent of all loaded data, and it covers 293–353 K |
| Calibration, neutral | Hilliard D.2 (46 rows, P_PZ and P_H2O) for PZ–water k(T); **owner decision** whether to add or substitute Hartono 2013 (24 points) once read | It is the only paper-checked unloaded PZ volatility block, but Yi 2024 rejected it because its PZ partial pressures (< 1 Pa) are at the FTIR detection limit (SI Table S1) |

An alternative primary hold-out is Dugas 80/100 °C (11 rows at 2–12 m), but it shares the UT lab and TIC loading method with Hilliard.

**Kamps 2003 versus Ermatchkov 2006 as the primary hold-out (#239).** Recommendation: keep Ermatchkov 2006 as primary and use Kamps 2003 as the secondary high-loading test above. The reasons, with locators in the [source note](sources/kamps-2003-yi-2024.md):

1. *Lab independence is equal.* The two papers share authors and the Kaiserslautern lab. Both are independent of Hilliard D.5; neither is independent of the Ermatchkov 2003 NMR calibration.
2. *Method independence slightly favours Kamps, but the difference does not decide the choice.* Kamps uses a synthetic bubble-point method (p.2664). Ermatchkov 2006 uses a separate low-pressure method, because the synthetic method suits only p > 0.2 MPa (Ermatchkov 2006, p.1788). Both differ from Hilliard's hot-gas FTIR.
3. *Observable.* Ermatchkov 2006 reports P_CO2, the quantity calibrated on Hilliard D.5. Kamps reports total pressure. At 374–395 K and low pressure, much of the total pressure is water: the unloaded 395 K rows are 0.19–0.20 MPa. A total-pressure residual there mixes water and CO2 volatility.
4. *Domain.* Ermatchkov 2006 covers 0.05–0.95 mol CO2/mol PZ and 0.1–95 kPa, the absorber region. Kamps covers 0.50–1.69 mol/mol at up to 9.6 MPa: 66 of 93 loaded rows exceed 1 mol/mol. There the residual mainly tests CO2 physical solubility and vapor nonideality from the frozen MEA shared-species record, plus zwitterion and PZH+–HCO3− interactions, rather than the carbamate chemistry. Only the 313 K Kamps block (7 rows without precipitation, 2 of them below 0.2 MPa) lies inside the 283–333 K carbamate-law domain.
5. *Data issues.* Kamps's printed temperatures are 0.6–1.8 K low (corrected in Ermatchkov 2006, Table 6). Below 0.1 MPa its uncertainty reaches 10 %. In an independent eNRTL fit, Yi 2024 accepted Ermatchkov 2006 (AARD 7.8 %) and rejected Kamps 2003 (Table 3), with systematic deviations at 2 m (SI Fig. S10). That fit does not quantify the reason.
