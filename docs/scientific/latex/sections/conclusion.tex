\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-mea
An ePC-SAFT model of \COtwo-loaded aqueous MEA with 11 jointly fitted parameters and literature equilibrium constants deviates by 11.3\,\% from its 47 fitted \COtwo partial pressures at 30 wt\% and by about 3\,\% or less from its fitted heats, heat capacities and densities.
Its pressures at 15 and 45 wt\% MEA and at stripper temperatures, its carbonate shares and its absorption heats at high temperature remain inaccurate, and the cause of the carbonate deficit is unresolved.

% P:conclusion-transfer
With the water, \COtwo and inorganic-ion parameters of that model held fixed, DEA, MDEA and AMP could each be fitted: the fitted loaded-solution pressures deviate by 42.71\,\% for DEA, 4.15\,\% for MDEA and 10.84\,\% for AMP, on 90, 66 and 105 observations.
\emph{Transfer} means no more than this.
DEA and AMP each fit a carbamate-hydrolysis correlation, MDEA needed its loaded-solution pressures weighted against its many density and heat data, and for DEA no tested water--DEA interaction represents the binary excess enthalpies and bubble pressures together.

% P:conclusion-shared
The shared water--bicarbonate and water--carbonate interactions are fixed from sodium-salt osmotic coefficients, so that no amine data determine them.
They are adopted for that reason, not because the amine data are fitted better.
With the first MDEA weights they traded the fitted MDEA total pressures against unfitted \COtwo partial pressures, compared with an interaction fitted to the MEA data; with the pressure weights of the MDEA model they fit both sets better.

% P:conclusion-phase
Phase-stability searches of the fitted models detect no instability for MEA or at the 666 searched AMP states.
For DEA they confirm liquid--liquid splits at four states whose densities were measured on samples treated as one liquid, so the DEA model fails there.
For MDEA a bound keeps the \COtwo-free solution free of a calculated liquid--liquid gap only up to \SI{423}{\kelvin}, and two calculated splits remain at loaded states far above the measured pressures, at 204 and \SI{263}{\mega\pascal}, the first confirmed only with the reaction references at the system pressure.
A first AMP fit, whose loaded-solution pressures ended at \SI{353}{\kelvin}, gave calculated liquid--liquid splits at 363--\SI{393}{\kelvin}; the refit that added 18 pressures of Hartono et al.\ and 40 of Tong et al.\ at up to \SI{393}{\kelvin} confirms none, although at four of the six measured states where the first fit split the phase identity is unresolved.
A phase-stability search is therefore a useful check on where a fit has been extrapolated or fails.
The four models are calibrations against data examined during development; \cref{sec:limits} states what they do not establish.
