# ChemRxiv submission draft

## Title

Electrolyte PC-SAFT with carbamate chemistry for CO₂ in aqueous monoethanolamine: joint calibration and a finite phase test

## Author and affiliation

Tanner W. Polley; ORCID 0009-0008-5957-4152; tpolley3@byu.edu. Department of Chemical Engineering, Brigham Young University, Provo, UT 84602, United States.

## Abstract

We fit a nine-species electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) model of CO₂ absorption in 30 wt% aqueous monoethanolamine (MEA), with explicit carbamate chemistry and a solvation-shell Born term. Sixteen parameters are fitted jointly to 307 quantities: CO₂ partial pressures, species fractions, integral heats, densities, heat capacities and binary phase equilibria. The reported parameter set gives average absolute relative deviations of 11.86% for 47 fitted pressures and 7.10% for 94 species fractions, and an integral-heat root-mean-square error of 1.83 kJ/mol CO₂. Densities are calculated without volume translation; 44 of 47 fitted loaded densities lie within twice their assigned scale. Tangent-plane searches at 73 states (313.15–333.15 K, loading 10⁻⁵–0.5) detect instability at 12 states for a fit with the original parameter bounds; at 333.15 K and loadings 0.4 and 0.5 that fit splits into a water-rich liquid and a liquid richer in amine and ions. Narrowing the bounds on the protonated-amine diameter and its water interaction gives the reported set: it removes every detected instability, raises the total cost by 0.45% and leaves three densities 0.40–0.53% low at 353.15 K. The fitted protonated-amine dissociation constant exceeds the Bates–Pinching value by 0.49 pK at 313.15 K. Carbonate shares, titration bicarbonate, an unloaded heat capacity and pressures at 45 wt% MEA remain discrepant. The phase searches do not reach the 353.15 K density failures or the higher-temperature pressure data, and the temperature dependence of the heat is untested beyond 313.15 and 322.5 K.
