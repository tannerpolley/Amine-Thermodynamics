\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-question
A phase-stability search of a fitted electrolyte equation of state can show where the fit lacks data.
An ePC-SAFT fit of loaded aqueous AMP whose loaded-solution pressures ended at \SI{353}{\kelvin} gave 73 confirmed liquid--liquid splits among 666 searched states, all at 363--\SI{393}{\kelvin}, and it underpredicted every total pressure measured there.
A second fit of the same data, with one coefficient fixed, had no split and the same pressure error, so excluding the split by a constraint would have hidden the defect.
Fitting the measured pressures at 373--\SI{393}{\kelvin} removed every split with all parameters free: the reported AMP model deviates by 10.9\,\% from 105 fitted total pressures and has none in 666 finite searches.
The search identified the temperatures at which data were missing; it did not establish whether loaded AMP solutions form a second liquid, which no measurement used here tests.

% P:conclusion-transfer
The shared parameters matter as much as the amine parameters.
The MEA data leave the water--bicarbonate interaction undetermined between about 0.13 and 1.0, and within that range the calculated MDEA pressures and splits change.
Fixing it and the water--carbonate interaction from sodium-salt osmotic coefficients gives a shared set that does not depend on an amine fit, at a stated price: the fitted MDEA total pressures improve from 20.1 to 10.1\,\%, but the 101 \COtwo partial pressures of Ermatchkov et al.\ worsen from 17.9 to 38.4\,\% and the fitted DEA pressures from 28.5 to 36.6\,\%.
\emph{Transfer} in this work therefore means that DEA, MDEA and AMP could be fitted with the shared parameters held fixed, not that those parameters are right for them.

% P:conclusion-models
The MEA model, an 11-parameter calibration with its equilibrium constants at literature correlations, deviates by 11.3\,\% from 47 fitted \COtwo partial pressures and shows no detected instability in 2646 searches; its pressures at 15 and 45 wt\% MEA, carbonate shares and absorption heats at high temperature remain inaccurate.
The DEA model deviates by 36.6\,\% from 30 fitted \COtwo partial pressures, and its water--DEA binary is a negative result: no tested interaction represents the excess enthalpies and bubble pressures together.
The MDEA model deviates by 10.1\,\% from 66 fitted total pressures; a bound keeps its \COtwo-free solution free of a calculated gap only through \SI{423}{\kelvin}, and its searches confirm one split, at \SI{121}{\mega\pascal}.
Fits from other starting sets and joint fits of the shared parameters remain to be made, and \cref{sec:limits} states what the four calibrations do not establish.
