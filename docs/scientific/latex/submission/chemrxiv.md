# ChemRxiv submission draft

## Title

Modeling CO₂ solubility in aqueous alkanolamine solutions with ePC-SAFT

## Author and affiliation

Tanner W. Polley; ORCID 0009-0008-5957-4152; tpolley3@byu.edu. Department of Chemical Engineering, Brigham Young University, Provo, UT 84602, United States.

## Abstract

An electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory model is calibrated for aqueous monoethanolamine (MEA) loaded with carbon dioxide (CO₂), and its water, CO₂ and inorganic-ion parameters, with water–anion interactions from sodium-salt osmotic coefficients, are held fixed while diethanolamine (DEA), methyldiethanolamine (MDEA) and 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol (AMP) are each fitted. With every reaction reference at 100 kPa, equilibrium, density and enthalpy follow from one Gibbs energy, whose pressure derivative differs from the volume by at most a relative 1.4×10⁻⁵, at numerical resolutions of 0.12–10.5%. Fitted CO₂ partial pressures of the 11-parameter MEA model at 30 wt% deviate by 11.34%, more at other concentrations and stripper temperatures, and those of DEA by 42.75%; fitted total pressures deviate by 10.82% for AMP and 4.39% for MDEA. The MDEA fit, provisional because only one of 64 drawn starts was evaluable, deviates by 29.21% from 101 unfitted Ermatchkov CO₂ partial pressures. Phase-stability searches find no instability for MEA or at 666 bubble-pressure-scaled AMP states, and no confirmed MDEA split. For DEA they confirm splits at four states whose measured densities the single-liquid calculation matches; these are failures of the DEA model. A first AMP fit, with pressures to 353 K, split at 363–393 K, also at six measured-pressure controls; a refit adding 58 pressures to 393 K confirms none, and at those controls four descents point toward a vapor and two have none. The searches show where a fit extrapolates or fails; the models are calibrations, not design tools.

## Keywords

ePC-SAFT; monoethanolamine; diethanolamine; methyldiethanolamine; 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol; parameter transfer; phase stability.

## Data availability

The electronic data comprise processed observations, parameter values, numerical results and the analysis and figure code under the MIT license; third-party measurements remain under the terms of their sources. They will be deposited in Zenodo, and the DOI will be added at submission. A software index in the deposit lists the versions, checksums and commands needed to repeat the calculations. The four reported models were calculated with version 1.3.1 of the epcsaft package, and the MEA fits with the MEA-fitted shared set with version 1.3.0; the current release, 1.3.2, was not used for them. Referees will receive access to the compiled epcsaft package through the corresponding author.
