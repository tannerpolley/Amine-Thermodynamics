\begin{abstract}
% P:abstract-01
This work examines whether a single electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) equation of state can describe the equilibrium, caloric and density properties of aqueous monoethanolamine loaded with carbon dioxide (\COtwo), using literature equilibrium-constant correlations and fewer adjustable parameters, without predicting an additional liquid phase.
Eleven parameters are estimated jointly from \COtwo partial pressures, species fractions, integral absorption heats, heat capacities, densities and binary vapor--liquid equilibria, with the five reaction equilibrium constants fixed at those correlations and the amine-ion Born diameters tied to the segment diameters.
Finite phase-stability searches detect no additional liquid phase, although phase stability was not imposed in the fit.
For nominal 30 wt\% monoethanolamine, the fitted \COtwo pressures have an average absolute relative deviation of 11.64\,\%, the integral absorption heats a root-mean-square error of \SI{1.98}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} \COtwo, and the fitted loaded densities a deviation of 0.152\,\% without volume translation.
These restrictions reduce the agreement with data outside the fit: for the Ma'mun pressures, which are not fitted, the deviation is 33.47\,\%, against 19.43\,\% for a 16-parameter calibration that also shifts two of those constants and fits separate amine-ion Born diameters.
Pressures at 15 and 45 wt\% monoethanolamine, carbonate shares and high-temperature absorption heats remain inaccurate.
All data used for comparison had been examined during model development, so the results describe a calibration rather than an independent validation, and the parameter uncertainty is conditional on the fit.
Part I supplies the equations and the shared parameters for the companion study of other amines.

\end{abstract}
