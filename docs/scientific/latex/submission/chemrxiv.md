# ChemRxiv submission draft

## Title

Thermodynamics of CO₂-loaded aqueous amines with ePC-SAFT: monoethanolamine calibration, transfer to DEA, MDEA and AMP, and phase stability

## Author and affiliation

Tanner W. Polley; ORCID 0009-0008-5957-4152; tpolley3@byu.edu. Department of Chemical Engineering, Brigham Young University, Provo, UT 84602, United States.

## Abstract

Do two calibrations of an equation of state that reproduce the pressures of aqueous amines loaded with carbon dioxide (CO₂) about equally well predict the same liquid phases? An electrolyte perturbed-chain statistical associating fluid theory (ePC-SAFT) model of monoethanolamine (MEA) is calibrated first, and its water, CO₂ and inorganic-ion parameters are held fixed while diethanolamine (DEA), methyldiethanolamine (MDEA) and 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol (AMP) are each fitted. For AMP, two fits with objective values of 61.3 and 62.1 deviate by 10.3 and 10.5% from the same 47 fitted total pressures, yet the first gives 73 calculated liquid–liquid splits at 363–393 K, above its fitted temperatures, and the second none in 666 finite phase-stability searches. For MDEA, changing one shared interaction within the tolerance of the MEA fit lowers the deviation of the 66 fitted total pressures from 20.1 to 11.2%, raises that of the pressures outside the fit from 25.8 to 33.4% and introduces two splits. The MEA model deviates by 11.6% from 47 fitted CO₂ partial pressures and the DEA model by 28.5% from 30; neither has a detected instability, but the DEA model gives the wrong sign of the water–DEA excess enthalpy. With the reported MDEA model no second liquid is confirmed at the measured states, and one two-liquid solution is found at 262.9 MPa, far above process pressures. These are calculated phase behaviors of calibrated models: the measurements do not establish coexistence, and the models, calibrated against data examined during development, provide neither validation nor a basis for absorber design.

## Keywords

ePC-SAFT; monoethanolamine; diethanolamine; methyldiethanolamine; 2-amino-2-methyl-1-propanol; parameter transfer; phase stability.

## Data availability

The electronic data comprise processed observations, parameter values, numerical results and the analysis and figure code under the MIT license; third-party measurements remain under the terms of their sources. They will be deposited in Zenodo, and the DOI will be added at submission. A software index in the deposit lists the versions, checksums and commands needed to repeat the calculations. The MEA calculations were made with version 1.3.0 of the epcsaft package, and the DEA, MDEA and AMP calculations with version 1.3.1. Referees will receive access to the compiled epcsaft package through the corresponding author.
