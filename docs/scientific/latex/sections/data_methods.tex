\section{Data and parameter estimation}\label{sec:data-methods}
\subsection{Experimental data}\label{sec:data-roles}
% P:methods-01
Loading is $\alpha=n_{\COtwo,\mathrm{added}}/n_{\mathrm{amine,total}}$, and the amine content is the amine mass fraction of the unloaded solution, written in wt\%.
\emph{Fitted} means that an observation enters the objective of the reported fit; \emph{not fitted} means that it is outside that objective; \emph{comparison only} means a different quantity or different conditions from those the model was built for.
Every observation had been examined during model development, and some observations that are not fitted informed a choice of parameters or of an initial parameter set; these are \emph{development} data.
No comparison in this paper is therefore an independent validation or a blind test, a limit that applies to every deviation reported below and is not repeated there.
\Cref{tab:calibration-data} lists, for each amine, the data in the objective, the data fitted beforehand and the development data; Tables~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-mea-comparison} and \ref{supp-tab:supplement-transfer-data} give every other set that is compared.

% P:methods-selection
The fitted sets were chosen amine by amine, not by one rule.
The MEA objective contains \COtwo partial pressures only at nominal 30 wt\% and 313--\SI{333}{\kelvin}; the pressures at 15 and 45 wt\% and at higher temperatures are not fitted.
The DEA objective has one loaded-pressure source, Lawson and Garst, whose cell also contained methane, which the calculation omits (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-corrections}); its isotherm at \SI{394}{\kelvin} is not fitted.
For MDEA the loaded total pressures of Kuranov et al.\ at \SI{413}{\kelvin} are not fitted, whereas their \COtwo-free pressures at that temperature are.
For AMP the objective contains every loaded total pressure of Hartono et al., at 313--\SI{393}{\kelvin}, and of Tong et al.; the first AMP fits of this work contained only the 47 pressures of Hartono et al.\ at 313--\SI{353}{\kelvin}, the measured range of the AMP dissociation constant (R5), and \cref{sec:results-amp} reports what that restriction caused.
The fitted loaded pressures of MDEA and AMP are total pressures, and their \COtwo partial pressures are not fitted.

% P:methods-range
A calculation is attempted for every observation, including those outside the measured temperature range of an equilibrium constant (\cref{tab:reaction-equilibrium-constants}).
For MEA the reacting calculations span 293.15--\SI{393.15}{\kelvin}, so R4 and R5 are extrapolated above \SI{323.15}{\kelvin}; the fitted pressures of every other amine also extend above its R5 range, and Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-ranges} reports the deviations inside and outside each range.
Each observable follows its experimental definition: a sum reported by nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) is compared with the same calculated sum \cite{Bottinger2008}, and bubble calculations omit carrier gas, so calculated $y_{\COtwo}P$ is compared with a measured \COtwo partial pressure and calculated $P$ with a measured total pressure (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-corrections}).

\subsection{Calculated quantities}\label{sec:heat-method}
% P:methods-heat-01
Pressures are reactive bubble pressures at the measured temperature and apparent composition, and species fractions are those of a single liquid at an assigned pressure.
The integral absorption heat per mole of added \COtwo is
\begin{equation}
 \Delta h=\frac{H_{\mathrm{final}}-H_{\mathrm{initial}}-\Delta n_{\COtwo}h_{\COtwo}^{\mathrm{feed}}}{\Delta n_{\COtwo}},
 \label{eq:addition-heat}
\end{equation}
with both equilibrium liquids and the pure-\COtwo feed at the same temperature and pressure; negative values denote exothermic absorption.
The semi-differential heats of Kim and Svendsen are calculated over each finite addition \cite{Kim2007}.

\subsection{Objective, deviations and conditional standard errors}\label{sec:parameters}
% P:methods-04
Estimation is sequential.
The water parameters, the \COtwo--water interaction, fitted to the pressures of Kiepe et al.\ \cite{kiepeExperimentalDeterminationPrediction2002}, the permittivity coefficients and the two salt-fitted interactions of \cref{sec:species} are determined first and remain fixed.
Eleven MEA parameters are then estimated jointly (\cref{tab:species-parameters,tab:interaction-matrix}): the MEA--water interaction and its temperature coefficient, the diameters and dispersion energies of \MEAH and \MEACOO, their interactions with water, one with a temperature coefficient, the \MEAH--\MEACOO interaction and the \MEAH--bicarbonate interaction.
With all shared parameters fixed, four DEA, eight MDEA and nine AMP parameters are fitted (\cref{tab:transfer-fitted}).
Parameters were also set beforehand: the two permittivity coefficients of each amine, the DEA--water interaction and the five pure-AMP parameters (\cref{tab:transfer-species}, Table~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-transfer-parameters}); with these, 7 quantities are adjusted for DEA, 10 for MDEA and 16 for AMP.
Each fit minimizes, by bounded least squares,
\begin{equation}
 C(\boldsymbol\theta)=\frac12\sum_jw_jr_j^2,\qquad
 r_j=\frac{y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}-y_j^{\mathrm{obs}}}{s_j}
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 r_j=\frac{\ln(y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}/y_j^{\mathrm{obs}})}{s_j},
 \label{eq:fit-objective}
\end{equation}
with the logarithmic form for pressures.

% P:methods-weights
The scales $s_j$ and weights $w_j$ are assigned for each data set, several without a statistical derivation, and they differ between the amines (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-objective}).
An objective value therefore sets fitting priorities within one amine and does not compare accuracy between amines, and two objective values can be compared only on the same weights.
The objectives are also dominated by different observables.
In the MEA objective the 50 MEA--water observations of Cai et al.\ \cite{caiBinaryIsobaricVaporLiquid1996} contribute 59.9\,\% of the objective value, 152.96, although they respond only to the MEA--water interaction and its temperature coefficient.
The fitted loaded-solution pressures contribute 98\,\% of the DEA objective and 96\,\% of the AMP objective, but 1\,\% of the MDEA objective, in which 24 densities of the \COtwo-free solution contribute 84\,\% (Table~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-objective-contributions}).
\Cref{sec:results-mdea} and Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-objective} report what changes when the weights are changed.

% P:methods-starts
Each fit is started from two initial parameter sets, three for MDEA, and the converged solution with the lower objective value is reported; Table~\ref{supp-tab:supplement-fit-starts} gives the result of every start.
These are local solutions.
No global search was made, and the sensitivity of the four fits to other starting sets has not been tested, which limits every statement below about what a fit ``gives'' to the solutions found.
For DEA the two objective values, 10.932834014425 and 10.932834014387, differ by less than the tie tolerance of $10^{-10}$, and the lower one is reported.
For MEA the joint fit was first made with fewer restrictions: the 16-parameter calibration also shifts the R2 and R5 correlations, fits separate Born diameters for the two MEA ions and is fitted with the stability penalty of \cref{sec:methods-phase}.
Fixing R2 and R5 at their literature forms and tying the Born diameters to the segment diameters gives the 11-parameter \emph{MEA model} used throughout; its optimization used no stability penalty, but both of its starts descend from the penalized calibration.

% P:methods-06
Agreement is reported as average absolute relative deviation (AARD), relative bias and root-mean-square error (RMSE),
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{AARD}=\frac{100\,\%}{N}\sum_j\left|\frac{y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}}{y_j^{\mathrm{obs}}}-1\right|,
 \qquad \operatorname{bias}=\frac{100\,\%}{N}\sum_j\left(\frac{y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}}{y_j^{\mathrm{obs}}}-1\right),
 \qquad \operatorname{RMSE}=\sqrt{\frac1N\sum_j(y_j^{\mathrm{calc}}-y_j^{\mathrm{obs}})^2},
 \label{eq:aard}
\end{equation}
where $N$ is the number of observations in the set, restricted for AARD and bias to nonzero observations.
For the parameters that are not on a bound, the local covariance is
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{cov}(\boldsymbol\theta_I)=s_C^2(J_I^{\mathsf T}J_I)^{-1},\qquad
 s_C^2=\frac{\sum_{j\in\mathcal R}w_jr_j^2}{N_{\mathrm{resp}}-|I|},
 \label{eq:conditional-covariance}
\end{equation}
where $J_I$ is the weighted residual Jacobian and $\mathcal R$ is the set of the $N_{\mathrm{resp}}$ observations in the objective whose calculated value depends on at least one fitted parameter; a parameter on a bound is held fixed and has no standard error.
An observation that responds to no fitted parameter is left out of both the sum and the count: the 39 Kiepe pressures of the MEA objective, which leaves 268 observations and 259 residual degrees of freedom, and three pure-water pressures of the MDEA objective, which leaves 278 and 272.
Compared with counting every observation, this raises the MEA standard errors by 7.3\,\% and those of MDEA by 0.5\,\%; all DEA and AMP observations respond.
These standard errors are conditional on the model form, the equilibrium-constant correlations, the fixed shared parameters, the scales, the weights and the local linearization; they exclude model discrepancy and systematic differences between sources and do not define prediction intervals.
Where two fits are compared by their objective values, the reference is the change $\Delta C=\tfrac12s_C^2\chi^2_{1,0.95}$ of a nominal 95\,\% likelihood-ratio interval for one parameter, which carries the same conditions.

\subsection{Phase-stability search}\label{sec:methods-phase}
% P:methods-phase-01
Each fitted parameter set is searched for a phase split with the tangent-plane criterion \cite{Michelsen1982}, with electroneutral trial phases \cite{Perdomo2025} and reaction-equilibrium constraints \cite{Tsanas2022,Ascani2022}:
\begin{equation}
 D(\boldsymbol w)=g(T,P,\boldsymbol w)/(RT)-\boldsymbol w^{\mathsf T}B^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\lambda,
 \quad \sum_iw_i=1,\quad\sum_i z_iw_i=0,\quad w_i>0.
 \label{eq:tpd}
\end{equation}
Here $\boldsymbol w$ is the composition of a trial phase, restricted to the species that can form from the feed and, for a vapor, to \COtwo, water and the molecular amine; $B$ is the conservation matrix, whose rows are the independent conserved groups of the true species, so that $B\boldsymbol\nu_r=0$ for every reaction; and $\boldsymbol\lambda$ holds the Lagrange multipliers of the corresponding balances at the searched (parent) state, divided by $RT$, so that $B^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\lambda$ is the tangent plane of the parent.
At each state $D$ is minimized from several initial trial compositions: a uniform one, one rich in each neutral species and, for a vapor, mixtures of the three neutral species.
A negative distance counts only when a finite, mass-conserving transfer of the trial phase lowers the Gibbs energy by more than its numerical error estimate; it is then a \emph{detected instability}, toward a second liquid or toward a vapor according to the density of the trial phase.
A liquid--liquid split is \emph{confirmed} when a separate equilibrium calculation at the same feed, temperature and pressure returns two distinct liquids with positive amounts and a Gibbs energy below that of the single liquid by more than its numerical error.
Trial compositions at which other parameter sets of this work gave a Gibbs-energy decrease are tested as well.

% P:methods-phase-states
The searched states are grids of temperature, amine content and loading, mostly at pressures of 1.05 and 3 times the calculated bubble pressure, and, for DEA, MDEA and AMP, also the measured states (Section~\ref{supp-sec:s-phase}).
A search that does not finish is counted as not completed, never as stable, and the grids differ between the amines, so the counts do not measure equal coverage.
The search is finite and local: it gives no proof of global stability, a count of splits is a lower bound, and the set of phases with the lowest Gibbs energy was determined only at the states where the text says so.
For the 16-parameter MEA calibration alone, the searched states also entered the objective, through the penalty $C_{\mathrm{pen}}=C+\tfrac12\sum_k(\tau_k/s_{\mathrm{stab}})^2$, where $\tau_k$ is the most negative tangent-plane distance found at state $k$, or zero, and $s_{\mathrm{stab}}$ is $10^{-2}$ and then $3\times10^{-3}$.
No reported model was fitted with a penalty.
For MDEA one interaction is bounded from the calculated phase behavior of the \COtwo-free solution (\cref{sec:results-mdea}); for AMP no bound or penalty is used.
