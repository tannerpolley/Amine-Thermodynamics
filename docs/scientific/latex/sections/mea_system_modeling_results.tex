\section{Results and discussion}\label{sec:mea-system-modeling-results}
\subsection{Phase-search and density trade-off}\label{sec:results-tradeoff}
% P:results-01
At the 73 test states (313.15--\SI{333.15}{\kelvin}), the original-bound fit has resolved instability at 12 states and the restricted-bound fit at none in 657 trial phases (\cref{fig:phase-family}).
These states lie below the \SI{353.15}{\kelvin} density failures and many pressure comparisons.
Nine of the original-bound instabilities occur at \SI{333.15}{\kelvin}, where separately seeded calculations confirm two-liquid states at loadings 0.4 and 0.5 (Section~S4).
The other three lie at 313.15 and \SI{318.15}{\kelvin}, loading 0.1--0.2 and the highest test pressure; a seeded calculation at \SI{313.15}{\kelvin} and loading 0.1 finds a second liquid coexisting with liquid and vapor (Table~S10).
At loading 0.4, the calculated split separates a water-rich liquid from a liquid richer in amine and ions; the water mole fractions are approximately 0.958 and 0.589.
The cited MEA property studies describe homogeneous liquid data rather than an observed two-liquid split \cite[Sect.~4.2]{neumannNewModelCombining2022}; the calculated splits have no experimental confirmation in these sources.
All trial minimizations converged for the restricted-bound fit, which gives 69 liquid--vapor and four single-liquid classifications.

% P:results-02
Restricting the bounds increases total cost from 148.24 to 148.91, or 0.45\,\%, and changes the loaded-density count within $2s$ from 47/47 to 44/47 (\cref{fig:density-errors,tab:density-failures}).
The original-bound fit's largest absolute scaled error is $1.98s$; the three restricted-bound misses are $2.05$--$2.43s$, so the count is sensitive to the chosen threshold.
The three errors are 0.40--0.53\,\% below measurement at \SI{353.15}{\kelvin}.
All other comparison limits in \cref{tab:acceptance-limits} are met.
This comparison quantifies the difference between two local fits; it does not establish a minimum unavoidable density penalty or an isolated Born-term effect.

\subsection{Agreement with fitted properties}\label{sec:results-calibration}
% P:results-04
\Cref{tab:residual-summary} reports deviations by observable, separating fitted quantities from those used only to choose between fits.
The \MEA--water constraints give Cai temperature RMSE \SI{1.79}{\kelvin} and vapor-water-fraction RMSE 0.0204; their signed biases are \SI{0.109}{\kelvin} and $-0.00259$.
The 39 Kiepe \COtwo--water pressures \cite{kiepeExperimentalDeterminationPrediction2002} are in the objective, but the \COtwo--water interaction they inform is fixed (Section~S9), so they check that input; their cost is approximately 0.0007.
Cai contributes approximately 62\,\% of the objective cost, 91.91 of 148.91; the binary temperature/fraction data dominate the remaining weighted mismatch.
For comparison, Baygi and Pahlavanzadeh report liquid- and vapor-MEA-fraction deviations of 3.54 and 1.54\,\% for their selected 3B-MEA/4C-water binary model \cite[Table~3]{Baygi2015}.
Those relative composition errors differ from the temperature and absolute vapor-water-fraction RMSEs used here, and their selected association scheme differs from the present 2B model.

% P:results-06
The 47 fitted loaded densities give AARD 0.177\,\%, but the three failures in \cref{tab:density-failures} remain visible despite that small average.
All five \COtwo-free densities lie within the \SI{1.0}{\kilogram\per\cubic\metre} absolute-error limit (\cref{tab:acceptance-limits}).
\Cref{fig:density-errors} also shows all 210 restricted-bound fit densities, separating fitted from non-fitted and excluded observations; the $2s$ requirement applies only to the 47 fitted loaded densities.
For the 68 Amundsen observations matched in temperature, loading and unloaded-solvent mass fraction, densities calculated without volume translation have AARD 11.84\,\% for the reference fit and 0.491\,\% for the restricted-bound fit (Table~S1).
The reference fit was fitted without density and evaluated at \SI{101325}{\pascal}; the restricted-bound fit uses \SI{100000}{\pascal}, and this pressure difference is not corrected.
Among the densities not fitted, calculated-minus-observed errors reach \SI{-21.51}{\kilogram\per\cubic\metre} for Amundsen at 40 wt\% \MEA, \SI{353.15}{\kelvin} and loading 0.4, and \SI{-20.02}{\kilogram\per\cubic\metre} for Han at 30 wt\% \MEA, \SI{393.15}{\kelvin} and loading 0.56.
All 67 Han densities are comparisons outside the objective, with RMSE \SI{5.905}{\kilogram\per\cubic\metre}.

\subsection{Comparisons outside the objective}\label{sec:results-comparisons}
% P:results-08
Across all 159 six-source pressures, 47 fitted and 112 not fitted, the AARD is 20.55\,\%.
Wagner pressures near 353 and \SI{392}{\kelvin} give 22.20 and 9.25\,\%; their mean logarithmic biases are $-0.252$ and $-0.0719$.
At 100--\SI{120}{\degreeCelsius}, the 54 six-source pressures have AARD 24.35\,\%; the mean logarithmic bias changes sign between sources (Table~S3), so no single high-temperature bias direction describes the model.

% P:results-09
Pressures at 15 and 45 wt\% MEA \cite{Aronu2011}, calculated with parameters fitted at 30 wt\%, give AARDs of 29.51 and 56.02\,\%.
Their mean logarithmic biases are $-0.204$ and $+0.149$, so, on logarithmic average, the model underpredicts at 15 wt\% and overpredicts at 45 wt\%; the 45 wt\% error limits use of these parameters away from 30 wt\%.

% P:results-10
Arcis heat RMSE is \SI{8.11}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} \COtwo, with calculated-minus-observed bias \SI{+7.18}{\kilo\joule\per\mole}; the restricted-bound fit is less exothermic on average.
The pressure-separated RMSEs are \SI{10.33}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} at \SI{0.51}{\mega\pascal} and \SI{3.37}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} at \SI{1.03}{\mega\pascal}.
This discrepancy remains visible beside the smaller Vinjarapu calibration error in \cref{fig:heat}.
The two Arcis pressure series themselves differ: at nearby loadings 0.181 and 0.183 their heats are $-92.6$ and $-85.9$~\si{\kilo\joule\per\mole}, and at 0.391 and 0.405 they are $-94.4$ and $-85.5$~\si{\kilo\joule\per\mole} \cite[Table~5, pp.~3355--3356]{arcisEnthalpySolutionCarbon2011}.
The loading differences prevent an exact matched-pressure comparison.
Both source pressure and temperature differ from the Vinjarapu calibration; this comparison does not isolate one physical cause.

% P:results-11
The 19 non-fitted Matin bicarbonate + carbonate totals give AARD 67.48\,\% and mean log ratio $-1.266$ (\cref{fig:speciation}).
The calculated aggregate is systematically low, even though other titration species enter the fit; Matin et al.\ themselves found bicarbonate substantially higher than NMR, so part of this discrepancy may reflect a conflict between data sources \cite[pp.~6616--6617]{matinFacileMethodDetermination2012}.
The ten Jakobsen carbonate-share observations are all compared: calculated/observed share has mean 0.0731 and range 0.00633--0.267.
The calculation uses unloaded \MEA mass fraction 0.2714, accounting for NMR sample dilution with \ce{D2O} and dioxane, and the NMR loading of each sample \cite[Sample preparation]{Jakobsen2005}.
Jakobsen et al.\ caution that components below 10\,\% of the total are not quantitatively reliable, as discussed by Matin et al.\ \cite[p.~6616]{matinFacileMethodDetermination2012}.
These shares differ from the six amount ratios to total amine for \MEAH, \MEACOO and \ce{HCO3- + CO3^{2-}} at each of two Jakobsen states used for calibration.
The carbonate, bicarbonate + carbonate total, heat-capacity and 45 wt\% discrepancies together limit where the restricted-bound parameters can be used.

\subsection{Parameters and local identifiability}\label{sec:results-uncertainty}
% P:results-12
Six of the 16 parameters are at bounds: \MEAH--water and \MEAH--\MEACOO interactions, \MEAH segment diameter, both fitted Born diameters and total R5 enthalpy shift.
The free bicarbonate--water coefficient is $0.725$ with conditional standard error $1.27$; the R2 total shift is approximately $-1.4\pm5.3$~\si{\kilo\joule\per\mole}, and the \MEAH--water slope is $-41.9$~\si{\kelvin} with conditional standard error \SI{60.3}{\kelvin}.
Both Born diameters reach their 5~\si{\angstrom} upper bound, and $k_{ij}(\MEAH,\MEACOO)=-1$ doubles the unlike-ion dispersion energy under \cref{eq:dispersion-combining-rule}.
These values indicate compensation among solvation, dispersion and reaction terms, rather than measured ion properties.
The fitted R5 differs from Bates--Pinching by 0.49 pK at 313.15 K, with dissociation enthalpy 63.4 rather than 50.5~\si{\kilo\joule\per\mole}; its total enthalpy shift reaches the +10~\si{\kilo\joule\per\mole} bound.
These local uncertainties and the correlations in Section~S6 limit physical interpretation of individual coefficients.
They do not identify unique ion dimensions or reaction enthalpies, and they do not supply prediction intervals for pressures or process duties.

\subsection{Comparison with published models}\label{sec:results-literature}
% P:results-13
Zhang et al.\ provide the closest multi-property calibration, using 12 ternary parameters for solubility, differential heat, heat capacity and NMR \cite[Sect.~3.3, Tables~9--11]{Zhang2011}.
Their fitted pressure deviations range from 13.5 to 35.5\,\% by source, compared with 11.86\,\% for the present 47-pressure calibration and 20.55\,\% across the six-source comparison.
Baygi and Pahlavanzadeh and Najafloo and Zarei report 36.42 and 34.71\,\% pressure deviations on 691-observation ternary comparisons after pure/binary parameter estimation \cite[Table~5]{Baygi2015}\cite[Table~5]{Najafloo2018}.
The explicit-ion electrolyte cubic two-state association model of T\'ellez-Arredondo and Medeiros reports 12.5\,\% fitted pressure deviation, while Neumann et al.\ report ternary density errors of 0.92\,\% for Hartono and 3.5\,\% for the complete Han data \cite[Tables~5 and 8]{tellez-arredondoModelingCO2H2S2013}\cite[Sect.~4.2]{neumannNewModelCombining2022}.
These statistics place the present agreement in the range of related MEA models, but their different data, calculated quantities and fitting roles prevent a common-data accuracy ranking.

\subsection{Scope and limitations}\label{sec:results-limits}
% P:results-14
The phase searches cover only 313.15--333.15 K and loading $10^{-5}$--0.5; phase behavior remains untested at the 353.15 K density failures and at higher-temperature pressure comparisons.
The restricted-bound fit misses three loaded-density limits and retains discrepancies in carbonate share, titration bicarbonate, the unloaded 318.15 K heat capacity and pressures at 45 wt\% MEA.
R5's 0.49 pK departure at 313.15 K and its 50.5 to 63.4 kJ/mol dissociation-enthalpy change may compensate for model error.
The heat temperature dependence is untested beyond the fitted 313.15 K and compared 322.5 K states; no fit with R5 fixed and no matched fit omitting heat was made.
Two sequential single-start fits, with bounds chosen from fixed-parameter probes (Section~S5), give local solutions; the conditional covariance gives neither unique physical parameters nor prediction intervals.
Use of the permittivity law in reacting mixtures and reaction-law extrapolation above their source ranges remain assumptions.
Fitted agreement is calibration, the phase searches and solution residuals are numerical evidence, and previously examined comparisons and Arcis-based selection do not provide independent validation of phase behavior or absorber/stripper performance.
