\begin{abstract}
% P:abstract-01
The equilibrium \COtwo partial pressure over aqueous monoethanolamine (\MEA) depends on liquid speciation as well as phase equilibrium.
We present a nine-species electrolyte PC-SAFT (ePC-SAFT) model of \COtwo solubility in 30 wt\% \MEA with explicit carbamate chemistry, five reactions and a modified Born term.
Five interaction coordinates were fitted to 48 \COtwo partial pressures at 40 and \SI{60}{\degreeCelsius} and 94 NMR and titration species measurements at 20--\SI{60}{\degreeCelsius}, giving average absolute relative deviations (AARD) of 13.53\,\% and 8.28\,\%.
Prediction here means calculation outside the current fit.
For the 11 Jou 1995 targets at \SI{80}{\degreeCelsius}, the pressure AARD is 9.24\,\%; all 21 canonical rows at \SI{80}{\degreeCelsius} give 20.27\,\%.
These are tests outside the present (142-target, five-coordinate) fit, not data unseen during model development.
The solvation-shell and dielectric-saturation (SSM+DS) Born term of Figiel et al.\ and the Original Born term were fitted with identical coordinates and data.
Their ranking depends on the role of 18 bicarbonate observations from Matin et al., whose titration values the source authors report as higher than NMR values.
With those observations fitted, Original Born has the lower weighted cost, 40.20 against 47.63 on 160 targets; with them report-only, SSM+DS is lower, 32.99 against 34.58 on 142 targets.
Pressure transfer to 15 and 45 wt\% \MEA gives AARDs of 75.95\,\% and 46.15\,\%, and extrapolation to 100--\SI{120}{\degreeCelsius} gives 26.44\,\%.
The bicarbonate--water coefficient lies at its bound, and density and heat capacity remain unqualified.
The results are numerical verification and assessment on existing measurements, not physical validation.
\end{abstract}
