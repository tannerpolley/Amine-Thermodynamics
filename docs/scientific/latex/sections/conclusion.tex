\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-01
An ePC-SAFT calibration with literature equilibrium-constant correlations and tied amine-ion Born diameters describes pressure, speciation, heat, heat capacity and density with a single equation of state, and the finite phase-stability searches detect no additional liquid phase.
The reduced parameter freedom worsens the agreement with data outside the fit, including the Ma'mun pressures, while pressures at other MEA concentrations, the carbonate shares and high-temperature absorption heats remain inaccurate.

% P:conclusion-limits
These are comparisons of a calibration with data examined during development, not independent validation.
The standard errors remain conditional on the fitted model and inputs, and finite searches do not establish global stability.
Using one equation of state for all properties makes the comparisons consistent, but the remaining discrepancies do not support unrestricted use in process calculations.

% P:conclusion-shared
Part II holds fixed the shared parameters of \cref{tab:shared-parameters}.
The water--bicarbonate interaction is poorly determined, the permittivity in loaded solutions is assumed, and the equilibrium-constant correlations are extrapolated, so agreement for MEA does not establish the accuracy of these parameters for other amines; Part II assesses the consequences.
