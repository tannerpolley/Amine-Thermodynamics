\section{Model inputs and fixed-parameter provenance}\label{sec:s-parameters}
% P:supplement-18
Neutral MEA parameters are the 2B values of Baygi and Pahlavanzadeh, except the association volume described below \cite[Table~2]{Baygi2015}.
\COtwo segment count, diameter and dispersion energy are from Gross and Sadowski \cite[Table~2]{Gross2001}.
Bicarbonate, carbonate and hydroxide sizes and dispersion energies follow Uyan et al.; their hydrogen-ion parameters are transferred here to hydronium \cite[Table~4]{Uyan2015}.
Ion segment count one and the diameter ties are modeling assumptions.
Fixed Born diameters for bicarbonate, carbonate, hydronium and hydroxide, and the fixed water interactions with carbonate, hydronium and hydroxide, are model inputs without independent MEA-specific parameter measurements.
All other interactions are zero by assumption except the fixed water interactions listed below.
\Cref{tab:supplement-species-parameters,tab:supplement-interaction-matrix} list their exact values; they are not attributed to the cited size/dispersion source.
Water's solvation factor and ion-region permittivity follow Figiel et al.; unit solvation factors for the other components are assumptions \cite[Table~2, Eqs.~5--10]{Figiel2025}.
The ion packing and Debye--H\"uckel diameters follow the ties stated in the main text.

\input{tables/supplement_interaction_matrix}

% P:supplement-association-01
The \COtwo--water cross-association energy is \SI{878.325}{\kelvin}, half the fitted water self-association energy, with the same association volume as water.
The topology follows Schick et al.\ and Pabsch et al.\ \cite[Table~1, Sect.~2.4.2]{Schick2023}\cite{Pabsch2020}.
The association strength here carries $\sigma_{ij}^3$, whereas Baygi and Pahlavanzadeh report their MEA association volume, 0.037470, for a $d_{ij}^3$ strength \cite[Table~2]{Baygi2015}.
The value used here, 0.036305439, is the constant fitted by least squares, with the ePC-SAFT library's fitter, to the vapor pressures and saturated-liquid densities of their own model at 21 temperatures from 293.15 to \SI{393.15}{\kelvin}.



% P:supplement-19
The main text reports the fits of the five water parameters to IAPWS-95 \cite{internationalassociationforthepropertiesofwaterandsteamRevisedReleaseIAPWS2018}, the \COtwo--water interaction to Kiepe pressures \cite{kiepeExperimentalDeterminationPrediction2002} and the \MEA dielectric coefficients to permittivities.
In the water fit, the two exponential terms in $\sigma_w(T)=\sigma_0+a_1\exp(b_1T)+a_2\exp(b_2T)$, with $a_1=10.11$, $b_1=-0.01775$, $a_2=-1.417$ and $b_2=-0.01146$, were fixed while $\sigma_0=2.7804326$~\si{\angstrom} was fitted; diameters are in angstrom and temperature in kelvin.

% P:supplement-20
In the permittivity fit, water coefficients, 0.3777 L/mol and 1403 K, follow Yang et al.; the \COtwo dielectric volume $10^{-8}$ L/mol and temperature 1403 K are assumptions \cite[Table~6]{Yang2024}.
At the measurement temperature, two coefficient pairs give identical permittivities at any density.
The larger-volume solution is used here; the permittivity data alone do not distinguish these solutions, so that selection is a model assumption.
The 14 unloaded measurements at \SI{298.15}{\kelvin} do not determine temperature or loading dependence; direct static-permittivity data over the reacting domain are missing from this calibration, so extrapolation remains a model assumption.

% P:supplement-objective-00
Table~\ref{tab:objective-groups} gives the scales, weights and contributions to the joint objective of main-text Eq.~\ref{main-eq:fit-objective}.
Pressure residuals are log ratios with unit scale; other residuals divide a difference by a scale in the observable's units.
Species fractions and amount ratios use the assigned scale $0.1y^{\mathrm{obs}}+0.001$.
The fitted heat-capacity scale is $0.02C_P^{\mathrm{obs}}/n_{\mathrm{feed}}$ in J/(mol feed K), not a mass-specific heat capacity.

Vinjarapu heat scales use the printed uncertainty converted to J/mol \COtwo \cite[Table~4]{HemaBhavya2025}.
The species relative allowances and floors, log-pressure allowance, carbon-free-density allowance, Cai allowances and group weights are assigned choices whose detailed rationale was not recorded.

\section{Source conventions and density scales}\label{sec:s-corrections}\label{sec:s-density}
% P:supplement-density-01
Amundsen's density and loading inputs are \SI{2}{\kilogram\per\cubic\metre} and 2\,\% of loading for the fitted loaded series, with unresolved high-temperature/loading applicability of the density estimate \cite[pp.~3099--3100]{Amundsen2009}.
Hartono's inputs are \SI{0.02}{\kilogram\per\cubic\metre} and 0.01 mol/mol loading; the density input is an expanded uncertainty with coverage factor two and is used without division by that factor \cite[Table~3]{Hartono2014}.
The secant construction in main-text Eq.~\ref{main-eq:density-scale} combines these with loading uncertainty.
These scales therefore do not uniformly represent standard uncertainties.

% P:supplement-sources
All density observations, including the excluded Amundsen loading-0.4 series, remain in the electronic data with source pressure, composition and calculated value.
The source comparison does not identify one experiment as correct.
For Jakobsen NMR calculations, dilution changes the solvent composition, and the reported NMR loading is used instead of the maximum loading \cite[Sample preparation, Tables~A1--A2]{Jakobsen2005}.
The carbonate-share comparison differs from the fitted species-to-total-amine ratios.

\section{Caloric functions and reproduction values}\label{sec:s-files}
% P:supplement-calorics
Neutral caloric functions use NIST-JANAF for \COtwo, the MEA heat-capacity function tabulated by Zhang et al., and an ideal-gas water fit to IAPWS-95 \cite{chaseNISTJANAFThermochemicalTables1998,Zhang2011,internationalassociationforthepropertiesofwaterandsteamRevisedReleaseIAPWS2018}.
The ionic functions are completed from the reaction laws and their infinite-dilution reference corrections.
A charge-proportional reference freedom cancels in electroneutral phases; no independent ionic heat-capacity functions are fitted.
The reaction enthalpy is included once through these completed standard states.

\section{Phase-search conditions and split interpretation}\label{sec:s-phase}\label{sec:s-probes}\label{sec:s-fits}
% P:supplement-phase-grid
The liquid grid contains 1800 states at 15, 25, 35 and 45 wt\% MEA, 313.15--\SI{393.15}{\kelvin} in \SI{10}{\kelvin} steps, and loading $10^{-5}$ plus 0.025--0.6 mol/mol in 0.025 steps, at both pressure ratios stated in the main text.
Another 108 states use the liquid--vapor-search feed at those two ratios.
The separate 576-state liquid check uses 20, 30 and 40 wt\%, 318.15--\SI{388.15}{\kelvin} and loadings 0.0125--0.5875, at 1.5 times bubble pressure.

% P:supplement-phase-census
The liquid--vapor search contains six temperatures, 313.15, 318.15, 333.15, 353.15, 373.15 and \SI{393.15}{\kelvin}, and nine loadings, $10^{-5}$, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.256, 0.3, 0.4, 0.5 and 0.6 mol/mol.
Each uses three pressure fractions, 0.50, 0.80 and 0.95, of a fixed reference bubble pressure from the comparison calculation.
Every compared parameter set is evaluated at these same physical states.
The feed is 9.7292821 mol MEA, 76.9751925 mol water and $9.7292821\alpha$ mol \COtwo.
Thus extensive split-energy changes divided by $RT$ are in mol on this feed basis.

% P:supplement-phase-method
Charge-neutral liquid starts include a uniform composition and near-pure neutral-species compositions; vapor starts contain only the three neutral species.
The optimized search and the evaluations at earlier resolved split compositions are reported separately.
Only a negative tangent-plane distance followed by a material-conserving finite transfer beyond the numerical energy-error estimate counts as a resolved descent.
Search completion, residual convergence and positive pressure--density derivatives are numerical checks rather than physical measurements.
The raw state results identify incomplete or unavailable cases explicitly.

% P:supplement-phase-cause
The split-energy figure evaluates the same phase compositions under two parameter sets and closes density at the imposed pressure for each.
It does not relax the frozen F2 phases to equilibrium, nor identify a microscopic interaction uniquely.
The isolated segment-diameter change leaves the other diameter definitions unchanged and is outside F2's fitted diameter ties.
The alternative sufficient interaction change also lacks fitted-property evidence.
Neither probe can be compared with F2's covariance as if it were an uncertainty interval for liquid splitting.

\section{Shared inputs for Part II}\label{sec:s-shared}
% P:supplement-shared
The shared coefficient table in the main text is generated from the adopted parameter document, not from a sodium-salt substitution.
Part II also keeps the shared component charges, segment counts, association topology, electrostatic model choices, standard states and R1--R3 reaction definitions fixed.
The water--bicarbonate interaction is the MEA-fitted value, whereas the carbonate--water interaction is an assumed fixed value.
The weakly determined bicarbonate interaction and the dielectric and source-law extrapolations limit the strength of a transfer claim before amine-specific comparisons.
