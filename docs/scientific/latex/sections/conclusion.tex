\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusion}
% P:conclusion-01
With sixteen jointly estimated parameters, one ePC-SAFT formulation with carbamate chemistry represents the equilibrium, caloric and density properties of loaded aqueous MEA from a common Helmholtz energy and reaction standard states.
For nominal 30 wt\% MEA, pressure AARD is 11.86\,\% for 47 fitted and 24.2\,\% for 112 non-fitted pressures (20.55\,\% overall), 94 of the 101 fitted species fractions give 7.10\,\%, integral-heat RMSE is \SI{1.84}{\kilo\joule\per\mole} \COtwo at \SI{313.15}{\kelvin}, and loaded-density AARD is 0.18\,\% over 47 fitted values through \SI{353.15}{\kelvin}, without volume translation.

% P:conclusion-02
Similar property agreement can conceal an additional calculated liquid: instability is detected for the wider-bound fit at 54 states, while the final parameter set has no additional liquid detected at the 162 searched states from 313.15 to \SI{393.15}{\kelvin} and loadings $10^{-5}$--0.6.
Restricting the protonated-amine diameter and its water interaction removes that detected instability, increases fitting cost by 0.45\,\% and leaves three fitted densities outside $2s$.

% P:conclusion-limits
Six parameters are on bounds, four of the remaining ten have standard errors larger than their absolute values, and the R5 shift of 0.49 p$K$ lies within its conditional standard error of 0.55 p$K$; these results are consistent with compensation rather than unique physical parameters.
The comparisons constrain nominal 30 wt\% MEA at property-specific conditions: fitted pressures and species end near \SI{333}{\kelvin}, fitted densities at \SI{353.15}{\kelvin}, and absorption heat is compared with measurements up to \SI{393.15}{\kelvin}, where the Kim heat deviations are largest even after anomalous source rows are excluded (Section~\ref{sec:results-heat}).
Bicarbonate and carbonate fractions, the \SI{0.51}{\mega\pascal} Arcis heats, unloaded heat capacity at \SI{318.15}{\kelvin} and higher-temperature densities remain discrepant; R4 and R5 are extrapolated above their \SI{323.15}{\kelvin} source limit.
Pressure deviations rise to 29.5 and 56.0\,\% at 15 and 45 wt\% MEA, respectively.
All comparison data were accessed during development, the parameter errors are conditional, and the phase search supplies numerical evidence at specified states rather than physical validation.
Broader caloric and phase-behavior comparisons and improved concentration and minor-species descriptions are needed before process use.

% P:conclusion-03
Application of the estimation and comparison workflow to MDEA demonstrates the procedure for one tertiary amine.
The MDEA fits reverse their pressure-error ranking between Ermatchkov (26.21 versus 19.15\,\%) and Xiao (20.40 versus 28.28\,\%), in single-sphere/charged-molecule order, despite retaining the shared-species parameters.
With different active bounds, these comparisons do not identify a generally preferable cation representation.
Transfer to amines in general remains unvalidated, including the additional carbamate chemistry required for primary and secondary amines.
