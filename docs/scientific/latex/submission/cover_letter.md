Dear Editors of Fluid Phase Equilibria,

Please consider “Modeling CO₂ absorption in aqueous monoethanolamine with ePC-SAFT” for publication as a research article.

The paper examines whether one explicit-ion ePC-SAFT formulation with carbamate chemistry represents the equilibrium, caloric and density properties of loaded aqueous MEA. Sixteen parameters are fitted to pressure, species, integral heat, density, heat capacity and binary VLE data. Pressure deviation is 11.86% over 47 fitted observations and 20.55% over the 159-pressure comparison. Loaded-density deviation is 0.18% over 47 fitted observations, without volume translation. The manuscript presents calculated property curves and discusses the remaining discrepancies beside each property.

Pressure deviations fall within the published range for related models using different data sets and fitting roles; no common-data accuracy ranking is claimed. The application introduces explicit carbamate ions within ePC-SAFT. A finite phase search at 313.15–333.15 K and loading 10⁻⁵–0.5 also illustrates how similar property agreement can conceal additional calculated liquids. Narrowing two parameter bounds removes the detected instabilities at a 0.45% increase in fitting cost, with three fitted density limits missed. This finite numerical result does not establish phase behavior outside the searched states.

The model represents selected nominal 30 wt% observations. Concentration transfer, minor species, caloric temperature dependence and warm density remain limitations, and the available comparisons do not establish suitability across an absorber's operating range.

Processed observations, parameters, calculations and presentation code are prepared for a Zenodo deposit, with the DOI to be added at submission. The compiled ePC-SAFT library used here will be supplied to referees through restricted access coordinated by the corresponding author. Deposit publication, access arrangements and submission remain subject to the author's approval.

The manuscript and Supplementary Material include the approved funding, competing-interest and generative-AI declarations. This letter is a local submission draft.
